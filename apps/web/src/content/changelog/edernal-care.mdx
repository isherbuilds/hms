## A new name

HMS is now **Edernal Care**. Page titles, printed documents and the sign-in
screens carry the new name and wordmark. Your hospital's data, logins and
addresses are unchanged.

## A green accent

The app now uses Edernal Care green to mark where you are and what you picked:
the current page in the sidebar, ticked checkboxes, links, and the highlighted
day on the dashboard chart. Buttons and status colours work as before.
