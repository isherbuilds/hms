Registered hospitals print **Bill of Supply** for exempt OPD care, **Tax Invoice**
for counter pharmacy and **Receipt Voucher** for advances against future care.
Without a GSTIN, the titles stay **Invoice** and **Advance Receipt**, with no GST
claims.

## What prints

- The GSTIN, with its state as the place of supply, and the Form 20/21 drug
  licences on pharmacy bills.
- Rate-wise taxable value and the CGST/SGST split on pharmacy Tax Invoices.

## What does not change

The issue-time snapshot controls the paper: a later settings change never
reclassifies an old document.
