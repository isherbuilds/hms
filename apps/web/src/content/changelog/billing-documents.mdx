Invoices, payment receipts and credit notes now render as PDFs from one
server-side endpoint. Preview in the browser, print at the desk and the file a
patient downloads are the same bytes from the same source facts.

## What prints

- The hospital's legal name, address and GSTIN, from organization settings.
- Every charge as it was priced at the time — catalog and tax facts are snapshotted
  onto the charge, so a later price change never rewrites an old bill.
- Patient names in Devanagari where that is how they were registered.

## What does not

A payment receipt is never labelled as the itemized bill; they are different
documents with different requirements. And until the pilot's chartered accountant
signs off on classifications, the itemized document carries the neutral label
**Invoice** and makes no _Tax Invoice_ or _Bill of Supply_ claim. We would rather
print less than print something we cannot yet stand behind.

## Corrections

An issued invoice is immutable. A discount after issue is a credit note; money
returned is a refund. The trail shows what happened, not only the final number.
