The billing screen now leads with exceptions.

- **Waiting to be billed** — appointments where care has happened and no invoice
  exists. A configurable threshold decides when "not yet" becomes "late", and
  the count tells the cashier how many people are still in the building.
- **Outstanding** — issued invoices with money still owed, with **Over 30 days**
  broken out so the number nobody has chased is visible on its own tile.
- **Refund due** — credit notes whose money has not gone back to the patient.

The open-money list underneath is bounded and filterable — _To bill_, _Unpaid_,
_Over 7 days_ — and searchable by patient, MRN or invoice number. An invoice past
seven days changes colour in the list rather than waiting for a report.

## Also

The same lists work on a phone. A cashier can see what is outstanding from the
ward without walking back to the desk.
