## Less to fill in at the desk

Registration starts with the patient's name, phone, sex, and date of birth or
age. **Contacts**, **Personal details**, **Sponsor**, and **Medical details**
start collapsed. Open only the sections you need. Closing a section keeps what
you entered; a section opens again if a field needs correction before saving.

## Patient relation and emergency contact

Open **Contacts** to add a relation and name, such as `W/o Gurmeet Singh`.
This appears after the patient's name on the record, OPD slip, and invoice.
The relation's mobile number is optional.

Choose **Copy from relation** to fill the emergency contact with their name and
mobile number, then correct it if needed. The relation needs a mobile number
before you can copy it. You can also enter a different person directly. The
emergency contact's relation is optional.

## Consistent names

New or edited patient, practitioner, and phone-booking caller names use
consistent capitalisation on screen and in printed documents. Existing records
keep their saved spelling until updated. Service, payer, and department names
keep their chosen case, including abbreviations such as `ECHS`.
