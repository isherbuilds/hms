## Find or register a patient

Choose **Patients** in the left menu. Type a name, phone number, or patient
number into **Search patients**. Check the results before you register someone.
This helps stop two records being made for the same person.

![Patient list with the search box, patient rows, and Register Patient button.](/docs/images/guides/patients-list.webp)

Choose **Register Patient** when the person is not already listed. Fill in the
main details:

1. Type the person's full name and mobile number.
2. Choose their sex.
3. Enter their date of birth or their age.
4. Open any extra sections you need.
5. Read the details once more, then choose **Save**.

![Patient registration form with the required details and extra sections.](/docs/images/guides/patient-registration.webp)

The extra sections are optional:

- **Contacts** — the person's relation or guardian and an emergency contact.
  If the relation has a mobile number, **Copy from relation** can copy that
  person's details into the emergency contact. Check the copied details before
  you save. An emergency contact needs a name and phone number.
- **Personal details** — email, identity number, and address.
- **Sponsor** — the insurer, company, or other payer, plus policy and employee
  numbers when they apply.
- **Medical details** — blood group, allergies, and medical history.

You can close an extra section after you enter its details. HMS keeps the
details in the form. If something needs fixing, HMS opens that section and shows
you which field to correct. Only enter details the patient or hospital has
confirmed.

## Read a patient's record

Open a patient's name to see the record. The top strip shows the patient's name,
number, age, and allergy warning so you can check you opened the right person.
**Book Appointment** starts an appointment for this patient.

![Patient record with allergy information and the Record, Visits, Billing, and Treatment tabs.](/docs/images/guides/patient-record.webp)

The tabs show different parts of the same person's record:

- **Record** — phone, address, medical details, and **Edit Patient**.
- **Visits** — earlier appointments. Open a row to see its files and Charges.
- **Billing** — the person's invoices, payments, and any money kept for future
  care.
- **Treatment** — a summary of the patient's treatment plans. Start or bill a
  plan from a checked-in OPD visit. See
  [the dentist example](#example-a-dentist-treatment-plan).

![Treatment tab. This example has no treatment plan.](/docs/images/guides/patient-treatment.webp)

![Visits tab before a row is opened.](/docs/images/guides/patient-visits.webp)

Open a visit row to see its billable items and any attached files. Choose **Open
Visit Record** to go to the full visit.

![An opened visit row showing its Charges and the Open Visit Record button.](/docs/images/guides/patient-visit-details.webp)

The Billing tab keeps documents for one patient together. Choose **Take
Advance** only when you mean to hold money for later care. See [Billing and
corrections](/docs/billing-and-corrections) for what each document means.

![Patient Billing tab with invoices and the Take Advance action.](/docs/images/guides/patient-billing.webp)

![Take Advance form. The example is not submitted.](/docs/images/guides/patient-advance.webp)

**Edit Patient** opens the same patient form used at registration. Check the
record at the top before you change it, then save only details the patient or
hospital has confirmed.

## Worked example: one patient, from start to medicine

This is a training example with a made-up patient called **Mira Example**, age 8. Do not copy the name or any demo amount into a live record. In a real visit,
use the details the patient or guardian confirms.

1. **Look first.** Choose **Patients** and search Mira's name or confirmed
   phone number. If a matching record appears, open it and check the name and
   age before you continue. Do not register a second record just because the
   first search did not show the full name.
2. **Register only if needed.** Enter the patient's full name, confirmed phone
   number, and sex. Enter the date of birth from a trusted record if you have
   it. If you do not know the date, enter the patient's age instead; HMS marks
   the birth date as estimated. For Mira, enter age 8 and the confirmed
   guardian's details.
   Add an allergy only when the patient or guardian has told you about it.
3. **Book the visit.** Choose **OPD** → **New Appointment**. Choose the
   patient, department, and practitioner. Choose **Now** only when the patient
   is here. Check the service and price. The training hospital may show
   **Paediatrics consultation · ₹500**; your hospital's price can be
   different. Choose **Confirm**. For a billable visit, review the payment
   and advance credit, then choose **Confirm Walk-In** once. This saves the
   visit and its invoice together. Record only money actually received.
4. **Let the clinician decide the care.** Open the visit and check the patient
   name again. The doctor decides the diagnosis, treatment, medicine, and
   directions. HMS's **Clinical** tab can hold a scan of the signed paper
   prescription. This guide does not provide a diagnosis or medicine dose.
5. **Check the visit bill.** Open the visit's **Billing** tab. The invoice
   made during **Now** intake is already there; do not bill the same work
   again. Record any unpaid balance against that invoice. Use **Review And
   Collect** only when new Charges are waiting to be billed, such as work from
   a treatment plan. See [Billing and corrections](/docs/billing-and-corrections).
6. **Record medicine only when it is handed out.** The pharmacist chooses
   **Patient** and searches for Mira. The pharmacist selects the exact medicine
   on the signed order, then chooses an in-date batch from stock. If the order
   names `Crocin Suspension 120 mg/5 mL`, check that the bottle has the same
   name and strength. Do not substitute the `240 mg/5 mL` product. The
   clinician's signed order gives the directions; this example gives no dose.
   Check the pack, expiry, and prescribed quantity. The sale has its own
   pharmacy Invoice and payment; it does not replace the OPD visit bill. See the
   [medicine examples](/docs/pharmacy#practice-enter-three-products-correctly).
7. **Check the result.** Open the patient's record. Use **Visits** to see the
   visit and **Billing** to see the invoices and receipts. If the patient needs
   another visit, book it and follow the supervisor's instructions.

Ask for the guardian before entering a child's record. Enter only confirmed
details. Do not fill an unknown allergy, diagnosis, or medicine direction with a
guess.

## Book an appointment

Choose **OPD**, then **New Appointment**. You can also choose **Book
Appointment** in a patient's record. In that case HMS fills in the patient for
you.

![New appointment form. The example is not submitted.](/docs/images/guides/new-appointment.webp)

Fill the form from top to bottom:

1. **Patient** — search and choose the correct person. If there is no safe
   match, register the person first.
2. **Care team** — choose the department and practitioner.
3. **When** — choose **Now** for a person who has arrived, or **Later** for a
   future appointment.
4. **Services** — add a billable service if one is known. This part is optional.
   An attendance fee may also appear. Ask a supervisor if you do not know which
   service or price to use.
5. Check the amount and choose **Confirm** once.

If you change the form and then leave, HMS asks what you want to do with the
unsaved form. Choose to stay if you have more to enter. Choose to leave only if
you mean to discard those changes.

### Choose Now or Later

**Now** means the patient is here. For a billable visit, **Confirm** opens
**Review and collect**. Check the amount, advance credit, and payment lines,
then choose **Confirm Walk-In**. HMS saves the checked-in visit, token, invoice,
and any payment together. If you received no money, clear the payment amount;
check any advance credit before saving. The unpaid amount stays due.

If the total is zero, **Confirm** saves the visit and token directly, without an
invoice or payment. After saving, HMS opens the visit's **Clinical** tab.

**Later** means the patient will come in the future. Pick a future date and
time. Services are optional. If you add one, HMS saves its current price for the
visit; check the price again when the patient arrives. Find the appointment in
the day list and choose **Check In**.

**Check In** changes a linked patient's appointment right away. If an older
booking has only caller details, HMS first asks you to find or register the
patient. Check the patient before confirming. Use check-in only after arrival;
it gives the patient a token and adds the configured attendance fee for billing.

## Work from the OPD day list

Choose **OPD** to see the day's appointments. Search for a patient, choose a
different date, or open **Filters** to narrow the list. Each row shows the
patient, token, time, practitioner, visit status, and any balance.

![OPD day list with appointments, visit status, balance, and Check In actions.](/docs/images/guides/opd-day-list.webp)

- **Booked** — the patient has a future or planned visit.
- **Checked In** — the patient has arrived.
- **Cancelled** — the appointment was cancelled, including a visit that had
  already checked in.
- **No show** — the booked patient did not arrive.

For a booked patient who has arrived, choose **Check In** once. For a checked-in
patient, choose the patient's name to open the visit. If the status or name
looks wrong, stop and ask the supervisor before changing it.

Use **Load More** at the bottom of a long list to see older rows. Cancelled and
No show visits are closed. Their waiting Charges are not left available to bill.

## Reschedule, cancel, or record a no-show

Open the appointment by choosing its patient or caller name. Check the date and
status before choosing an action.

- **Reschedule:** available for a booked appointment. Choose the new date and
  time, then **Reschedule**. Find the appointment under its new date in OPD.
- **Cancel:** enter the reason, then choose **Cancel Appointment**. A booked
  or checked-in appointment can be cancelled.
- **No Show:** use this for a booked patient who did not arrive. Read the
  confirmation and choose **Mark No Show**. Reading an older day's list also
  marks remaining past bookings as no-shows.

Cancellation and no-show remove items still waiting to be billed. They do not
cancel an issued invoice or return money. Use the [billing correction
steps](/docs/billing-and-corrections#correct-an-issued-invoice) when money needs a
separate correction.

## Use the visit screen

The visit screen has two tabs. **Clinical** shows who arrived and which team
will see them. It also has **Print Slip** and **Paper prescription**.

![Clinical visit screen with Print Slip and Paper prescription controls.](/docs/images/guides/opd-appointment-clinical.webp)

To attach a signed prescription scan, check the patient name, choose **Attach
Scan**, and select an image or PDF. Selecting the file starts the upload and
attaches it to this visit. Wait for it to appear, then choose **Open** to check
it. The signed paper remains the clinical source.

If you attached the wrong file, use its remove button. This removes the link
from the visit but keeps the private file in **Files**. Attach the correct scan.
An authorized administrator can delete the unused file from **Files**.

Choose **Billing** to see Charges and invoices for this visit. A Charge records
a priced service or item. A pending Charge is waiting to be billed. Do not issue
it until the person responsible for billing has checked the patient, item, and
amount.

![Visit Billing tab with a Charge waiting for review.](/docs/images/guides/opd-appointment-billing.webp)

If HMS says the Charges changed since you opened the bill, choose **Review
Updated Charges** and check the new items and amount before you continue.

Continue with [Billing and corrections](/docs/billing-and-corrections) before you
issue an invoice, record money, or correct a bill.

## Call patients for their next sitting

A **sitting** is one visit in a treatment plan.

1. Open **OPD**, then choose **Follow-ups** in the **Status** filter.
2. Check the patient's name, phone number, plan, and next-sitting note.
3. Agree the next visit with the patient. Use the row's booking button to start
   an appointment with the patient and plan already selected.
4. Choose the care team, **Later**, and the agreed date and time. Save once.
5. Check that the booking appears on that day's OPD list.

This list shows open plans that are due or have no next date, with no sitting
booked for today or later. Booking a sitting removes the plan from the call
list.

## Example: a dentist treatment plan

A treatment plan helps the desk track agreed procedure work over several visits.
It does not choose treatment for the dentist. The dentist decides what work is
needed, its price, and how many visits it will take.

### Prepare the dental services once

An administrator sets up a **Dental** department, its dentist, a **Dental
consultation** service, and the procedure services the dentist approves. For
example, the catalog may have **Root canal treatment** as a procedure. Set the
real price, tax, and fees from the hospital's approved list. Follow
[Administration and handover](/docs/administration-and-handover).

### Record one made-up course

Suppose the dentist accepts a two-visit root-canal course for **₹2,400**. This
amount is only a training example. Use the amount the dentist and hospital
approved for the real patient.

1. Open the correct patient's **checked-in** dental visit.
2. In **Treatment plan**, choose **New plan**.
3. Check the dentist's name. Choose **Root canal treatment** from the
   procedure list.
4. Enter **₹2,400** as the total price and **2** as the estimated sittings.
   The screen shows about **₹1,200 per sitting**. If the price differs from
   the service list, enter a note that explains the approved price.
5. Enter the next sitting date and a short note. The dentist should enter any
   tooth or site detail from the clinical chart. Do not guess a tooth number.
6. Check the patient, procedure, total, and date. Choose **Create plan** once.

The current visit becomes the first sitting. To bill the work delivered at that
visit, choose **Bill this sitting**. HMS adds a Charge to that visit. Open its
**Billing** tab, check the Charge, and issue or collect the bill using the
[billing
steps](/docs/billing-and-corrections#check-a-visit-before-making-its-invoice).

For the second sitting, book a new appointment and choose the plan under
**Sitting for**. If the visit was created without that link, open its
**Clinical** tab, choose the plan, and use **Add visit to plan**. After
check-in, check the work before you choose **Bill this sitting**. The last
sitting takes any small rounding difference that remains. Complete the plan only
after its work is finished and every item has been billed or dropped with a
reason.

The patient's **Treatment** tab shows a summary. Use the OPD visit to create a
plan, add a sitting, or bill work. A plan records procedures and amounts; it is
not a medicine prescription or a substitute for the dentist's clinical chart.

## Change or finish a treatment plan

Open a linked visit's **Clinical** tab to work on the plan:

- **Add Item:** add another approved procedure and its total course price.
- **Next Sitting:** save the requested date and a short note. This updates the
  reminder; it does not book an appointment.
- **Other Amount:** bill an approved amount for this sitting, up to the amount
  left on the plan item. Check the figure before saving.
- **Drop Item** in the item's **⋯** menu: stop the remaining work on that item
  and enter a reason. This does not undo earlier bills.
- **Complete Plan:** use it when the course is finished. It appears when each
  item is fully billed or dropped.

If the patient stops the course early, open the patient's **Treatment** tab,
choose **Close Plan**, and enter the reason. Check unpaid bills and unused
advances separately. Closing a plan does not cancel bills or refund money.
