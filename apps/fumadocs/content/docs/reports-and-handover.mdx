> **About the pictures:** These pictures use made-up demo records. Names,
> dates, amounts, and report rows are examples.

## Open a report

Choose **Reports** in the left menu. The list shows reports your role can open.
Choose the report that matches the question you need to answer.

![Reports list with daily, OPD, tax, and accounting reports.](/images/guides/reports-home.png)

Choose a date or date range near the top of a report. Check the dates before you
print or export. Choose **Print / PDF** for a paper or PDF copy. Choose **Export
Excel** when you need a spreadsheet. Refresh the report before handover so it
includes recently entered money. Check that the spreadsheet's dates and totals
match the report you intend to hand over.

## Close a shift: Daily Collections

Use **Daily Collections** to see money recorded for a day or a month. Each row
is one date in the hospital's time zone. Payments are money paid against bills.
Advances are money held for later care. Refunds and advance refunds are money
returned. **Net** is payments plus advances, minus both kinds of refund. The
payment-method summary helps compare cash, UPI, Card, and Bank transfer.

![Daily Collections report with daily amounts and print and export buttons.](/images/guides/report-daily-collections.png)

Example: to close today's shift, choose **Today**, check the row for today's
date, then compare each payment method with the cash drawer, payment machine,
and bank record. Check refunds and advances too. Ask the supervisor about any
difference before you hand over the shift.

## Check visits: OPD Register

Use **OPD Register** to review visits in a date range. Each row is one visit.
The report includes the patient, visit, practitioner, and billing details that
HMS recorded.

![OPD Register report with one row for each visit.](/images/guides/report-opd-register.png)

Example: choose the dates for last week's clinic, then print the list for the
manager. This report does not replace the patient's own visit record.

## Review tax: GST outward register

Use **GST outward register** to review tax on issued Invoices and Credit Notes
for a period. It groups the values and tax by the saved invoice details.

![GST outward register with taxable totals and CGST and SGST amounts.](/images/guides/report-gst.png)

The report's CGST and SGST split assumes the sale was within the same state.
This report is not a tax filing form. Have the hospital's accountant check the
tax setup and every filing before it is sent to a tax authority.

## Review account totals

Use **Trial Balance** to see opening amounts, changes, and closing amounts for
each billing account.

![Trial Balance report with account totals.](/images/guides/report-trial-balance.png)

Use **Balance Sheet** to see the financial position from the HMS billing
records.

![Balance Sheet report with totals for the period.](/images/guides/report-balance-sheet.png)

An accountant should check both reports. They only include activity recorded in
HMS. They do not include expenses, salaries, bank checks, inventory accounting,
manual journal entries, or the hospital's final accounts.

## Hand over the report

1. Check the organization name and the dates at the top.
2. Read the totals and compare them with the source records.
3. Choose **Print / PDF** or **Export Excel**.
4. Note any unpaid bills, refunds still due, unused advances, or differences
   that the next shift must check. Name the person responsible for each follow-up.
5. Give the report and notes to the person responsible for the handover.

If a total looks wrong, check the original patient, invoice, payment, or refund.
Do not change old money records to make a report total match. Ask the billing
supervisor or accountant to find the cause.
