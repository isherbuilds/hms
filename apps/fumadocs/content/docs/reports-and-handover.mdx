## Open a report

Choose **Reports** in the left menu. The list shows reports your role can open.
Choose the report that matches the question you need to answer.

![Reports list with daily, OPD, tax, and accounting reports.](/docs/images/guides/reports-home.webp)

Choose a date or date range near the top of a report. Check the dates before you
print or export. Choose **Print / PDF** for a paper or PDF copy. Choose **Export
Excel** when you need a spreadsheet. Reload the page before handover so the
report includes recently entered money. Check that the spreadsheet's dates and
totals match the report you intend to hand over.

## Close a shift: Daily Collections

Use **Daily Collections** to see money recorded for a day or a month. Each row
is one date in the hospital's time zone. Payments are money paid against bills.
Advances are money held for later care. Refunds and advance refunds are money
returned. **Net** is payments plus advances, minus both kinds of refund. The
payment-method summary helps compare cash, UPI, Card, and Bank transfer.

![Daily Collections report with daily amounts and print and export buttons.](/docs/images/guides/report-daily-collections.webp)

Example: to close today's shift, choose **Today**, check the row for today's
date, then compare each payment method with the cash drawer, payment machine,
and bank record. Check refunds and advances too. Ask the supervisor about any
difference before you hand over the shift.

## Check visits: OPD Register

Use **OPD Register** to review visits in a date range. Each row is one visit.
The report includes the patient, visit, practitioner, and billing details that
HMS recorded.

![OPD Register report with one row for each visit.](/docs/images/guides/report-opd-register.webp)

Example: choose the dates for last week's clinic, then print the list for the
manager. This report does not replace the patient's own visit record.

## Review every issued bill: Invoice Register

Owners, administrators and accountants can open **Invoice register** and
**Revenue control**. Report access does not grant access to a patient visit,
pharmacy sale or money document; source links use those records' own permissions.
Both reports currently cover OPD and pharmacy, not IPD or other hospital systems.

The Invoice Register starts at the hospital's configured fiscal-year start and
ends today. Choose up to 366 days, an OPD/pharmacy stream, or search by the full
invoice number, patient name or MRN. Each row is one issued Invoice, with its
original number and fiscal year. **Number series** shows the count and
first/last issued numbers for each stream and year; it does not renumber
history.

The dates choose the bills, but the balances are as of now: Payments, allocated
advances, Credit Notes and Refunds include later movements. This is not the debt
position at the end of an old month. **Paid** means Payments only;
**Allocated credit** is separate. The billing worklists combine both. Advance
money does not reduce a bill until allocated. Negative outstanding means
**Refund due**, not zero.

Issued line value excludes GST and includes exempt care; discounts are already
deducted. The Invoice total includes GST and round-off. Net billed is Invoice
total less Credit Notes; outstanding is net billed less Payments and allocated
credit, plus Refunds. Do not compare OPD and pharmacy subtotal as if they used
the same tax-inclusive pricing basis.

The totals and **Number series** cover every matching Invoice, not just the
loaded rows. Choose **Load more** to see more documents, newest issue date
first. Open an authorized Invoice, Visit or Sale to check its source. Excel has
every match, on a register sheet and a number series sheet. Print / PDF has the
totals, the number series and only the loaded rows.

## Owner review: Revenue control

Choose up to 92 days for billed revenue and review signals. The expiry section
always shows stock as it is now. For one day's collections by payment method,
use [Daily Collections](#close-a-shift-daily-collections). Reading this report
does not change source records.

### Keep billing and collections separate

**Net billed revenue** uses issued Invoice line value on the Invoice's Business
Date, less Credit Note line value on the Credit Note's own Business Date.
It excludes GST, includes exempt care, has discounts already netted and shows
round-off separately. A return against an older bill can make a correction-only
period negative. Refunds change cash and balances, not revenue again.
Advances are receipts for later care, not billed revenue.

Review billed revenue by stream, practitioner and saved service revenue category.
OPD follows the Appointment's practitioner. Pharmacy follows its linked visit's
practitioner, not prescriber earnings; counter sales with no linked visit stay
**Unassigned**. Practitioner names are current display names.
Categories come from issued lines, not today's Service master or the
Appointment's department. Round-off is not assigned to practitioners/categories.
These totals are not doctor payouts, profit or a department revenue report.

**Why the invoice register differs** explains why the Invoice Register and this
report's net billed revenue can differ. On the same pre-GST line-value basis:

> Net billed revenue = register net line value − credits to older invoices +
> later credits on these invoices.

Do not alter old records just to force these two different date bases to match.

### Review signals, not an accusation or loss estimate

Next to **Review**, choose a kind. HMS shows the count and total amount of all
matching records. For invoice discounts and credit notes, a table also groups
them by who issued them and the reason. Choose **Load more** for more rows, and
use the link in **Open** to check the source record. Counts and amounts use
different bases and can overlap. Never add them to a “total lost revenue.”
Check the recorded person, reason and source before deciding whether something
needs correction.

- **Checked-in visits with no Charge:** shows a count, not an amount. Each row
  says whether the visit was never charged or all its Charges were voided. A
  zero-priced non-voided Charge counts as charged. Deliberate free care is
  valid; no missed fee is guessed and no reason is required for intentional
  omission. The separate aged pending-Charge worklist still follows the
  hospital's unbilled alert-hours setting.
- **Voided Charges:** use recorded last-update date in the hospital timezone,
  not a captured historical void time. The price/quantity, void reason and
  creator are shown; the creator is **not** the void actor, which was not captured.
- **Invoice discounts:** use Invoice Business Date, recorded issuer and exact
  saved note. Issuer/reason groups preserve the saved text. Do not subtract
  discounts twice or invent a reason category.
- **Credit Notes:** use their own Business Date and recorded issuer/reason.
  They can represent returns or mistakes, not only discounts. Missing legacy
  reasons remain **Not recorded**.
- **Sales below MRP:** lists issued pharmacy lines with a discount or a price
  below the saved batch MRP, showing the line subtotal and its discount. Paise
  rounding and returns are not new concessions; this is concession review, not
  evidence of stock theft.
- **Stock adjustments and write-offs:** lists count corrections, breakage and
  write-offs by movement creation date in the hospital timezone, with
  quantity, bucket, actor and note, valued at MRP. Internal issues and
  quarantine or release moves are not listed.
- **Refunds:** use stored Refund Business Date and distinguish invoice from
  advance Refunds, with method and refunder. Refunds do not store a reason;
  a linked Credit Note reason is shown if present, otherwise **Not recorded**.

### Review expiry now

Under **Expiry exposure now, at MRP**, choose **30 days** or **90 days**. This
does not depend on the report dates. The totals separate shelf from quarantine
and expired from upcoming batches. Expiry is valid through the printed date.
Stocked inactive/internal products remain included; batches without an expiry
date are excluded from exposure and counted visibly.

**MRP exposure is not purchase cost or inventory valuation.** It uses the saved
batch MRP and counted quantity, rounded once per batch/bucket. The same basis
applies to write-off review; it is not a profit-loss posting. Shelf/quarantine
and expired/upcoming are different views of the same exposure: do not add all
four totals. Unknown purchase/opening cost is not zero. Cost and recoverable
value require separate supported records and accounting assessment.

### Print or export Revenue control

**Print / PDF** prints the revenue totals and tables, why the invoice register
differs, and the four expiry totals. It leaves out the expiry batch rows and
the review signals. **Export Excel** has sheets for revenue by stream,
practitioner and category, the correction bridge, one sheet for each review
kind with every matching row, and expiry exposure. Check the dates and the
expiry horizon before you print or export.

## Review tax: GST outward register

Use **GST outward register** to review tax on issued Invoices and Credit Notes
for a period. It groups the values and tax by the saved invoice details.

![GST outward register with taxable totals and CGST and SGST amounts.](/docs/images/guides/report-gst.webp)

The report's CGST and SGST split covers the local same-state supplies supported
by HMS. The state comes from the organization's GSTIN, not the patient's home
state. Qualifying exempt OPD care uses **Bill of Supply**;
registered pharmacy uses **Tax Invoice**. Without GSTIN, neutral documents make
no GST claim. See [Organization setup](/docs/administration-and-handover#1-check-organization).
This report is not a tax filing form. Have the hospital's accountant check the
actual tax setup, saved documents and every filing before it is sent to a tax
authority.

## Review account totals

Use **Trial Balance** to see opening amounts, changes, and closing amounts for
each billing account.

![Trial Balance report with account totals.](/docs/images/guides/report-trial-balance.webp)

Use **Balance Sheet** to see the financial position from the HMS billing
records.

![Balance Sheet report with totals for the period.](/docs/images/guides/report-balance-sheet.webp)

An accountant should check both reports. They only include activity recorded in
HMS. They do not include expenses, salaries, bank checks, inventory accounting,
manual journal entries, or the hospital's final accounts.

## Hand over the report

1. Check the organization name and the dates at the top.
2. Read the totals and compare them with the source records.
3. Choose **Print / PDF** or **Export Excel**.
4. Note any unpaid bills, refunds still due, unused advances, or differences
   that the next shift must check. Name the person responsible for each follow-up.
5. Give the report and notes to the person responsible for the handover.

If a total looks wrong, check the original patient, invoice, payment, or refund.
Do not change old money records to make a report total match. Ask the billing
supervisor or accountant to find the cause.

For month/year accountant handover, keep four reconciliations separate: net
issued/credited line values on each document's own date (discounts already
netted, GST excluded and round-off separate); Payments plus advances less both
Refund kinds (allocations excluded from new money); GST outward exempt/taxable
values and tax with traceable credits; and billing-journal Trial Balance opening,
debits, credits and closing balances. Tie them to revenue, cash/bank, advance
liability, receivable and tax accounts. These remain partial billing books, not
the hospital's final accounts or tax return.

For Tally entry, retain one Sales voucher per Invoice and one Credit Note
voucher per Credit Note, with the full issued HMS number as voucher number and
bill-wise reference. Keep the original Invoice number/date on credits. Use
dedicated HMS voucher types with Manual numbering and duplicate prevention;
an automatic migration type must retain original numbers and be checked for
renumbering. Excel is source evidence, not a Tally import adapter.

Historical as-of debt/stock, department snapshots, purchase-cost valuation and
patient/payer claim-balance extensions require their own source-model features.
Do not fill those gaps with invented revenue, fake Payments or inferred names.
