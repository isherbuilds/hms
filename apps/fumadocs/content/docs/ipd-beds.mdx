---
title: IPD beds and admissions
description: Admit Patients to beds, issue their medicines, bill the stay, and record when they leave
---

IPD tracks who is in which bed, the medicines and services given during the
stay, and the one bill for it. Bed days are not billed yet: add them to the
bill as a service, or keep them on paper, as your administrator decides.

## Set up wards and beds

An administrator opens **IPD**, then **Wards and beds**:

1. **New ward**, for example General Ward or ICU.
2. **New bed type**, for example General bed or Private room, then **Set rate**
   on its row. Choose the service the day is billed as, the daily rate, and
   when the rate starts. A new rate applies to beds taken after it starts;
   Patients already in a bed keep their rate.
3. **New bed**: choose the ward and bed type and give the label staff use, such
   as GW-01. A label can repeat in another ward.

A bed type with no rate shows **No rate**, and its beds cannot take a Patient.
**Retire** removes a bed from the board without losing its history. A bed with a
Patient in it cannot be retired.

## The bed board

**IPD** opens on **Beds**: one panel per ward, each bed **Free**, **Occupied**
or **Blocked**. An occupied bed shows the Patient and opens their admission.

- **Admit** on a free bed: find or register the Patient and choose the admitting
  doctor.
- **⋯ → Block bed** takes a free bed out of use, with a reason, for example
  repairs.
- **Release bed** puts a blocked bed back in use once it is clean.

## During the stay

Open the admission from the board or from **Admissions**.

- **Move bed** moves the Patient to a free bed. The old bed is free at once.
- **Ready to leave** records the signed discharge handoff. The Patient keeps
  the bed until they leave.
- **Record departure** ends the stay. Payment is not needed first. The bed
  becomes **Blocked** until someone releases it after cleaning.
- **⋯ → Not leaving yet** returns a ready Patient to admitted, with a reason.

If someone else changed the admission on another screen, HMS refuses your
change. Refresh and check before trying again.

## Issue medicines to an inpatient

For the pharmacist. Medicines for a Patient in a bed are issued to their
admission, not sold at the counter.

1. Open the Patient's admission and choose **Issue medicines**.
2. Pick each medicine and batch, and the quantity in tablets, vials or other
   smallest units: the field names the unit, so a strip of 10 is 10, not 1.
3. For each line choose how it is supplied:
   - **Part of treatment (no GST)**: the usual choice. The medicine is part of
     the Patient's care and carries no GST.
   - **Sold separately (GST as on pack)**: only for something the Patient buys
     apart from their treatment. It carries the GST printed on the pack.
4. Enter the prescribing doctor where the medicine needs one, then **Issue**.

The stock leaves the shelf at once. Nothing is paid now: each line waits on the
admission until the stay is billed. You can issue while the Patient is admitted
or ready to leave, not after the bill is issued.

## Bill an inpatient stay

For the billing desk.

1. Wait for **Ready to leave**, the signed discharge handoff. The bill cannot
   be issued before it.
2. Open the admission. The bill lists every service and medicine not yet
   billed. Use **Add service** for anything missing, such as a procedure,
   before you issue.
3. Check the total. Services show their rate; medicines show their MRP, batch
   and expiry. For a GST-registered hospital the bill also shows **GST added**
   on services and **GST included in MRP** for medicines.
4. **Issue invoice**, and take payment now or record why some is left to pay.

A service added by mistake can be taken off before the bill is issued: on its
line, **⋯ → Remove**, and give the reason. Issued medicines cannot be taken
back yet.

An admission has one bill. Once issued, it cannot be edited or corrected yet,
and nothing more can be added to the stay.

If someone else issued medicines or added a service while you were reviewing,
HMS refuses the bill. Refresh, check the new lines, and issue again.

## Corrections

Only an administrator can:

- record an admission, move, ready or departure that happened more than ten
  minutes ago. Fill **Only if it happened earlier** with the time and a reason.
  HMS refuses a time that clashes with another Patient's stay in that bed.
- **⋯ → Cancel admission**, for an admission made in error. Once the Patient
  received care, or any medicine or service was recorded, record their
  departure instead.

Both are kept in the audit log with the reason.
