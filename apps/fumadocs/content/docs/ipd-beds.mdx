---
title: IPD beds and admissions
description: Admit Patients to beds, issue their medicines, take deposits, bill the stay, and record when they leave
---

IPD tracks who is in which bed, the medicines and services given during the
stay, the deposit held, and the bill. Bed days are worked out from the bed the
Patient was in, so nobody posts them each day.

## Set up wards and beds

An administrator opens **IPD**, then **Wards and beds**:

1. **New ward**, for example General Ward or ICU.
2. **New bed type**, for example General bed or Private room, then **Set rate**
   on its row. Under **Charged each day**, choose the service the room is billed
   as and its **Daily price**. Use **Add line** for anything else charged every
   day in that bed, such as nursing or a duty doctor (up to ten lines). Set
   **Starts** and choose **Save rate**. A new rate applies to beds taken after
   it starts; Patients already in a bed keep their rate. A line with a daily
   price of 0 adds nothing to the bill.
3. **New bed**: choose the ward and bed type and give the label staff use, such
   as GW-01. A label can repeat in another ward.

The form reminds you that non-ICU room rent above ₹5,000 a day carries 5% GST
on the whole amount: set that rate on the room service in the catalog. A
hospital without a GSTIN cannot use a service that charges GST.

A bed type with no rate shows **No rate**, and its beds cannot take a Patient.
**Retire** removes a bed from the board without losing its history. A bed with a
Patient in it cannot be retired.

## The bed board

**IPD** opens on **Beds**: one panel per ward, each bed **Free**, **Occupied**
or **Blocked**. An occupied bed shows the Patient, the **Running bill** and any **Deposit**,
and opens their admission. **Deposit short by** means the running bill is
higher than the deposit: ask the family for more. Care is never stopped for
money.

- **Admit** on a free bed: find or register the Patient and choose the admitting
  doctor. Add the **Attendant** and **Attendant phone**, a one-line
  **Diagnosis**, and tick **Medico-legal case** with the **Police intimation or
  MLC number** when it applies. Each new admission gets an **IPD No.**, such as
  2026-27/14. After saving, choose **Print admission slip**.
- **⋯ → Block bed** takes a free bed out of use, with a reason, for example
  repairs.
- **Release bed** puts a blocked bed back in use once it is clean.

## Reserve a bed for a planned admission

For reception. Open **IPD**, then **Reservations**. Choose **Reserve a bed**,
find the Patient, choose the bed type and the planned arrival and departure.

- A **request** asks for a bed type and goes on the **Waiting list**. It does
  not keep any bed free.
- A **held bed** keeps one bed for that Patient. Choose **Hold a bed**, pick a
  free bed and set **Hold until**: the latest time you will keep the bed if the
  Patient has not arrived. Nobody else can be admitted or moved into a held bed,
  unless an owner or administrator overrides the hold with a reason.

Only a free bed can be held. A bed with a Patient in it cannot be held, even for
next week: nobody knows for certain when that Patient will leave. Put the
Patient on the waiting list and hold a bed once one is free.

The views:

- **Arrivals**: held beds with Patients due in the next two days, and late
  Patients while their bed is still held.
- **Waiting list**: requests that still need a bed.
- **Needs attention**: reservations that need a decision now.
- **All**: every reservation.

When the Patient arrives, choose **Admit** on their reservation. The admission
uses the held bed. If they come outside the planned dates, **Change dates**
first. If someone else also has a reservation on that bed, even for later dates,
move it to another bed or cancel it before admitting: a Patient in a bed has no
fixed leaving time. **Cancel reservation** needs a reason.

For a late Patient, **Change dates** can keep the planned arrival and set a later
**Hold until**. Clearing **Hold until** on a waiting-list request removes its
deadline.

Under **Needs attention**:

- **Needs a bed again**: an owner or administrator blocked the held bed or
  admitted someone to it. Hold another bed or cancel the reservation.
- **Bed blocked**: the held bed was blocked by someone else. The bed stays held;
  release the bed before the Patient arrives, or move the reservation.
- **Hold lapsed**: **Hold until** passed before the Patient arrived. The bed is
  no longer kept. **Change dates** with a new **Hold until** to keep it again,
  or cancel.

Blocking a bed, for example for repairs, always works. When an owner or
administrator blocks it, its holds are released and show **Needs a bed again**.
When anyone else blocks it, the holds stay and show **Bed blocked** under
**Needs attention**; releasing the bed does not let a walk-in take it. A held bed
cannot be retired.

Only an owner or administrator can admit or move a Patient into a bed held for
someone else, and they must give a reason. That reservation then shows **Needs a
bed again**.

## During the stay

Open the admission from the board or from **Admissions**.

- **Move bed** moves the Patient to a free bed. The old bed is free at once.
- **Ready to leave** records the signed discharge handoff. The Patient keeps
  the bed until they leave.
- **Record departure** ends the stay. Choose **How the Patient left**: normal
  discharge, against medical advice, absconded, referred to another hospital,
  or death. Payment is not needed first. The bed becomes **Blocked** until
  someone releases it after cleaning. Then **Print discharge slip**: it has a
  signature line for the discharge summary, the ward nurse, pharmacy and
  billing.
- **⋯ → Not leaving yet** returns a ready Patient to admitted, with a reason.
- **⋯ → Edit details** changes the attendant, diagnosis, medico-legal details
  or the **Doctor** while the Patient is in hospital. Changing the doctor needs
  a reason.
- **⋯ → Print admission slip** prints the slip again at any time.

If someone else changed the admission on another screen, HMS refuses your
change. Refresh and check before trying again.

### Undo a departure recorded by mistake

For an owner or administrator, within 24 hours of the departure. Open the
admission and choose **⋯ → Undo departure**. Say **What went wrong** and
choose **Undo departure**. The Patient goes back to the same bed. Bills already
issued stay as they are; anything added later goes on a further bill.

HMS refuses, and the departure stays recorded, when:

- the bed was retired or blocked for anything other than cleaning;
- another Patient has used the bed, or this Patient was admitted again;
- the bed is held for another Patient.

Then admit the Patient again.

## Give a family an estimate

Before admission, open **IPD**, then **Estimate**. Choose the **Bed type** and
**Expected days**, add any **Expected services**, and fill **For (optional)**
with the name to print. The panel shows bed charges, services, GST where it
applies, the total, and a **Suggested deposit**: the total rounded up to a
whole ₹100. Choose **Print estimate**. The paper says **Estimate — not a bill**.
HMS does not save it.

## Issue medicines to an inpatient

For the pharmacist. Medicines for a Patient in a bed are issued to their
admission, not sold at the counter.

1. Open the Patient's admission and choose **Issue medicines**.
2. Pick each medicine and batch, and the quantity in tablets, vials or other
   smallest units: the field names the unit, so a strip of 10 is 10, not 1.
3. In a GST-registered hospital, choose how each line is supplied:
   - **Part of treatment (no GST)**: the usual choice. The medicine is part of
     the Patient's care and carries no GST.
   - **Sold separately (GST as on pack)**: only for something the Patient buys
     apart from their treatment. It carries the GST printed on the pack.

   A hospital without a GSTIN is not asked: every medicine is part of treatment.

4. Enter the prescribing doctor where the medicine needs one, then **Issue**.

The stock leaves the shelf at once. Nothing is paid now: each line waits on the
admission until the stay is billed. You can issue while the Patient is admitted
or ready to leave, also after a bill has been issued: the new lines go on the
next bill.

## Return ward medicine

For the pharmacist. Medicine that comes back from the ward unused is returned
to the admission, not sold back.

1. On the admission, find **Medicines issued** and choose **Return**.
2. Enter how many came back on each line, in the same smallest units as the
   issue. HMS shows how many are left to return.
3. Give the **Reason** and choose **Return**.

Returned medicine goes to quarantine, not back on the shelf. Medicine not yet
billed comes off the bill. Medicine already billed gets a credit note on its
bill: choose **Print credit note**. Return medicines from one bill at a time.
The line then shows how many were returned.

## Take a deposit

For the billing desk, while the Patient is in hospital.

1. Open the admission. In **Bill**, choose **Take deposit**.
2. Enter the amount and method, and the reference for a non-cash payment.
3. Choose **Record deposit**, then **Print receipt**.

The deposit is an Advance Receipt for this stay only. It pays only this stay's
bills, never a pharmacy counter sale or an OPD visit. The admission shows the
**Deposit** beside the **Running bill**.

To show the family what the stay has cost so far, choose **Print interim bill**.
It says **Interim bill — not an Invoice or Receipt** and has no number.

## Bill an inpatient stay

For the billing desk.

1. Wait for **Ready to leave**, the signed discharge handoff. The bill cannot
   be issued before it.
2. Open the admission. **Bill** lists every service and medicine not yet
   billed, and the bed days: **Bed days to bill** gives the count and dates.
   Use **Add service** for anything missing, such as a procedure.
3. Choose the **Last day**: **Full**, **Half** or **Don't charge**. Half and
   Don't charge need a **Reason**.
4. Check the total. Services show their rate; medicines show their MRP, batch
   and expiry. For a GST-registered hospital the bill also shows **GST added**
   on services and **GST included in MRP** for medicines.
5. Choose **Issue invoice**. In **Review and collect**, **Use advance credit**
   starts with this stay's deposit. Enter a **Discount** if one is approved,
   take payment for the rest, and write the **Note**: it is needed for a
   discount or for anything left to pay.

### How bed days are counted

The first day runs from admission to the checkout time on the next calendar
day. Each later day runs from checkout to checkout. Leaving exactly at the
checkout time adds no day. With a noon checkout: admitted Monday 15:00 and left
Tuesday 12:00 is one day; left Tuesday 12:01 is two. A day split between two
beds is charged at the bed type the Patient spent longest in that day. Your
administrator sets the checkout time; see
[Organization](/docs/administration-and-handover#1-check-organization).

If a new bed day starts at checkout while you are collecting, HMS refuses the
bill and shows the current one. Check the total and issue again.

When only an uncharged last day is left, there is nothing to charge: choose
**Mark day as dealt with**. No invoice is issued.

### After the bill

A stay can have more than one bill. A service or medicine added after the first
bill goes on the next one. An issued bill shows under **Bill** with **Record
payment**, **⋯ → Issue credit note** and **Record refund**, as for an OPD visit;
see [Correct an issued invoice](/docs/billing-and-corrections#correct-an-issued-invoice).

When nothing is left to bill and the stay still holds deposit, choose **Refund
deposit** beside the receipt to give the rest back.

A GST-registered hospital needs the Patient's address on file before the
invoice can be issued. If it is missing, add it to the Patient's record first.

A hospital without a GSTIN cannot bill GST. **Add service** will not take a
service set to charge GST: set it to 0% in the catalog first. If a service with
GST is already on the bill (the GSTIN was removed later), the invoice is
refused: remove that line and add the service at 0%.

A service added by mistake can be taken off before it is billed: on its line,
**⋯ → Remove**, and give the reason. Medicine comes back through **Return**.

If someone else issued medicines or added a service while you were reviewing,
HMS refuses the bill. Refresh, check the new lines, and issue again.

## Corrections

Only an owner or administrator can:

- record an admission, move, ready or departure that happened more than ten
  minutes ago. Fill **Only if it happened earlier** with the time and a reason.
  HMS refuses a time that clashes with another Patient's stay in that bed, or
  an earlier move that would change a bed day already billed. A bed day billed
  in error is corrected with a credit note on its bill.
- **⋯ → Cancel admission**, for an admission made in error. Once the Patient
  received care, or any medicine or service was recorded, record their
  departure instead.

Both are kept in the audit log with the reason.
