description: A short guide to the hospital application
---

HMS keeps patients, OPD appointments, billing, reporting, files, and organization settings together.
Your account and organization access are created by an
administrator; there is no public sign-up.

## Choose the correct organization

Each organization has its own URL. Use the organization switcher in
the sidebar when you need to move between organizations. The selected
organization applies only to that browser tab.

## Main areas

- **Dashboard** summarizes today's queue, collections, and pending work.
- **Patients** registers patients and holds the patient record.
- **OPD** is the day's token queue. Opening an OPD appointment gives you two tabs over the
  same record: the clinical view and its billing tab.
- **Billing** is the organization-wide worklist for unbilled charges and unpaid
  invoices. It opens the relevant OPD billing record to add catalog services,
  issue invoices, and handle payments, credit notes, and refunds.
- **Reports** provides trial balance, balance sheet, and GST views.
- **Files** stores private organization documents.
- **Settings** manages the organization, staff, catalog, members, and audit
  history according to your role.
