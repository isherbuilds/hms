## Learn with examples

Start with the [full patient
example](/docs/opd-desk#worked-example-one-patient-from-start-to-medicine) to follow
one made-up patient from first search, through a visit, bill, and pharmacy sale.
The [dentist example](/docs/opd-desk#example-a-dentist-treatment-plan) shows how to
record a course of care over more than one visit. The [Pharmacy
guide](/docs/pharmacy#practice-enter-three-products-correctly) shows how to enter a
tablet, syrup, or eye-drop product using the details printed on its own box or
bottle.

## Before you save

Check the hospital, patient or buyer, and amount. A review panel lets you check
an action; its final button saves it. If a payment or sale result is unclear,
open the record before trying again. Ask your supervisor if you cannot confirm
whether it saved.

## The first screen

Choose **Dashboard** in the left menu to see today's work. The picture shows a
practice dashboard. Your numbers will be different.

![Dashboard example showing today's appointments, arrivals, bills to make, and money received.](/docs/images/guides/dashboard.webp)

If you are unsure where to start, ask your supervisor which job is yours. Your
role decides which buttons you can use.

## Words used in HMS

- **OPD:** outpatient care, where the patient comes for a visit without admission.
- **MRN:** the hospital's patient number.
- **Practitioner:** the doctor or other care professional for the visit.
- **Charge:** a priced service or item recorded for billing.
- **Invoice:** the bill for those services or items.
- **Receipt:** proof that a payment was recorded.
- **Advance:** money received now and held for later care.
- **Quarantine:** stock held aside and unavailable for sale.
- **MRP:** the maximum retail price printed on the medicine pack.
- **GST:** goods and services tax. **HSN** and **SAC** are product and service
  tax codes. Ask the hospital's accountant which values to enter.
