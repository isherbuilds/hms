HMS keeps patients, OPD appointments, billing, reporting, files, and organization settings together.
Your account and organization access are created by an
administrator; there is no public sign-up.

## Start here

- [Getting started](/getting-started) explains accounts, invitations, organization URLs, and access.
- [OPD desk](/opd-desk) covers patient registration, Now/Later appointments, the day list, and appointment records.
- [Billing and corrections](/billing-and-corrections) covers Charges, Invoices, Payments, Credit Notes, refunds, and printing.
- [Administration and handover](/administration-and-handover) covers setup, members, files, audit, and reports.

## Choose the correct organization

Each organization has its own URL. Use the organization switcher in
the sidebar when you need to move between organizations. The selected
organization applies only to that browser tab.

## Main areas

- **Dashboard** summarizes today's queue, collections, and pending work.
- **Patients** registers patients and holds the patient record.
- **OPD** is the dated appointment desk for booked and checked-in work. Opening an OPD
  appointment gives you two tabs over the same record: the clinical view and its billing tab.
- **Billing** is the organization-wide worklist for unbilled charges and unpaid
  invoices. Opening a row shows quick collection; use the Patient link or
  **Open visit billing** for the full OPD billing record, catalog services,
  invoices, payments, credit notes, and refunds.
- **Reports** provides the GST outward register and billing-ledger trial balance
  and balance sheet. These are HMS handover views, not a complete accounting
  system or GST filing return.
- **Files** stores private organization documents.
- **Settings** manages the organization, staff, catalog, members, and audit
  history according to your role.
