## Set up the hospital in this order

Choose **Settings** in the left menu. Do this setup with the person responsible
for the hospital. These choices affect future visits, bills, and printed papers.

### 1. Check Organization

Open **Organization**. Check the hospital's legal name, address, time zone,
financial year and document numbers. Check the currency before any money is
recorded. HMS does not let you change the currency later.

Enter **GSTIN** only for the organization's actual GST registration.
**PAN / tax id** is a separate field; it does not prove GST registration. A
registered hospital needs its legal name and address, and its financial year
must start in April. HMS takes the state from the first two digits of the
GSTIN. That state is the place of supply for the local in-person care and
counter sales supported here. Enter the pharmacy's actual
**Drug licence (Form 20)** and **Drug licence (Form 21)** numbers when
applicable; do not invent them.

Financial document prefixes use at most three letters, digits, `/` or `-`. That
leaves room for 99,999 numbers in each series every financial year. If numbering
reaches its limit, stop and ask the administrator: HMS refuses overflow instead
of issuing an invalid number.

Choose **Save settings** only after an authorized person has checked the values.
New registered OPD bills print **Bill of Supply**, pharmacy bills **Tax Invoice**,
and exempt-care advances **Receipt Voucher**. Without GSTIN, papers use
**Invoice** and **Advance Receipt** without GST claims. Issued papers keep their
saved registration, state and applicable licence details; a later change does
not change their title or particulars. Before live use, the accountant and
licensing adviser must check actual registration, tax setup and A4/80 mm prints.

![Organization settings with hospital details and document number settings.](/docs/images/guides/settings-organization.webp)

### 2. Add departments

Open **Staff**, choose **Departments**, and add the hospital's departments. Give
each department its correct default consultation fee after the matching service
exists.

![New department form. The example is not saved.](/docs/images/guides/settings-new-department.webp)

### 3. Add services and prices

Open **Services**. A service is a billable item such as a consultation, lab
test, or procedure. Choose **New Service** and enter the approved name,
category, price, tax rate, and tax code if one applies. Use the rate-at-intake
option only when the price can change for each patient.

![Services list with prices, tax rates, and active status.](/docs/images/guides/settings-catalog.webp)

![New Service form. The example is not saved.](/docs/images/guides/settings-new-service.webp)

Changing a price affects future work. It does not change an invoice that was
already issued.

### 4. Add payers when needed

Open **Payers** to add an insurer, third-party administrator, company, or
scheme. Choose **New Payer**, type the approved name, choose its type, and save.
Choose a payer on a patient record only when coverage is confirmed. This records
the sponsor details; it does not send a claim or move the bill to the sponsor.
The patient bill still shows any unpaid amount.

![Payers list.](/docs/images/guides/settings-payers.webp)

![New Payer form. The example is not saved.](/docs/images/guides/settings-new-payer.webp)

### 5. Invite people and choose their access

Open **Members**, then choose **Invite**. Type the person's email address,
choose the role, and review both before you create the invitation. Copy the
invitation link and share it directly with that person. HMS does not send it by
email. They can create an account from the link if they do not have one.

Keep the link private. If it reaches the wrong person, cancel the invitation and
create a new one. See [Getting started](/docs/getting-started#join-your-hospital).

![Members list with current people and their roles.](/docs/images/guides/settings-members.webp)

![Invitation form showing the email and role fields. Nothing is sent.](/docs/images/guides/settings-invite-member.webp)

Roles are like keys. Give each person only the key needed for their job:

- **Owner** — full access to the organization.
- **Administrator** — manages people and settings and can use the work areas.
- **Reception** — works with patients and OPD visits and can collect at intake.
- **Cashier** — handles bills, payments, and daily collection reports.
- **Accountant** — reviews bills and reports and can issue Credit Notes.
- **Pharmacist** — sells and returns medicine, receives goods, and reads Daily
  Collections. An Administrator or Owner manages products and stock adjustments.

You can change a person's role, remove a member, or cancel an unused invitation
when your role allows it. Check with the owner first if you are not sure.

### 6. Add practitioners and link the right records

Return to **Staff**, choose **Practitioners**, and add each practitioner under
the correct department. If the staff member has an HMS login, link it here.
Choose the matching consultation and follow-up fee items. Do not guess a
practitioner number or fee. Ask the person who manages staff if you are unsure.

![Staff list showing practitioners and their departments and fee items.](/docs/images/guides/settings-staff.webp)

![New practitioner form. The example is not saved.](/docs/images/guides/settings-new-practitioner.webp)

## Change access when staff duties change

Open **Settings → Members** and check the person's name and email.

- To change their role, open their **⋯** menu and choose the new role under
  **Change role**. The choice saves immediately; check the new role label.
- To stop their access to this hospital, choose **Remove from organization**
  and confirm **Remove**. Their history stays on the record.
- To stop an unused invitation, choose its cancel action and confirm
  **Cancel Invitation**. Send a new invitation if the person still needs access.

Ask the owner before changing an administrator's access. A staff record under
**Practitioners** describes care work; removing it from use is not the same as
removing a person's login access under **Members**.

## Store and find files

Choose **Files**, then **Upload** and select the correct file. HMS uploads the
file as soon as you choose it. Check the hospital name and the file name before
you pick it. Use **Search files** to find a file later; choose its download
button to open it.

![Files page with upload and search controls.](/docs/images/guides/files.webp)

Files are private to the hospital. A download link works for a short time. Do
not paste it into a message or leave it where other people can use it.

Do not delete a file unless an authorized person has checked it. A file linked
to an appointment must be detached from that appointment before it can be
deleted. Use the file's delete button, read the confirmation, and choose
**Delete** only for the file you intend to remove. A delete is permanent.

For a signed prescription scan, attach the file from the visit's **Clinical**
tab. Uploading a file to **Files** alone does not attach it to a patient's
visit.

## Read the Audit list

Open **Audit** to look for a recorded sensitive action or a permission problem.
The list is not a history of every click or every ordinary change. Check the
patient, invoice, or setting itself to understand the full record.

![Audit list showing recorded events.](/docs/images/guides/settings-audit.webp)

## Backups and a copy of your records

The Edernal team runs backups and restores. For a full copy of the hospital's
records, or to end the service, the owner emails
[support@edernal.com](mailto:support@edernal.com).

## Prepare a handover

Use [Reports and handover](/docs/reports-and-handover) for daily money, OPD, tax, and
accounting reports. HMS reports include transactions entered in HMS. They do not
replace expense records, payroll, stock accounting, bank checks, or the
accountant's final books.
