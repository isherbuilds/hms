## Set up the organization

Follow the in-app **Setup path**, then return to **Staff** after the catalog and
members are ready:

1. **Organization** — confirm legal name, address, tax identifier, currency,
   timezone, fiscal-year start, and document prefixes. These values shape money,
   business dates, MRNs, and printed document numbers. Confirm the displayed
   currency before billing begins; it cannot be changed for the organization.
2. **Staff** — create departments and practitioners. Leave member links and
   attendance fee assignments until their required records exist.
3. **Catalog** — create the active consultation and follow-up items, then
   maintain every service's code, name, category, price, tax rate, tax code, and
   active state.
4. **Members** — invite existing operator-created accounts, then assign the role
   each person needs:
   - **Owner** — full organization, operational, billing, reporting, audit, and file access.
   - **Administrator** — manage people and settings with full operational, billing, reporting, audit, and file access.
   - **Reception** — register patients, manage OPD visits, collect at intake, and upload or read files.
   - **Cashier** — view patients and OPD visits, handle billing, read files, and use Daily Collections for the shift close.
   - **Accountant** — review patients, visits, and billing; issue credit notes; and access reports, audit, and files.
     You can change a person's role, remove them, or revoke a pending invitation
     when authorized.
5. **Staff again** — link practitioners to member logins when applicable and
   assign the existing consultation and follow-up fee items.

Review identifiers, currency, timezone, prices, and taxes with the responsible
operator before live use. Changing configuration later does not rewrite already
issued financial documents.

## Files and prescription scans

**Files** stores organization documents privately. Downloads use signed links
that expire after 15 minutes. A file attached to an appointment must be detached
from that record before it can be deleted from Files. Treat a signed link as a
temporary bearer link and do not place it in messages or logs.

The OPD clinical tab is the correct place to attach a signed prescription scan to
its appointment. The general Files page does not create that clinical link.

## Audit

The Audit settings tab records selected sensitive or destructive actions and
permission denials. It is not a history of every read or every ordinary edit.
Use it to investigate who performed a recorded action; use the source record and
financial documents for the business history itself.

## Reports and handover

The available reports are derived only from transactions recorded in HMS:

- **Daily collections** — Payments minus Refunds by method and business date, for the shift close.
- **OPD register** — one row per OPD visit with attendance and billing for a date range.
- **GST outward register** — Invoice and Credit Note tax reporting for a period.
- **Trial balance** — opening, movement, and closing balances by billing-ledger account.
- **Balance sheet** — financial position produced by the HMS billing ledger.

Filter the required period or date, review the result, then print or export it for
handover. These views do not include expenses, payroll, inventory accounting,
manual journals, bank reconciliation, period close, or final accounts. The GST
register is not a filing return. Reconcile daily and have the pilot's accountant
approve classifications and statutory fields before relying on an export.

Return to [Billing and corrections](/billing-and-corrections) when a discrepancy
comes from an Invoice, Payment, Credit Note, or Refund rather than setup.
