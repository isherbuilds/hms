## Find work that needs attention

Open **Billing** for the organization-wide worklist. Its views separate all open
work, Charges waiting to be billed, unpaid Invoices, and Invoices more than seven
days old. Opening a row keeps your place in the worklist and opens a quick sheet:

- For an issued Invoice, choose the payment method, amount, and any required
  reference, then choose **Collect**.
- For Charges not yet invoiced, choose **Issue invoice** to open the full OPD
  Appointment Billing tab.

The Patient link and **Open visit billing** also open the full Billing tab when
you need to review Charges, issue a document, or make a correction.

## Issue from pending Charges

Open the full OPD Appointment Billing tab from the worklist sheet or Patient
link. Before issue, review the patient, pending Charge lines, quantity, price,
tax, and grand total. You may add catalog services or void a pending Charge when
your role allows it. Enter any required discount note and payment references
before submitting.

- Choose **Issue invoice** when nothing is collected now.
- Choose **Issue and collect** when one or more payment lines carry money.
- A collection can use up to four payment lines. Card and UPI lines require their
  transaction reference; cash does not.

If HMS reports that the Charges changed since review, reload the appointment and
review the new lines and total. Do not retry against a stale screen.

## Know the documents

- A **Charge** is a priced billable event waiting to be included in an Invoice.
- An **Invoice** is the immutable itemized supply document. Until the pilot's
  accountant approves a statutory classification, it is labelled simply
  **Invoice**.
- A **Payment** records money received. Each payment has its own **Receipt**.
- A **Credit Note** reduces an issued Invoice; it does not rewrite that Invoice.
- A **Refund** returns an available credited amount and produces a refund voucher.

An itemized Invoice and a Payment Receipt answer different questions. Do not hand
one to a patient as though it were the other.

## Correct issued work

Issued financial documents are not edited in place:

1. Use **Issue credit note** for the relevant full or partial Invoice lines.
2. Use **Refund** only against an available Credit Note amount.
3. Supply the required reason and any non-cash transaction reference.

These actions depend on separate permissions. Ask an authorized supervisor when
the controls are unavailable.

## Print or download

Under **Invoices** on the OPD Appointment Billing tab, use **Invoice print** for the
itemized document and expand **Documents** for its Receipts, Credit Notes, and
refund vouchers. Invoices support A4 and 80 mm layouts; the other documents open
as their own PDFs. Preview, print, and download use the same stored source facts,
so a later payment or correction does not change an earlier document.
