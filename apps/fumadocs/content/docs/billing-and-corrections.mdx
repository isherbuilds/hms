## Find the work waiting for you

Choose **Billing** to see open money. This means visits waiting for a bill and
invoices that still have money due. Use the search box or **Filters** to narrow
the list.

![Billing worklist with visits to bill, invoices, and amount due.](/docs/images/guides/billing-worklist.webp)

Choose a visit reference to see a small summary. For a visit that is not yet
billed, choose **Open Visit Billing** to see all the details before you make an
invoice.

![Summary for a visit waiting for a bill, with Issue Invoice and Open Visit Billing buttons.](/docs/images/guides/billing-unbilled-sheet.webp)

Choose an invoice reference to see how much has been paid and what is still
owed. Enter a payment only after you have the money or proof of payment in front
of you.

![Payment form for an existing invoice. The example is not submitted.](/docs/images/guides/billing-collect-sheet.webp)

## Check a visit before making its invoice

Open the visit's **Billing** tab. Check the patient's name, each Charge, and the
total. A **Charge** records a priced service or item. Only pending Charges need
an invoice; invoiced and voided Charges remain in the history.

![Visit Billing tab with a Charge waiting to be billed.](/docs/images/guides/opd-appointment-billing.webp)

Choose **Review And Collect** to open the final check. Read the amount again. If
there is a discount, enter it and add the reason in **Note**. Add payment lines
only for money received now. You can split one collection into up to four
payment lines.

![Review and collect form with the total, payment method, amount, and note.](/docs/images/guides/opd-review-and-collect.webp)

The form starts with a cash amount for the balance after the proposed advance
credit. Check both fields. If you received no money, clear the payment amount.
Set **Use advance credit** to zero if no advance should pay this bill.

Choose **Issue Invoice** once. It creates the invoice and records the payment
lines and advance credit shown in the form. With no payment or credit, the full
amount stays due. With a partial payment, the rest stays due.

Cash does not need a transaction reference. Card, UPI, and Bank transfer need
the reference from the payment machine, app, or bank record. If the amount,
patient, or service looks wrong, close the form and ask the billing supervisor.

> **Stop before the final button:** After you issue an invoice or record a
> payment, do not press the button again. If the result is unclear, check the
> record first or ask the supervisor.

## Take payment for an invoice

On the Billing worklist, choose an invoice reference. Check the invoice number
and amount owed. Choose the method used, enter the amount received, and add the
payment reference when required. Choose **Collect** once.

HMS creates a Payment record and a Receipt. The receipt proves that money was
received. The Invoice lists what was sold or done. They are different papers.

## Know the papers

- **Charge** — a priced service or item; its status shows whether it is pending,
  invoiced, or voided.
- **Invoice** — the itemized bill. An issued invoice is not edited in place.
- **Payment** — a record of money received.
- **Receipt** — proof that the payment was recorded.
- **Credit Note** — a paper that lowers an issued invoice after a correction.
- **Refund** — money returned against an available Credit Note amount.

## Remove a wrong item before billing

Open the visit's **Billing** tab and find the pending item. Choose **Void**,
enter the reason, and confirm. Check that the item is no longer in the amount to
bill. Voiding keeps a record of the change; it does not delete the history. An
item already on an invoice needs a Credit Note instead.

## Correct an issued invoice

If an issued invoice is wrong, do not try to change the old paper. Ask an
authorized supervisor to check the lines. If the invoice needs a correction, the
authorized person can:

1. Open the visit's **Billing** tab.
2. Open the invoice's **⋯** menu and choose **Issue Credit Note**.
3. Select the lines to credit, enter the reason, and check the amount.
4. Choose **Issue credit note** to save the correction. Wait for the dialog to
   close and the invoice to update.
5. On the updated invoice, choose **Record refund** when a refund is due and
   money will be returned. This is a separate action from issuing the Credit Note.
6. Enter the refund method and its transaction reference when needed. Choose
   **Record refund** to save it.

The original invoice stays on the record. The new Credit Note shows the
correction. A Refund voucher shows money given back. Do not issue a Credit Note
or refund unless you are sure of the reason and amount. For pharmacy goods, use
[Return medicine](/docs/pharmacy#return-medicine-from-a-sale) so the bill and stock
change together.

## Money held for later

Choose **Take Advance** on a patient's Billing tab or a visit's Billing tab only
when money should be held for future care. Pick the treatment plan, or choose
**Patient credit · future services** when the money is not for a named plan.
Enter the amount and the method used to receive it. Add the reference for a
non-cash payment. Choose **Record Advance**, then **Print Receipt**. Check that
the receipt appears under the patient's **Advance receipts**.

![Take Advance form. This example is open but not saved.](/docs/images/guides/patient-advance.webp)

An advance is not the same as paying today's invoice. Check with the supervisor
before using an advance to pay for care or returning it.

Choose **Refunds due** to see money that may be returned after a Credit Note.
Choose **Advances held** to see money being held for later care. Open a row and
check the patient and amount before taking any action.

![Refunds due list showing an invoice and the amount that may be returned.](/docs/images/guides/billing-refunds.webp)

![Advances held list showing money saved for future care.](/docs/images/guides/billing-advances.webp)

### Use an advance to pay a bill

On the collection form, check **Use advance credit** before saving. HMS starts
with credit for the bill's treatment plan plus credit not tied to any plan.
Credit held for another plan is not used by default. Change the amount only when
the hospital has approved its use for this bill.

For example, a ₹1,000 bill paid with ₹300 of advance credit needs ₹700 more.
Enter only the money received now in the payment lines. Recheck the payment
amount after changing credit or a discount; HMS keeps the amount you typed.

### Return unused advance money

1. Open the patient's **Billing** tab and expand **Advance receipts**.
2. Find the original receipt and check its unused amount.
3. Choose **Refund**. Enter the amount actually being returned, the method,
   and a reference for a non-cash refund.
4. Choose **Record Refund** once, then **Print Voucher**.
5. Check the receipt's remaining amount.

Only unused money from that receipt can be returned here. A refund against an
invoice follows the Credit Note steps above.

## Print or download a paper

On the visit's **Billing** tab, use **Invoice print** for the itemized Invoice.
Use the document links below each invoice to find Receipts, Credit Notes, and
Refund vouchers. Choose the document you need, then use its print or download
option. The printout uses the facts saved on that document. A later correction
does not rewrite an older paper.
