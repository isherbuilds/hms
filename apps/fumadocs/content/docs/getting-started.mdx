## Sign in

1. Open the HMS link given to you by the hospital.
2. Type your account email address and password. If this is your first visit,
   open your administrator's invitation link to create your account first.
3. Choose **Sign in**.

![Sign-in screen with an email box, password box, and Sign In button.](/docs/images/guides/sign-in.webp)

There is no public sign-up button. If you do not have an account, cannot sign
in, or forgot your password, ask your hospital administrator for help.

## Join your hospital

The administrator creates an invitation for your email address and shares its
link with you. HMS does not send an email automatically.

1. Open the invitation link. Check the hospital name and invited email.
2. If you have no account, enter your name and a password, then choose
   **Create account**. If you already have an account, sign in with the invited
   email.
3. Choose **Join**. If you are signed in with a different email, switch accounts
   first.

On the **Join** page, use **Review invitation** to open a pending invitation.
Keep the link private: it lets the recipient create an account for that email.

If the invitation has expired, ask the administrator to send a new one. Do not
make a second account to get around an expired invitation.

## Check the hospital name

Look at the hospital name at the top of the left menu before you open a patient
or take money. If you work in more than one hospital, choose that name and then
choose the correct hospital from the list. Each browser tab remembers its own
hospital.

## Read the Dashboard

Choose **Dashboard** in the left menu. The cards are a quick count for the
current hospital-local day:

- **Booked** means a visit is planned.
- **Checked In** means the patient has arrived.
- **Unbilled** means a visit has waited longer than the hospital's alert time
  for a bill. A newer visit may still need billing even when this count is zero.
- **Collected** means money has been recorded as received.

The lists below the cards help you open the next item. The Dashboard does not
replace the patient record or the receipt.

![Dashboard example with today's work and collection totals.](/docs/images/guides/dashboard.webp)

## Find the main areas

On a desktop, use the left menu. On a smaller screen, open it with the menu
button beside the page title:

- **Patients** — find a person, register a patient, or open their record.
- **OPD** — see the day's outpatient visits and open a visit.
- **Pharmacy** — sell medicine and look after medicine stock.
- **Billing** — find visits to bill and bills with money still due.
- **Reports** — check totals and prepare a handover report.
- **Files** — find hospital files you are allowed to open.
- **Settings** — manage hospital details, staff, services, people, and access.

![Patients list with search and Register Patient.](/docs/images/guides/patients-list.webp)

## Finish work on a shared computer

Save or discard the form you are working on. Open your name or email in the menu
and choose **Sign out**. Check that the sign-in screen appears before the next
person uses the computer. Closing a tab alone does not sign you out.

## If something goes wrong

- **A field has a message:** correct that field, then try saving again.
- **The record changed:** refresh it and read the new details before editing.
  Another staff member may have saved a change.
- **A payment or sale seems stuck:** check the patient bill or Sales list before
  you retry. If you cannot tell whether it saved, ask the supervisor.
- **A list is empty:** check the hospital, date, search text, and filters.
  Empty results do not always mean the record is missing.
- **The connection is down:** follow the hospital's paper procedure. Keep each
  paper reference for later entry. Check for a saved record before entering it
  in HMS when the connection returns.

## Why a button may be missing

HMS shows actions that your role allows. For example, seeing a bill does not
always mean you can change it or return money. If you need an action that is not
there, ask an administrator. Do not switch to a different hospital to try it.

Continue to the [OPD desk](/docs/opd-desk), [Billing and
corrections](/docs/billing-and-corrections), or [Pharmacy](/docs/pharmacy).

To practise the whole path with made-up details, use the [worked patient
example](/docs/opd-desk#worked-example-one-patient-from-start-to-medicine). It starts
with an empty patient search and ends with the visit bill and any medicine sale.
