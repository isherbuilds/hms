## Accounts and invitations

HMS has no public sign-up. An operator creates your account, and an organization
administrator invites that account by email address.

1. Sign in with the account details supplied by the operator.
2. Open the invitation link, or choose the pending invitation on the **Join** page.
3. Choose **Join**. HMS opens that organization's setup page; use its dashboard
   link when setup is complete.

If an invitation has expired or was revoked, ask an administrator to send a new
one. Creating another account does not restore the invitation.

## Organization URLs

Every organization has its own URL. The organization switcher uses normal links,
so each browser tab can stay in a different organization. Before registering a
patient, issuing an invoice, uploading a file, or changing settings, check the
organization name in the sidebar.

## Access and roles

Your role controls which destinations and actions appear. Being able to view a
record does not necessarily allow you to change it, collect money, issue a
credit note, manage members, or delete a file. Ask an organization administrator
when an expected action is unavailable; do not work around it in another
organization.

## Main destinations

- **Dashboard** shows the parts of today's queue and collections your role may read.
- **Patients** finds, registers, and opens patient records.
- **OPD** runs the outpatient day list and appointment records.
- **Billing** finds work still to bill or collect.
- **Reports**, **Files**, and **Settings** support handover and administration.

Continue with the [OPD desk](/opd-desk) or
[Administration and handover](/administration-and-handover) guide for the task
you are performing.
