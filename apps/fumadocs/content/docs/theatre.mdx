---
title: Theatre schedule
description: Book surgical cases into theatre rooms with their equipment and consultant, and keep rooms and machines out of use for maintenance
---

**Theatre** is the coordinator's calendar for operating theatres, procedure
rooms and named machines. It plans who uses which room, machine and consultant,
and when. It does not record what was done in theatre, and it bills nothing:
procedures and supplies are billed on the Patient's admission as before.

Reception can view and book cases. Only an owner or administrator can add rooms
and equipment or block them.

## Set up rooms and equipment

An administrator opens **Theatre**, then **Rooms and equipment**:

1. **Add room**: give the name staff use, such as OT 1, and say whether it is an
   operating theatre or a procedure room.
2. **Add equipment**: one entry per machine, named so staff can tell it apart,
   such as C-arm 1 or Anaesthesia workstation 2. Two C-arms are two entries.
   Consumables are stock, not equipment.

A room or machine with a confirmed case still ahead cannot be taken out of use.
Move or cancel those cases first.

## Book a case

Open **Theatre**, then **Schedule**, and choose **Book a case**. Find the
Patient, choose the consultant and the procedure, the planned start and end,
the minutes needed to prepare the room before and clean it after, the room and
the machines the case needs.

A booked case is a draft. **A draft holds nothing**: another case can still
take the same room, machine or consultant. The room can be left empty until you
know it.

## Confirm

**Confirm** holds the room and each machine for the case, from the start of
preparation to the end of cleaning, and the consultant for the planned time.
Either everything is held or nothing is.

If HMS refuses, the message says what is in the way:

- **booked at that time**: another confirmed case uses that room or machine,
  preparation and cleaning included;
- **the consultant has another case at that time**;
- **blocked for maintenance** or **not in use**: the room or machine cannot be
  booked then;
- **choose a theatre room** or **that start time has already passed**.

Change the time, room or machine and confirm again. A case can start exactly
when the previous one finishes cleaning. Clinics and other staff are not
checked: confirm the anaesthetist, nurses and the consultant's clinic yourself.

## Change or cancel

- **Change** edits a case. On a confirmed case, the new time, room and machines
  are checked again. If anything clashes, nothing changes and the case keeps
  its old booking.
- **Cancel case** needs a reason and frees everything the case held.

If someone else changed the case on another screen, HMS refuses your change.
Refresh and check before trying again.

## Link the admission

A case can be booked before the Patient is admitted. Once they are in a bed,
open the case and choose **Link admission**. Only the same Patient's current
admission can be linked.

## Block a room or machine for maintenance

Open **Rooms and equipment** and choose **Block** on the room or machine, with
the dates and a reason, such as a service visit. Nobody can confirm a case on it
while the block lasts. **Remove block** puts it back in use.

A block that overlaps a confirmed case is refused. An owner or administrator can
block anyway by giving a reason. Those cases then show **Needs rescheduling**:
they hold nothing any more. **Change** them to another room, machine or time and
**Confirm** again, or **Cancel case**.
