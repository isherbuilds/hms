## Sell medicine

Choose **Pharmacy** in the left menu. The **Sales** tab shows sales already
recorded for the selected date. Choose a sale number to see the buyer, items,
payment, and print option.

![Pharmacy sales list with sale number, date, buyer, and total.](/docs/images/guides/pharmacy-sales.webp)

Choose **New Sale** to start a sale. First choose **Patient** or **Walk-In**:

- Choose **Patient** when the sale belongs on a patient's account. Search for
  the right patient and select them.
- Choose **Walk-In** for a person who is not using a patient record. Type the
  buyer's name. A phone number is optional.

![New Sale form with buyer, item search, optional prescription details, and total.](/docs/images/guides/pharmacy-new-sale.webp)

Next, choose an item from the stock search. Check the product name, batch,
expiry, price, and quantity before you add it. The batch matters: two boxes of
the same medicine may have different expiry dates or prices.

For example, a demo shelf has **Paracetamol 500 mg**, batch **PCM-EXP**, at
**₹80 per 10 tablets**. If that is the right item, choose that batch and check
the quantity. HMS shows the amount and tax in the Payment box.

The printed MRP includes GST. For a registered hospital, HMS extracts taxable
value and CGST/SGST from each discounted line; it does not add GST above MRP.
The printed bill also groups taxable value and CGST/SGST by rate. Without GSTIN,
the bill makes no GST claim.

![Example sale with a walk-in buyer and one Paracetamol batch in the basket. It is not saved.](/docs/images/guides/pharmacy-sale-item.webp)

Open **Add Details** if you need to record the person the medicine is for, the
prescriber, or the prescription reference. Some medicines require prescriber
details. HMS will show those fields when needed.

Read the buyer, medicine, batch, quantity, and **Payable** total once more.
Choose **Collect** once. On the next screen, check the amount and payment
method. Choose **Split payment** if the buyer used more than one method. Then
choose **Record sale** once. A saved sale appears in **Sales** and opens its
sale record.

![Sale payment review with the amount, method, and final Record Sale button. Nothing is saved.](/docs/images/guides/pharmacy-sale-checkout.webp)

![Split payment example with two payment lines. Nothing is saved.](/docs/images/guides/pharmacy-split-payment.webp)

**Collect** opens the review; **Record Sale** saves the sale, invoice, stock
movement, and any payment together. A Walk-In buyer must pay in full. A sale
linked to a Patient can use advance credit or leave an unpaid balance.

If the final result is unclear, open **Sales** and check for the saved sale
before trying again. A repeated submission can record a second sale.

## Receive a medicine delivery

Choose **Stock**, then **Receive Goods**. Choose **Supplier** for a new delivery
from a supplier. Use **Opening** only when a supervisor is entering the
hospital's first counted stock.

![Receive Goods form with supplier bill details and a line for each batch.](/docs/images/guides/pharmacy-receive-goods.webp)

For a supplier delivery:

1. Type the supplier name, bill number, and **Bill total**: the grand total
   printed on the bill.
2. Check the date the goods arrived.
3. Choose the product and enter its batch number and expiry date.
4. Check whether the line is priced per pack or per counted unit. Enter the
   billed and free quantities in that unit, then the rate, discount, tax, and
   printed MRP. For a product without expiry, no expiry date is needed.
5. Add another batch if the bill has another batch or product.
6. If the bill shows freight, packing or a discount outside the medicine lines,
   choose **Add freight or discount**. See
   [Add freight or a discount](#add-freight-or-a-discount).
7. Check that the totals show **Matches bill** before you choose
   **Receive Goods**. The lines, plus charges, minus discounts, must match the
   bill total within ₹0.99.

Use one line for each product and batch. Do not guess the batch, expiry, tax, or
price. An Administrator or Owner can choose **New Product** and enter the
approved product details. A Pharmacist without that access must ask them to add
the product before receiving it.

![New Product form with unit, pack, expiry, and prescription choices.](/docs/images/guides/pharmacy-new-product.webp)

### Add freight or a discount

Choose **Add freight or discount** once for each extra line on the bill. Then
fill in the row:

- **Kind:** **Charge (freight, packing)** adds to the bill. **Discount** takes
  away from it.
- **Reason:** what the bill calls it, for example freight.
- **Amount** and **GST:** type both as positive numbers. Leave GST at 0 when
  the bill shows none. The kind decides whether the row adds or subtracts.

For example, medicine lines of ₹4,172 plus freight ₹270 and freight GST ₹48.60
come to ₹4,490.60. A bill total of ₹4,491 matches, with ₹0.40 round-off.

Do not enter a discount twice when it is already in the medicine lines. If the
difference reaches ₹1, check the bill and your entries; do not invent a charge,
a discount or a stock product to force a match. These rows do not change the
MRP or the sale price.

### Enter the first stock count

Use **Opening** when first bringing a batch into HMS. It is not a supplier
bill, so it has no bill total and no freight or discount rows.

1. Count each product and batch on the shelf.
2. Open **Receive Goods**, choose **Opening**, and set **Counted on**.
3. Enter each product, batch, expiry where needed, counted quantity, and printed
   MRP. Check whether the quantity is in packs or individual units.
4. Keep the count sheet and enter a short reference in **Note**.
5. Choose **Receive Goods**, then check the quantities in **Stock**.

If the batch already has movements, use a checked **Count correction** instead
of entering the opening stock again.

### Practice: enter three products correctly

Use the actual carton or bottle in the hospital. These examples show where each
label detail goes. They are not instructions to give a medicine to a patient. A
doctor chooses the medicine and directions. The pharmacist checks the signed
prescription and the exact product before a sale.

#### Crocin 650 tablet

- **Name:** `Crocin 650 Tablet`
- **Generic name:** `Paracetamol`
- **Form and strength:** `tablet`, `650 mg`
- **Counted in:** `tablet`
- **Pack:** If the box has 10 tablets, enter `10 tablets` and `10` units per
  pack. Use the count printed on the box.

#### Crocin Suspension

- **Name:** `Crocin Suspension 120 mg/5 mL`
- **Generic name:** `Paracetamol`
- **Form and strength:** `oral suspension`, `120 mg/5 mL`
- **Counted in:** `bottle`; one bottle is one unit.
- **Pack:** Enter the bottle volume printed on that bottle.

The manufacturer also lists a different `240 mg/5 mL` suspension. Check the
exact bottle. Do not swap strengths. See the manufacturer's [Crocin 650 tablet
page](https://www.crocin.com/crocin-products-for-adult/crocin-650-tablets/) and
[Crocin suspension
leaflet](https://www.crocin.com/content/dam/cf-consumer-healthcare/panadol/en_in/kids/crocinchildrensrange/pdfs/Crocin-suspension-and-Crocin-Suspension-DS-Product-Info.pdf).

#### Eye drops

- **Name:** `Carboxymethylcellulose 0.5% eye drops` (example only)
- **Generic name:** `Carboxymethylcellulose sodium`
- **Form and strength:** `eye drops`, `0.5%`
- **Counted in:** `bottle`; one bottle is one unit.
- **Pack:** Enter the bottle volume printed on the carton.

Use the actual brand, ingredient, strength, and bottle size in your hospital.
For example, one [official eye-drop
label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=3b94f67c-d675-4e16-846a-3ca39b0405e0)
lists carboxymethylcellulose sodium 0.5%; another product may have different
ingredients or a different strength.

Then fill the other fields from the product and the hospital's approved list:

1. Set **Has an expiry date** to match the product. Most medicines expire;
   use the pack to check.
2. For **Prescription**, follow the product's local legal category and the
   hospital pharmacist's instructions. Do not choose a category by guessing.
3. Leave **Sold at the counter** on for medicine sold to a patient. Turn it
   off only for stock used inside the hospital and never charged to a patient.
4. Enter **GST %** and **HSN** from the approved tax list or supplier record.
   Do not copy them from another product.
5. Choose **Add product**. When stock arrives, record its supplier bill,
   batch, expiry, quantity, and printed price under **Receive Goods**.

HMS does not have a dose, times-per-day, or number-of-days field in the product
catalog. Keep those directions on the prescriber's signed order. Never turn a
training example into a medicine instruction. If a pack differs from this
example, follow the pack and ask the pharmacist before adding it.

### Count packs and loose units

**Counted in** is the smallest unit tracked in stock. **Units per pack** is the
number of those units in one pack. The printed **Pack** text describes the pack;
it does not set the conversion.

For a strip of 10 tablets, set **Counted in** to tablet and **Units per pack**
to 10. Receiving 5 strips adds 50 tablets. Selling 4 tablets leaves 46 tablets,
shown as 4 strips and 6 tablets. Enter 10 tablets to sell a whole strip. For a
bottle sold whole, one bottle is one counted unit.

Check these choices before the first receipt. Once the product has a batch, HMS
locks its counted unit, units per pack, and expiry setting.

## Check stock

Choose **Products** to look up a medicine. An Administrator or Owner can add or
edit products. Open **Stock** to see each product batch. The product form is the
same whether you open it from Products or from **New Product** while receiving a
delivery.

![Products tab with medicine and supply details.](/docs/images/guides/pharmacy-products.webp)

Choose **Stock** to see each product and batch. **Shelf** is the quantity
available for sale. **Quarantine** is medicine held aside and not available to
sell. Check the batch and expiry when you count medicine.

![Stock list with batches, expiry dates, shelf amounts, and quarantine amounts.](/docs/images/guides/pharmacy-stock.webp)

Use the search box to find a product or batch. **Filters** can show medicine
expiring within 30 or 90 days, quarantined stock, or batches with zero stock.
Choose a row's **⋯** menu for **Movements** or **Adjust stock**.

An Administrator or Owner can use **Adjust stock** after checking the real
shelf. Choose the correct reason, enter the quantity, and write a short note.
For example, use **Breakage** if one bottle broke. Use **Count correction** only
to fix a stock count. A count correction can add or remove units, so ask a
supervisor if you are unsure.

![Adjust Stock form showing the reason, quantity, and note fields. The example is not saved.](/docs/images/guides/pharmacy-adjust-stock.webp)

The **Movements** tab shows why stock changed, such as a delivery, sale, return,
or adjustment. Use it to check a change; do not make a second adjustment just
because you are still looking for the first one.

![Stock movement history.](/docs/images/guides/pharmacy-movements.webp)

### Choose the right stock adjustment

Open the batch's **⋯** menu, then **Adjust Stock**. An Administrator or Owner
can record these changes:

| Reason             | Use it when                                   | What changes                                   |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Hold in quarantine | Stock must be kept out of sale                | Moves units from shelf to quarantine           |
| Release to shelf   | A pharmacist has approved held stock for sale | Moves units from quarantine to shelf           |
| Breakage           | Stock broke                                   | Removes units from the selected place          |
| Write off          | Stock must be discarded                       | Removes units from the selected place          |
| Count correction   | A checked physical count differs from HMS     | Adds or removes the difference                 |
| Internal issue     | Stock goes to a hospital department           | Removes shelf units and records the department |

Enter the quantity in counted units and explain the change in **Note**.
**Bucket** means the place to change: shelf or quarantine. For **Internal
Issue**, choose the receiving department. Choose **Adjust Stock** once, then
check **Stock** and **Movements**.

For a count correction, enter the difference, not the new total. If HMS shows 12
bottles and you counted 10, enter **-2**. If you counted 14, enter **2**. Other
adjustment reasons take a positive quantity.

### If an item cannot be sold

Check its active status, **Sold at the counter** setting, batch expiry, and
shelf quantity. Quarantined stock is not available to sell. If another counter
sold the last units, choose an available batch and review the amount again. Do
not create a second product or a false receipt to bypass the problem.

## Return medicine from a sale

Choose **Sales**, open the sale number, and check the buyer and item. This panel
also has **Print**. Choose **Return** only when the medicine is being returned.
Enter the quantity and choose the reason. Returned medicine goes to quarantine
and a Credit Note is recorded against that sale. Enter returns in the counted
unit: returning 3 tablets means 3, not 3 strips. Returned units stay unavailable
for sale until an authorized stock adjustment releases them from quarantine.

![Sale record with its medicine batch, payment, Print, and Return actions.](/docs/images/guides/pharmacy-sale-details.webp)

![Accept a return form showing the quantity, reason, and optional refund fields. Nothing is submitted.](/docs/images/guides/pharmacy-return.webp)

Leave **Refund now** blank if the money will be returned later. If the hospital
is returning money now, enter the approved amount and method. Add the payment
reference when required. Read the patient, batch, quantity, reason, and refund
once more, then choose **Record Return** once.

If you only meant to look at the sale, close the panel without choosing
**Return**. Ask a supervisor if the medicine or amount is not correct.

## Print a sale paper

Open the sale number in **Sales**, check the buyer and total, then choose
**Print**. Use the invoice for the items sold. A payment receipt is a separate
paper that proves money was received.

A registered organization prints **Tax Invoice** with its saved GSTIN, state
and configured Form 20/21 drug licence numbers. Without GSTIN, it prints neutral
**Invoice** without GST claims. Later settings changes do not reclassify an old
sale paper. Ask the administrator to check [Organization
setup](/docs/administration-and-handover#1-check-organization) before the first live sale.
