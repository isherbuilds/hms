# Spec: IPD admission to discharge

Status: staged draft — design accepted in D056; implementation remains market-gated
Authority: Founder request for implementation-ready next-build options, evaluated only on hospital desire and moat; October 2026.
Supersedes: none

## Problem

A 10–50 bed hospital needs to know which Patient occupies each bed, what the stay has earned, how much deposit remains, and what is missing before discharge. Edernal Care currently has OPD Appointments, counter Pharmacy sales, and immutable financial documents, but no Admission. A counter Pharmacy sale immediately creates its own Invoice; using it for inpatient issues would split the running bill and risk charging medicines twice.

## Solution

Give reception a bed board and a separate IPD desk at `/$orgSlug/ipd`. An Admission links the Patient, Practitioner, occupancy history, delivered Charges, and existing Advance Receipts. Staff review bed-day suggestions, issue medicines against batches, see package coverage and excluded items, print a clearly provisional interim bill, and issue the final `ipd` Invoice. Discharge releases the bed independently of collection; unpaid and unbilled departures remain visible exceptions, not hidden clinical states.

## Validation / Evidence

### Hospital desire and moat

**Desire: medium, directional rather than validated.** The supplied target-list findings identify 162 tricity prospects and 42 tier-A hospitals; most targets are IPD hospitals, 39 of 42 tier-A appear on insurer or government cashless lists, and two advertise an existing HIS. These are prospect characteristics, not requests for this workflow or evidence of insured volume. Source context: [pilot market report](../../../edernal/reports/Edernal%20Care%20pilot%20go%20to%20market.md). Cashless participation may make a patient-only IPD product insufficient; this spec does not assume otherwise.

The [buyer research](../../../edernal/research_notes/Edernal%20Care%20pilot%20go%20to%20market/buyers_market_competitors.md) found no independent study of Indian 10–50 bed HMS buyers. [Cliniqwise](https://cliniqwise.com/hms-for-small-and-mid-sized-hospitals-india) describes IPD/pharmacy/billing and leakage as purchase drivers: **vendor claims**. The [discovery research](../../../edernal/research_notes/Edernal%20Care%20pilot%20go%20to%20market/sales_script_motion.md) identifies missed bed days, pharmacy issues and discharge errors as interview hypotheses. [NestorBird's disconnected-workflow complaints](https://nestorbird.com/biggest-gaps-in-indian-hospital-software-and-how-to-fix-them/) are also **vendor claims**; quoted industry leakage percentages are not acceptance baselines.

**Moat contribution: high potential, not demonstrated.** Under the [moat thesis](../../../edernal/reports/Edernal%20Care%20moat.md), linking occupancy, issues, Charges, deposits, corrections and collections extends the trusted revenue system of record. Reconciled history and staff routines could compound operating value; a bed board or module checklist alone is copyable. Claims learning remains a later layer. Exports and source balances remain usable; trapped data is not the mechanism.

**Baseline and target:** before cutover, the billing lead measures 20 consecutive discharges: minutes from completed source review to bill issuance; missed Charge count/value found in independent register reconciliation; and net IPD revenue captured divided by independently reconciled net IPD revenue, counting inpatient medicines once. Proposed 30-day targets are at least 90% revenue coverage, zero unexplained source-to-bill discrepancies, and a 30% reduction in median bill-preparation time. These are founder proposals, not existing results or market benchmarks.

**Validation status:** no customer interviews have happened. The design below is decided, not awaiting founder/accountant consultation. A paying hospital and named operational owner must still supply real facility/tariff data, observe handoffs, measure monthly Admissions/cashless share and the baseline, and record a paid cutover with stable OPD. Existing-HIS hospitals need an operational cutover; no historic Invoice reconstruction or indefinite dual entry.

The [IPD/ADT product gate](../product.md#roadmap-gates) remains a **real market/operational gate**, not an open design question: paying hospital, named owner, stable OPD and a recorded admission-to-discharge/deposit/nursing/billing handoff. D056 accepts the paper-nursing boundary and all four conditional amendments now; it cannot manufacture a customer's adoption or signed operating commitment.

### Reference evidence and its limits

[ERPNext/Frappe Healthcare v13 Inpatient Record](https://frappehealth.com/docs/v13/user/manual/en/healthcare/inpatient_record) owns Admission and ADT; [Healthcare Service Unit Type](https://frappehealth.com/docs/v13/user/manual/en/healthcare/healthcare_service_unit_type) associates billable occupancy with a unit-specific item/rate; [Inpatient ADT](https://frappehealth.com/docs/v13/user/manual/en/healthcare/inpatient_adt) retains transferred units and separates discharge ordering from actual departure. Its documented discharge check requires invoicing, but not payment: we deliberately go further and record actual departure even when unbilled, exposing the exception.

[Bahmni's OpenMRS-backed ADT](https://bahmni.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/BAH/pages/114923665/Admit,+Discharge+and+Transfer+Patients) records distinct admission/transfer/discharge movements and configurable clinical observations. These references justify retaining occupancy segments and a clinical/financial boundary; neither cited manual establishes a universal Indian noon tariff or highest-tariff rule. Calendar checkout and weighted transfer pricing below are **our chosen tariff policy**, not a claimed legal mandate or copied ERPNext formula. [Odoo's third-party hospital module](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/17.0/cerevantix_health_care_management) advertises beds, inpatient-day invoicing and native stock/accounting; this is a vendor feature claim, not proof of Indian tax compliance or demand.

[NHA HBP 2.2 guidelines §§1.3–1.4](https://nha.gov.in/strapi/uploads/User_guidelines_for_HBP_2_2_6d2bc0412a.pdf) support explicit package inclusions (bed/nursing/medicines/diagnostics), permitted add-ons and special-case payment rules; their November 2021 edition explicitly allows state modifications, so it is not a current universal tariff table. [ICAI AS 9 §§7, 9](https://resource.cdn.icai.org/27275asb-as-9.pdf) supports delivery-based revenue rather than deposit revenue and separates later collection uncertainty from price corrections. Paper care records remain clinically authoritative under [NMC medical-ethics regulations §§1.3, 1.8](https://nmc.org.in/storage/cms/Ethics-Regulations-2002.pdf); the revenue desk cannot certify that medicine issued was administered.

## User Stories / Scenarios

1. As reception, I want to admit a Patient and transfer beds without double occupancy, so the bed board matches the ward.
2. As the cashier, I want Admission-linked Advance Receipts and a running bill, so deposits are not confused with revenue or Payments.
3. As the pharmacist, I want an inpatient issue to reduce the selected batch and appear once on the Admission bill, so stock and money reconcile.
4. As the billing lead, I want to approve bed days and package inclusions explicitly, so transfers, short stays and exclusions do not silently inflate bills.
5. As the cashier, I want a provisional interim bill and final Invoice with deposits, Payments and correction documents, so the Patient understands settlement.
6. As the administrator, I want census, running outstanding and unbilled-discharge exceptions, so financial completeness can be checked against actual departures.

All have `orgId NOT NULL`, tenant-leading indexes and composite tenant foreign keys. Admission stores Patient/Practitioner references, admitted/ready/discharged timestamps, timezone snapshot, lifecycle revision, `chargeRevision`, and attribution; nullable `billingEndAt` and `finalChargeRevision` snapshot the selected billing end and reviewed revision atomically at final issuance and remain immutable. Balances remain derived. Every reference is verified under scope. Vocabulary lives in constants/zod, not duplicate database enum checks (D042); retain relational, money and shape constraints. Generate append-only migrations (D022).

Permissions live only in `packages/auth/src/access.ts` (D003): add `ipd:read/create/update/manageFacility/correct`, `pharmacy:issueAdmission/returnAdmission`, and `report:readIpdCensus`. Owner/admin receive all; reception receives IPD read/create/update and census; cashier receives IPD read and existing billing grants; accountant receives IPD read, census and financial reports; pharmacist receives IPD read and the two issue grants. Only owner/admin correct backdating, authorize post-final additions or change facility configuration. Bed-day posting, packages and issuance require `billing:write`; Credit Notes and advance Refunds retain their distinct grants. No nursing/doctor login role without its owned workflow. All procedures use `orgProcedure`, explicit `orgSlug`, tenant predicates and scope-proven references. Billing attribution defaults to the admitting/primary Practitioner; owner/admin may reassign it on an unissued Admission with reason and sensitive audit. Each Invoice snapshots the consultant at issue; Credit Notes inherit the original attribution (D054), never rewrite issued history.

Sensitive facility/tariff changes, backdating, cancellation, reviewed waivers, package changes, issues/returns and financial corrections call fire-and-forget `audit()` after commit (D004). Record actor, identifiers, effective times and reasons, not clinical narrative. Ordinary reads are not audited. Accounting journals remain transactionally mandatory (D012).

### Facility and Admission lifecycle

A Ward has a name; a Bed has a unique Ward-local label, category and active/blocked configuration. A category names accommodation; effective-dated tariffs link an existing Service, daily price and approved tax classification. A stay snapshots its selected tariff; later master edits affect new occupancy segments only. Referenced masters deactivate rather than disappear. The board derives occupancy from open stay intervals and distinguishes available, occupied and blocked beds; staff explicitly unblock a cleaned bed.

States are `admitted` (physically occupying a bed), `discharge_ready` (staff records the authorized discharge handoff; still occupying), `discharged` (actual departure recorded and stay closed), and `cancelled` (erroneous Admission reversed, no delivered work). Admit creates `admitted`; transfer changes occupancy, not state. `admitted → discharge_ready → discharged`; `discharge_ready → admitted` requires a reason. Cancellation is allowed only before any delivered service, approved bed day or pharmacy issue; unused deposits remain liabilities for retention/refund. Once care occurred, use discharge, not cancellation. Terminal states never reopen.

Admission permits one active stay per Patient and one occupant per Bed. Admit locks the Patient before checking active Admissions; transfer locks the Admission and both beds in stable id order. Partial uniqueness protects open occupancy; historical intervals must not overlap. Transfers close the old interval and open the new one at the same effective instant. Cross-tenant, inactive or blocked destinations fail; concurrent losers receive `CONFLICT`. Backdating requires `ipd:correct`, a reason, effective time no later than now, and interval validation under bed locks; reject changes that conflict with historical occupants or issued billing. Keep actual timestamps separate from recording time.

Discharge never requires payment success. It records actual departure, closes occupancy and marks the bed blocked pending readiness. A bill can be finalized while ready or after departure. Compare actual `dischargedAt` against the persisted `billingEndAt`: expose the signed time difference and recompute covered intervals/tariffs under the Admission's snapshotted timezone and charging rule. A timestamp difference within the same charged coverage does not itself add a day. Later departure that adds chargeable coverage requires reviewed supplemental billing or a documented waiver; earlier departure that reduces billed coverage uses linked Credit Notes. Never rewrite `billingEndAt` or silently extend an issued Invoice. Cancellation/discharge and financial outstanding are independent.

### Reviewed bed-day charging

**Accepted rule: calendar-day checkout, default 12:00 noon, minimum one day.** Owner/admin configures local checkout time prospectively; Admission snapshots it and timezone. The first charged window starts at admission and ends at checkout on the **following local calendar date**; later windows run checkout-to-checkout. Departure exactly at checkout starts no additional day; any time after starts another. Admission and departure on the same date always cost one day. Example: admission Monday 11:00 or 15:00, departure Tuesday 12:00 = one day; Tuesday 12:01 = two. This deliberately uses calendar checkout, not rolling 24 hours; the first window may exceed 24 hours. No grace period. A snapshotted payer/package-specific charging rule overrides retail only through the accepted Claims tariff path, never by silently applying this retail formula to PM-JAY.

**Transfers split by actual occupancy, not the highest tariff.** Within each charged window, suggest one line per occupied tariff segment: `segment daily tariff × segment occupied seconds / total occupied seconds in that window`. Fractions sum to one bed-day per window; even a short single-bed window has the one-day minimum. Sum exact rational amounts, round to paise once per window and allocate remainder deterministically by largest remainder then segment start/id (D044); persist segment endpoints, numerator/denominator, daily tariff and final paise. Charge quantity is one per segment with the apportioned price; medicine quantities remain whole stock units. At ₹1,000/day for six hours and ₹2,000/day for six hours, the window costs ₹1,500, not ₹2,000 or two full days. Zero-duration transfers contribute nothing. This is a chosen transparent policy inferred from the references' occupancy traceability, not a claim that ERPNext or Bahmni uses this exact formula. Billing lead may waive/reduce with a reason, not enter an unsigned replacement tariff.

`ipd.previewBedDays` derives windows and their occupancy segments through explicit `throughAt`, capped at actual departure when discharged. Preview has no writes. `ipd.reviewBedDays` rechecks lifecycle/Charge revisions and occupancy, then records approved/waived windows and posts their segment Charges atomically. Store window/segment identity, tariff, exact share, reviewed amount, reviewer, time and reason; unique Admission/window/segment identity prevents duplicate active postings. Open windows are provisional: a later transfer or changed end can change shares without adding a day. Any change to covered occupancy or apportioned amounts invalidates affected reviews and advances `chargeRevision`; pending Charges are voided and replacement reviews required, never silently repriced. Invoiced coverage requires linked Credit Notes or approved supplemental Charges even if the day count is unchanged. No cron, read-triggered posting or automatic charging.

### Charges, inpatient pharmacy and packages

All IPD Charges have `admissionId`, `sourceType: admission`, `sourceId = admissionId` and an explicit kind `service | bed_day | pharmacy_issue | package`. Typed foreign keys link review, issue line or package where applicable. Services/procedures link verified `catalog_items`; bed/package identities use their typed records; medicines have null catalog reference and a real `stockBatchId`. Extend source/parent checks accordingly, preserving counter-sale `pharmacy_batch` behaviour. Every Charge snapshots quantity, description, tax, price units and revenue category; pending changes use the shared void path and increment Admission `chargeRevision` (D020).

`pharmacy.issueToAdmission` is distinct from `pharmacy.sell`: it atomically inserts typed issue/lines, negative shelf movements and pending Admission Charges, **without** a Pharmacy sale, Invoice, Payment or revenue journal. Verify active sellable Product, unexpired batch, Schedule X refusal, H1 prescriber facts, whole smallest-unit quantity and shelf sufficiency. Lock Admission, Products and batches; aggregate requested quantity per batch first. Keep batch/MRP/expiry and issue-line links on the bill. Included package medicines still move stock and retain zero-priced covered Charges. Internal supplies are not patient sales; ward-only departmental issues cannot stand in for billable Admission issues.

`pharmacy.returnFromAdmission` names exact issue lines/quantities, checks net issued quantity under the Admission lock, and moves goods into quarantine (D041). Before invoicing, void/split and repost remaining pending quantities. After invoicing, create a linked Credit Note for priced returned supply atomically; zero-price package returns create no financial credit. Refund remains separately authorized. Returns release package inclusion quantity before package delivery confirmation. Return authority permits stock-linked corrections, not arbitrary discounts. Never restock by deleting Charges or refunding money alone.

A fixed package snapshots an existing package Service, price/tax, inclusion limits by Service/Product or bed category/day, and explicit exclusions; anything not included is excluded. Attach before the first delivered Charge. Matching consumes inclusion quantities under the Admission lock in delivery order, splitting covered zero-price and excluded priced quantities; preserve all delivered source records and stock movements. At discharge-ready, staff confirms actual package delivery and posts its one fixed-price Charge. Deposits are liabilities until allocated, never package revenue (AS 9 evidence above). Cancellation before delivery creates none. Shorter stays do not automatically reduce a fixed retail fee; approved pre-Invoice changes require reason/review and post-Invoice reductions use Credit Notes. PM-JAY/contract special-case rules (LAMA, death, referral, daily medical packages and authorized add-ons) use the snapshotted Claims tariff rather than this retail fixed-fee default. No overlapping packages, guessed eligibility or automatic selection. Map inclusion Services/Products explicitly; unmatched contract items fail configuration and cannot activate the package. A PM-JAY included item may not become a patient extra merely because a local inclusion limit was exhausted.

### Invoice, deposit and settlement invariants

**Decision amendments accepted (D056), effective only when IPD ships:** **D025 accepted** — add stream `ipd`, exact Admission parent, counter `invoice:ipd:${fy}` and `ipdInvoicePrefix`; replace stream-derived tax basis with snapshotted per-line `priceBasis: exclusive | inclusive`, backfill existing OPD/pharmacy bases and migrate every math/preview/credit/print caller in one cutover. **D027 accepted** — services keep catalog identity; typed Admission sources carry bed/package/issue identities, medicine Charges have null catalog and real batch. **D042 accepted** — patient-specific IPD issue replaces the department-only interim path; unrelated departmental consumption remains. **D049 accepted** — add typed Admission source/parent/batch pairing without changing whole smallest-unit quantities, printed price units or D044 apportionment. D013/D019 remain unchanged. Header grand total sums line gross; print taxable base/GST/exempt amounts and exclusive base versus inclusive prices unambiguously. IPD `roundOff = 0`; counter Pharmacy rounding stays unchanged. `priceBasis` is arithmetic, not GST classification: an included inpatient medicine may retain an MRP-derived inclusive price while belonging to exempt composite healthcare.

Extend `billing.recordAdvance` with optional `admissionId`, mutually exclusive with `treatmentPlanId`; verify Patient and active Admission. An Admission deposit is an existing immutable Advance Receipt and Patient Advances liability (D035), not a Payment or balance column. Offer that Admission's unused Credit first, then untagged Patient Credit; another Admission/plan's Credit is not silently spent. Explicit allocations name receipt ids/amounts and recheck available balances under locks. Excess Credit stays held or uses `billing.recordAdvanceRefund`; never invent a Credit Note for unused advance money.

`ipd.previewFinalInvoice({orgSlug, admissionId, billingEndAt})` returns `billingEndAt`, `chargeRevision`, reviewed lines, `grandTotal`, bed-review completeness and eligible Credit; it writes nothing. `ipd.issueFinalInvoice({orgSlug, admissionId, billingEndAt, expectedChargeRevision, expectedGrandTotal, advanceAllocations, payments, outstandingReason?})` returns `invoiceId`, `receiptIds`, `chargeRevision`. Both validate `billingEndAt` as an explicit timestamp at or after `admittedAt`, no later than server time, and equal to actual `dischargedAt` when already discharged. Issuance requires ready/discharged state, approved or explicitly waived bed coverage through that end and delivered package confirmation. Under the Admission lock, revalidate the end against current lifecycle/occupancy, verify D020 revision/total and reviewed coverage, and snapshot `billingEndAt` with `finalChargeRevision = expectedChargeRevision`; issue via `issueInvoiceTx`, apply selected Advance Allocations, then record up to four Payments/Receipts and balanced journals in the same transaction. Invalid ends, incomplete/stale reviews or settlement failure leave no snapshot or financial writes. No overcollection; a positive remainder requires a reason. A zero-work discharge records a reviewed no-bill reason without an empty Invoice. Only one initial final Invoice is permitted; later corrections are linked Credit Notes or administrator-authorized supplemental Invoices for explicitly identified missed supply.

After initial final issuance, normal posting stops; missed supply requires correction authority. Pre-lock named Advance Receipts before issuance/counters, then recheck allocation balances inside the transaction.

Preserve D029: base IPD addresses the Patient; Sponsor remains measurement only. Patient-only IPD may ship first **only for self-pay Admissions**, not cashless cases disguised as patient debt or a fake insurance Payment. Measure insured Admissions/value over the pilot month; if the paying scope includes cashless workflows, release IPD with Claims instead of promising unsupported collections. The separate Claims spec extends preview with allocation output and issuance with `claimAllocation {claimId, expectedClaimRevision, allocationLines}` to post the Payer share to its distinct receivable. Both retain required top-level `billingEndAt`, the same atomic snapshot/reviewed revision and one issuance path.

An interim bill is a guarded printable estimate from current sources, timestamped and labelled **“Interim bill — not an Invoice or Receipt”**, showing reviewed Charges, unapproved bed suggestions separately, deposits and estimated balance. It consumes no counter or receivable/journal. Final paper uses immutable snapshots through D021. Statutory document fields/classification follow the accepted GST policy (D052), not a future accountant consultation: [CGST Rules 46/49 and 46A](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule46a_v1.00.html) distinguish Tax Invoice, Bill of Supply and an Invoice-cum-Bill of Supply to an unregistered recipient. Third-party payment does not by itself change the recipient of healthcare.

**Supply classification:** qualifying inpatient healthcare and its naturally bundled care/medicines/doctor-advised food are exempt composite healthcare, not separately taxable merely because itemized or paid by an insurer ([CGST Act §§2(30), 8](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/CGST-bill-e.html), [CBIC Circular 32/06/2018 §5](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/circularno-32-cgst.pdf)). Independent goods/optional non-healthcare supplies retain their own classification; a commercial package is not automatically an exempt composite supply. Non-ICU/CCU/ICCU/NICU room accommodation above ₹5,000/day carries 5% GST without ITC under [Notification 03/2022, entry 31A](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-05/03_2022-ctr-eng_1.pdf); splitting an occupancy line cannot disguise the daily tariff threshold. Snapshot actual treatment/bundle and room classification with reasons, rather than copying Product GST mechanically; correct exceptional tax changes through linked documents, not bill edits.

Extend D040's lock order to Patient (Admission creation only) → OPD Appointment → Admission → Beds by id → existing Treatment/Charge/Invoice/Product/batch/Advance Receipt order. Admission issue-return rows are read under their parent lock. Acquire numbering series only after domain locks, invoice then receipt; deposit/refund commands take one series. Preserve D039: no new replay keys or automatic retries; after a lost response staff inspect source history before repeating money/stock commands.

## Out of Scope

Claims/TPA tariffs and receivable allocation implementation, Emergency, lab/radiology order-result workflows, supplier accounts, general accounting, ABDM, payment gateway, bulk historic import, offline writes, backup automation, and cross-hospital analytics. IPD may charge a verified Service for delivered work but does not create an unowned clinical ordering system.

## Explicitly Deferred

Clinical nursing charts and vitals, MAR, OT workflow, and discharge-summary text remain on paper. **Boundary accepted:** registered clinicians/nurses author and sign the care chart, medication-administration record and clinical discharge summary; pharmacy records issue/return, not administration; reception records movement only after that signed handoff; billing lead reconciles delivered services/issues/occupancy to the paper register before final review. The desk stores document references and handoff actor/time, not unsigned clinical substitutes. Urgent discharge/transfer is never held for invoice review or collection; missing review is an actionable unbilled exception. The paying hospital's named owner must evidence this SOP and its daily reconciliation at cutover as part of adoption, not revisit the design. Do not market the feature as full digital nursing or EMR. NMC §§1.3/1.8 and the reference clinical/ADT boundaries above support that division of responsibility.

## Open Questions

Only market/real-use gates remain: a **paying hospital and named operational owner** satisfying the [IPD/ADT product gate](../product.md#roadmap-gates), including real facility setup, stable-OPD evidence, operating handoff/cutover commitment and measured baseline; and **measured insured volume or a signed cashless scope** to select self-pay-first versus IPD+Claims release. No CA, legal or founder design consultation remains. D056 accepts the paper-nursing boundary, calendar checkout/split occupancy, packages, per-line tax basis, patient-only boundary, Admission/public command seams and D025/D027/D042/D049 amendments. A citation or autonomous decision cannot manufacture payment, a named hospital owner or real pilot measurements.
