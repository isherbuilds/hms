# Spec: Owner revenue control

Status: Ready-for-implementation
Authority: Owner instruction dated 2026-10-04 to settle the reporting contract autonomously without CA/owner consultation; D054.
Supersedes: none

## Problem

An owner cannot answer “what was billed, what money arrived, and what needs explanation?” from one hospital-wide view. OPD and pharmacy have distinct Invoice streams, while the five shipped reports answer different questions. An OPD attendance with no Charge is invisible to the aged pending-Charge worklist. Discounts, corrections, Refunds and stock losses require opening individual records. Combining these into one unexplained “revenue” number would hide rather than resolve discrepancies.

Most prospective buyers operate IPD hospitals. A view over only OPD and pharmacy must state that coverage explicitly; it cannot claim to control the whole hospital's revenue before Admissions are recorded.

## Solution

Add Revenue control at `/$orgSlug/reports/revenue-control` and an Invoice Register at `/$orgSlug/reports/invoice-register`. Owners, administrators and accountants can inspect issued value, period corrections, collections, outstanding balances and review signals separately, then open the source record. Include doctor-wise and service-category billed revenue. Reuse Reports' Excel export and browser Print / PDF; do not introduce a messaging service.

Everything is a read model over existing domain records. No alert-resolution workflow, editable totals, analytics warehouse or shadow ledger is introduced. The same register includes `ipd` when the Admission increment exists; no empty IPD module is created now.

### Hospital desire and moat

**Desire evidence: weak, directionally consistent.** The [buyer research](../../../edernal/research_notes/Edernal%20Care%20pilot%20go%20to%20market/buyers_market_competitors.md) finds no independent study of 10–50 bed buying priorities. The [sales research](../../../edernal/research_notes/Edernal%20Care%20pilot%20go%20to%20market/sales_script_motion.md) reports repeated themes of unbilled services, disconnected tools and collection visibility. [Ichelon's 18–32% leakage estimate](https://ichelonconsulting.com/insights/hospital-revenue-leakage-guide) is an unverified consultancy claim and is not a baseline or forecast here. [Financial-report research](../research/hospital-wide-financial-reports.md) supports consolidating separate series, but the accountant's exact required export remains unvalidated.

The supplied target-list facts are 162 tricity prospects, 42 tier-A, and 39 of those 42 on insurer or government cashless lists; most are IPD hospitals and two advertise an existing HIS. These facts show segment context, not owner demand for this screen or actual insured revenue. The [pilot report](../../../edernal/reports/Edernal%20Care%20pilot%20go%20to%20market.md) proposes daily collection use as a pilot success criterion; that is a proposed test, not achieved usage.

**Moat contribution: medium, conditional.** The [moat thesis](../../../edernal/reports/Edernal%20Care%20moat.md) prioritizes a trusted revenue system of record. Source-linked reconciliation can establish the daily operating habit and historical context that makes replacement require more than copying a CSV. A dashboard itself is readily copied. Contribution depends on reconciled coverage and recurring use, not chart count or withheld exports. Claims learning remains a later layer.

**Baseline and target:** no measured hospital baseline exists. During a signed pilot, observe seven consecutive billing days: owner reconciliation minutes, unexplained differences, confirmed missed Charges, modules actually used and independently reconciled billing coverage. Pilot targets are all seven sampled days reconciled to source documents without unexplained paise differences; median owner review time at least 50% below the observed baseline; and at least 90% of net billed hospital revenue recorded by pilot day 30 within the agreed rollout scope. Report partial rollout scope separately from hospital-wide coverage. Count actual confirmed omissions, never estimated “recovered leakage.” A paying hospital and these real usage measurements remain market gates, not facts supplied by this decision.

**Validation status:** no customer interviews have happened, no real reconciliation failure or correction is observed, and no incumbent HIS/Excel comparison, redacted hospital month, actual IPD/cashless share, or digest-use baseline exists. The spec is Ready-for-implementation on the owner's instruction, not on validated customer demand. D054 accepts the date bases, current-balance labels, linked-attendance attribution, external denominator, daily review responsibility, MRP-only expiry panel, interfaces and deferrals below. Evidence is [ERPNext's separate sales, receivable and ledger reports](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/accounting-reports), [Tally's correction-period Credit Notes](https://help.tallysolutions.com/sales-return-using-credit-note/), [Bahmni's linked clinical billing and accounting reports](https://bahmni.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/BAH/pages/32604217/Patient+Billing+and+Accounting), [Marg's expiry reporting](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/expiry-stock/109499/1/how-to-view-expiry-stock-report-in-marg-erp-software) and [ICAI AS 2](https://resource.cdn.icai.org/27269asb-as-2.pdf), not interviews. Deliberate free care remains valid under D017; report signals require review, not automatic loss estimates. Reference-system capability does not prove daily adoption, desire, moat, or whole-hospital coverage.

## User Stories / Scenarios

1. As an owner, I want one row per Invoice across operating desks, so separate number series do not conceal issued documents or unpaid balances.
2. As an accountant, I want issue-period value distinguished from correction-period value and cash flow, so a later Credit Note does not silently rewrite a prior month's report.
3. As an administrator, I want source-linked review signals with actors and reasons where recorded, so authorized concessions are distinguishable from errors without accusing staff of theft.
4. As an owner, I want doctor/category revenue and method mix with explicit attribution, so decisions are based on billed supply rather than advance money or invented doctor earnings.

## Implementation Decisions

### Ownership, access and schema

Extend `reportRouter` in `packages/api/src/routers/report.ts` and `exportRouter` in `export.ts`. Reuse `dashboard.collections`, `report.dailyCollections`, the billing worklist balance semantics and `invoiceBalancesFor` for bounded document sets. Reports do not replace journal-based Trial Balance or Balance Sheet (D012). Extend shared labels only when screen and workbook both need them.

Reuse `report:readFinancial`, currently granted to owner, admin and accountant in `packages/auth/src/access.ts`; reception, cashier and pharmacist alone cannot read either new page, its APIs or exports. No new grant is needed. Each entry uses `orgProcedure`, explicit `orgSlug`, tenant predicates on every aggregate/join and organization-keyed queries (D001–D003). Route loaders use `requireOrgPermission`, membership currency/timezone, the singleton `orpc`, and the existing report loading/error and `ReportActions` patterns. Do not add sensitive-read audit events: denials remain centrally audited; existing mutation audits remain fire-and-forget (D004). No source writes, including lazy OPD no-show reconciliation, occur through these new reports.

No new domain table or summary column. Add only a tenant-leading query index if the implementation query plan proves it necessary, via generated append-only migration (D022); existing sources remain authoritative. No use of `audit_log` as financial evidence: it is intentionally best-effort. Clinical notes, phone numbers and prescription scans are excluded.

### Invoice Register contract

`report.invoiceRegister` accepts `orgSlug`, inclusive `from/to`, optional `stream`, full-number/name/MRN `query`, `limit` (25 default, 100 maximum) and optional cursor `(businessDate, createdAt, id)`. Range is at most 366 days; route defaults to fiscal-year start through today using Organization settings. Descending tuple order is deterministic, not a global invoice sequence. Select `limit + 1` base Invoices before display joins; summary totals cover all filtered matches, not the page. Aggregates must avoid multiplying documents by joining raw lines, Payments and Credit Notes together.

Return `from/to`, `generatedAt`, `balanceBasis: current`, rows, `nextCursor`, totals and per-stream/fiscal-year series summaries. Each row carries Invoice id, Business Date, stream, fiscal year, original full number, snapshotted Patient/counter-sale identity, subtotal, discount, taxable value, tax, round-off, grand total, Credit Note total, net billed, Payments, Advance Allocations, Refunds and signed outstanding. Taxable value is summed from issued lines; subtotal is not comparable across inclusive pharmacy and exclusive OPD without its stream. Preserve the original number; series summaries give count and first/last issued number by issuance order, never infer series from today's configurable prefix.

Before Claims ships, `netBilled = grandTotal − creditTotal`; `outstanding = netBilled − paymentsTotal − allocationsTotal + refundsTotal`. “Paid” means Payments only here; “Allocated Credit” is separate. Existing worklists combine them as paid, so describe this display difference without changing their contract. Negative outstanding means Refund due; do not clamp it to zero. Advance Receipts themselves never reduce an Invoice balance until allocation (D035). The [Claims spec](cashless-claims.md#invoice-liability-and-settlement-documents) owns extending the register fields and shared `invoiceBalancesFor` balance seam for signed claim adjustments, including TDS, write-offs and their reversals; the register then reports patient-plus-payer balances, with both subtotals reconciling to Invoice-wide outstanding.

The issue-date filter selects Invoices; their balance columns include all linked movements visible at generation, even after `to`. Label this “Invoices issued in period; balances now,” not an historical debt statement. Screen and Excel carry the generation instant. Concurrent writes can change a later export; refresh before handover as existing Reports documents. Drill-down returns typed source references plus Invoice id: OPD Appointment, Pharmacy sale, or Admission. Open existing guarded document/source surfaces; report permission does not bypass their guards.

### Date, doctor and category attribution

`report.revenueBreakdown({orgSlug, from, to})`, at most 92 days, returns `byStream`, `byPractitioner`, `byCategory` and `totals`, each separating issued taxable value, credited taxable value, net taxable value, tax and document round-off. Call these **net billed revenue**, not clinical earnings or profit. Invoice lines contribute on Invoice Business Date; Credit Note lines contribute negatively on Credit Note Business Date, including corrections to older Invoices. Join each credit line to its original issued line for category. Discounts are already included in issued taxable values and are never subtracted twice. Refunds alter cash and balances, not revenue. A correction-only period may legitimately have negative revenue. Round-off remains a separate unallocated amount, never spread across doctors/categories (D031, D044, D049).

OPD attribution uses the source Appointment's `practitionerId`, not Invoice `issuedBy`, Charge creator or plan owner. Names are current display labels, explicitly not immutable financial snapshots; grouping is by id. Pharmacy linked to an OPD Appointment is shown under that Practitioner as **linked attendance attribution**, never prescriber earnings; unlinked counter sales stay “Unassigned.” Never fuzzy-match `prescriberName`. Category is the issued line's `revenueCategory`, not today's Service master or the Appointment department (D018, D024, D027). Null/missing attribution stays a visible unassigned bucket. D054 defines **earning department** as the service-owning department, distinct from clinical attendance department, cashier desk and stream. A future department-report increment snapshots the owning department id and label on each issued line, carries it to revenue ledger entries and inherits it on credits; legacy lines without that snapshot remain unassigned. This read-only increment does not add the snapshot or claim a departmental financial report. [ERPNext line-level Cost Centers](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/cost-center) support that dimension; [Bahmni clinical drug-order integration](https://bahmni.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/BAH/pages/32604217/Patient+Billing+and+Accounting) supports explicit linkage, not a prescriber-payout claim.

Keep both reconciliation bases visible: issue-period register netting and event-period revenue differ by credits to older Invoices and later credits against period Invoices. Return those two bridge amounts so the UI explains the difference, rather than forcing the reports to equal artificially.

### Review signals

`report.revenueSignals({orgSlug, from, to, kind, limit, cursor})` returns one kind's paged source rows and whole-filter count/amount summary; limit 25/100 and maximum 92 days. `kind` is one of `no_charge`, `voided_charge`, `discount`, `credit_note`, `below_mrp`, `stock_adjustment`, `refund`. Cursor follows each kind's event date/time and id. Signals overlap and have incompatible value bases: never add them into a “total lost revenue.” Every row includes source reference, basis, reason where recorded and actor availability.

- **Checked-in OPD with no Charge:** Appointment Business Date in range and no non-voided Charge. Distinguish never-charged from all-Charges-voided; show review-needed counts, not guessed missed value. A zero-priced non-voided Charge counts as a Charge. Intentional fee omission needs no recorded reason under D017; this view cannot infer dishonesty or change that decision. Keep aged pending Charges separate and link the existing `unbilledAlertHours` worklist unchanged.
- **Voided Charges:** filter current `voided` rows by `updatedAt` in Organization-local boundaries, labelled recorded last-update date, with `voidReason`, creator and exact snapshot price/quantity. Source records have no `voidedBy` or `voidedAt`: actor of void is unavailable, creator is not the voider. Do not manufacture historical correction events or add capture fields in this read-only increment.
- **Discounts and Credit Notes:** use document Business Date, Invoice `issuedBy`/`discountAmount`/`note`, and Credit Note `issuedBy`/`reason`/`total`. Group by stable user id and exact stored reason; absent legacy reasons remain “Not recorded.” Reasons are free text, not a retroactively inferred taxonomy. Discounts reduce bill value; Credit Notes may also include returns and mistakes and must not all be called discounts.
- **Sales below MRP:** current sale Charges snapshot immutable batch MRP; the shipped path has no custom-price entry. Report pharmacy issued lines with allocated discounts, and any exact unit-price ratio below batch MRP; show undiscounted lineSubtotal, discount and line gross excluding document round-off. Compare rational prices by cross multiplication, not rounded unit costs. A paise allocation or full/partial return must not become a new below-MRP sale. Credit Notes remain separate corrections. Link source Invoice/Pharmacy sale and issuer/reason; this is authorized price concession review, not evidence of shrinkage.
- **Stock adjustments/write-offs:** read append-only stock movements by `createdAt` converted using the current Organization timezone; no stored Business Date exists. Show `count_correction`, `breakage`, `writeoff`, quantity, bucket, actor and reason/note. Separate internal issues and quarantine/release transfers; transfers between buckets are not hospital stock losses (D041–D042).
- **Refunds:** show Invoice-linked and Advance Receipt-linked Refunds on stored Business Date with `refundedBy`, method and source. A Refund stores no reason: show linked Credit Note reason when present, otherwise “Not recorded.” Preserve the distinction; Refunds do not reduce revenue again.

`report.expiryExposure({orgSlug, horizonDays})` accepts only 30 or 90, defaults 30, and returns current shelf/quarantine quantities, expired batches, upcoming-expiry batches, totals and `generatedAt`. Sum movements, include inactive/internal Products where stock exists, exclude null-expiry batches from expiry exposure but disclose their count. Printed expiry date is valid through that date. Exposure uses immutable batch MRP × counted quantity / `mrpUnits`, rounded half-up once per reported batch/bucket; show shelf and quarantine separately. Label **MRP exposure, not purchase cost or inventory valuation**. Write-off amounts use the same stated exposure basis, not a profit-loss posting. D054 accepts this operational exposure panel without a cost-valuation prerequisite: [Marg's expiry stock screen distinguishes MRP and purchase rate](https://care.margcompusoft.com/userfiles/CareImg070620190101479.png), and [ERPNext Gross Profit requires buying/valuation facts](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/sales-analytics). Accounting valuation remains a separate source-model increment under [ICAI AS 2 paragraphs 5–7, 16–17 and 20–25](https://resource.cdn.icai.org/27269asb-as-2.pdf): lower of cost and net realisable value, weighted-average cost for interchangeable goods, discounts deducted, recoverable GST excluded, non-recoverable taxes and attributable acquisition costs included, and total receipt cost spread across billed plus free units. Opening cost must be supported by incumbent books; absent cost is unknown, never zero. Expired stock has no ordinary sale NRV; a documented supplier recovery, if any, is assessed separately. These rules do not authorize inventory journals or claim that MRP is cost.

## Accepted dispositions

- **Period and daily responsibility (D054):** net a Credit Note in its own Business Date/posting period, not the original Invoice month; keep the issue-period register labelled “Invoices issued in period; balances now” with generation instant and a correction bridge. [Tally's later-month return example](https://help.tallysolutions.com/sales-return-using-credit-note/) and [ERPNext's separately posted Credit Note](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/credit-note) preserve the original sale; [Accounts Receivable](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/accounts-receivable-and-payable) distinguishes outstanding balances from sales. Linked-attendance pharmacy attribution and the external owner-entered denominator use the contracts above. Revenue control is a daily close task for the billing lead holding the existing `accountant` role, or `admin` where the lead also runs the desk; the owner reviews unexplained source-linked differences. There is no new billing-lead role or grant. [Existing role grants](../../packages/auth/src/access.ts) permit this split; [Bahmni accounting reports](https://bahmni.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/BAH/pages/32604217/Patient+Billing+and+Accounting) establish capability, while daily frequency is the accepted product policy, not observed customer practice.
- [Plan-sitting credits (D051)](../decisions.md#d051--credited-plan-work-returns-to-the-unbilled-balance): a Credit Note line's pre-GST taxable value reduces the item's posted amount and restores that value to unbilled work; a completed plan reopens when a non-dropped item becomes unfinished. The original Charge stays invoiced and the Invoice immutable. Report financial netting is not a mutation of those documents.
- [OPD earning milestone (D053)](../decisions.md#d053--multi-sitting-services-earn-by-proportionate-completion): D034/D047 post each delivered sitting's proportionate share, with the balance billed on early completion; no partial-work item or completion-only posting. [ICAI AS 9 §7.1(i), §12](https://resource.cdn.icai.org/69237asb55316-as9.pdf) supplies the service-recognition basis. Advance Receipt/GST particulars belong to D052, not a remaining CA question.
- **Entity and statutory classification:** follow [D052](../decisions.md#d052--gst-registration-determines-document-particulars-and-bounded-numbering): configure the real issuer identity/GST registration, never infer legal form from an organization name. With GSTIN, exempt OPD healthcare uses Bill of Supply and taxable pharmacy uses Tax Invoice; without GSTIN, documents remain neutral and never claim or collect GST. Preserve exempt/taxable split and tax-inclusive pharmacy presentation. Financial numbering is at most 16 characters in `[A-Za-z0-9/-]`, unique within the financial year and consecutive within each series; reporting preserves issued numbers rather than renumbering history. [CBIC invoice-series FAQ](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/sectoral-faq.html) supports separate series; D052 owns classification, issuance fields and numbering enforcement. Actual hospital legal identity and GSTIN are deployment facts, not fabricated by this decision.
- **Month/year handover:** export four reconciled measures on matched entity, date and scope: (1) issued line values less Credit Note line values on each document's own date, excluding GST, with discounts already netted and round-off separate; (2) cash/bank Payments plus Advance Receipts less both Refund kinds, excluding Allocations from fresh receipts; (3) GST outward exempt value, taxable value and tax by document/HSN/rate with Credit Notes separately traceable; (4) source-linked billing-journal Trial Balance opening, period debits/credits and closing balances. Bridge issue-period current netting to event-period sales rather than forcing equality. Reconcile to the corresponding revenue, cash/bank, advance-liability, receivable and tax accounts; the HMS Trial Balance remains partial books, not final accounts or an ITR. [ERPNext accounting reports](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/accounting-reports) distinguish these bases; [Tally Sales Register](https://help.tallysolutions.com/how-to-work-with-sales-register-in-tallyprime/) explicitly distinguishes tax-inclusive transaction value from tax-exclusive revenue.
- **Tally handover:** enter one Sales voucher per HMS Invoice and one Credit Note voucher per HMS Credit Note, using the full issued HMS number as voucher number and bill-wise source reference; retain the original Invoice number/date on credits. Use dedicated HMS voucher types with **Manual** numbering and duplicate-number prevention, not period-summary sales vouchers. If an existing automatic type is used for migration, enable **Retain Original Voucher No.** and prove that import preserves the supplied number; do not accept automatic renumbering. This follows [Tally numbering methods](https://help.tallysolutions.com/use-voucher-numbering-methods/) and its [voucher-wise Sales Register](https://help.tallysolutions.com/how-to-work-with-sales-register-in-tallyprime/): document-level correction, GST trace and receivable reconciliation remain possible. A Tally adapter/import format is outside this report increment; XLSX handover preserves source numbers and evidence.
- **Department and expiry:** earning department means the service owner, snapshotted at issue in the future dimension increment, never retrospectively inferred from a clinical appointment or today's master; [ERPNext invoice-row Cost Centers](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/cost-center) justify the separation. MRP exposure is sufficient for the present expiry panel; [Marg's expiry screen](https://care.margcompusoft.com/userfiles/CareImg070620190101479.png) distinguishes MRP from purchase rate, and [ICAI AS 2](https://resource.cdn.icai.org/27269asb-as-2.pdf) requires a separate cost/NRV basis for books as specified above. The panel is not cost of expiry, profit, or statutory inventory valuation.
- **IPD attribution:** default to the admitting/primary consultant; an authorized Admission reassignment records previous/new Practitioner, actor and reason in a sensitive audit.
