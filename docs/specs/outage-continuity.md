# Spec: Outage continuity

Status: accepted specification; implementation remains (D058)
Authority: Founder request to compare hospital desire and moat; [Product offline gate](../product.md#roadmap-gates)
Supersedes: none

## Problem

A receptionist, cashier or pharmacist cannot complete an online transaction when the hospital internet, API, database or web server is unavailable. The hospital still receives patients, provides services and handles money. A failed response also does not prove that a transaction failed: the server may already have committed it. Re-entering everything after reconnection can duplicate money or stock movements.

Edernal Care is online-only. [Operations](../operations.md#downtime-re-entry) already describes entering pharmacy paper sales individually, but a note containing a paper number does not establish completeness, original event time or duplicate protection. There is no observed outage baseline in the target segment. Building full offline writes now would bypass the gate requiring evidence that network/UPS remediation and controlled paper fallback are insufficient.

## Solution

Prepare a controlled downtime kit while online, measure unavailability during staffed periods, and recover paper activity through explicit online backfill and reconciliation. Staff keep paper, not an invisible browser write queue, as the downtime source. Offline information is visibly stale reference material. Digital financial documents are issued only after the server returns; their original paper activity remains traceable.

The first release contains measurement and a paper-recovery path, not general offline mode. The shift lead checks spare connectivity, router/terminal/printer power and prepared kit availability. D058 accepts the four-hour ceiling, document/date rules, custody and narrow decision amendments below. Residual outage measurements still gate any later PWA or synchronized-write commission; this decision does not invent a customer baseline or authorize an offline write queue.

## Validation / Evidence

### Hospital desire and moat

**Desire strength: weak for 10–50 bed purchase prioritization.** [Cliniqwise](https://cliniqwise.com/hms-for-small-and-mid-sized-hospitals-india) names “ignoring offline behaviour in tier-2/3 cities” as a buying mistake. This is a competing vendor claim, not buyer research. The [buyer packet](../../../edernal/research_notes/Edernal%20Care%20pilot%20go%20to%20market/buyers_market_competitors.md) labels it accordingly. The [Ujjain study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12087044/) provides independent evidence of an infrastructure concern: 42 of 100 respondents answering the internet question classified unreliable internet as a major barrier. The sample was 105 postgraduate doctors at an **800-bed** rural private teaching hospital; data were collected May–July 2023 and published in 2025. It does not measure outage frequency, small-hospital desk behavior or willingness to pay for offline writes.

The [discovery packet](../../../edernal/research_notes/Edernal%20Care%20pilot%20go%20to%20market/sales_script_motion.md) reports no credible local outage-frequency estimate. The [pilot report](../../../edernal/reports/Edernal%20Care%20pilot%20go%20to%20market.md) identifies downtime truth as unresolved. The supplied target list has 162 tricity prospects, 42 tier-A, with 39 of those appearing on insurer/government cashless lists; most targets are IPD hospitals and two advertise an existing HIS. These facts describe the discovery pool, not demand for this feature. Do not extrapolate an OPD rehearsal into safe IPD discharge or cashless operation.

**Moat contribution: low; possible sales blocker [INFERENCE].** Downtime recovery is readily copied. Its mechanism is protective: fewer missing Charges, duplicated Receipts and unreconciled paper records keep Edernal trustworthy as the [revenue system of record](../../../edernal/reports/Edernal%20Care%20moat.md). Reusable recovery routines could reduce installation/support work, but no compounding asset or retention effect is demonstrated. This is not the later claims-learning layer.

**Baseline:** unknown. Observe 14 staffed days, then 14 comparable days after feasible network/UPS remediation and kit rehearsal. Record covered staffed minutes, confirmed unavailable minutes, unknown minutes, affected desk transactions, paper slips, recovery minutes, duplicate transactions and unexplained rupee/stock differences. No sample means “unknown,” not zero downtime.

**Accepted pilot limits, not market benchmarks:** switch to the prepared kit immediately once the shift lead declares an outage; **controlled paper is the routine bridge for at most four staffed hours**, matching D057's recovery ceiling. Escalate to the Founder/operator at declaration, not hour four; at the ceiling the shift lead activates the hospital's prolonged-downtime contingency and reduces non-essential desk activity without denying emergency care. Complete backfill/reconciliation within **four staffed hours after restoration**, and before the next shift's final cash/stock sign-off. Account for every allocated slip, with zero unexplained rupee differences and zero duplicate effects. Missing/uncertain records remain signed exceptions, not a fabricated success.

**Evidence and limits:** [NABH's entry-level standards](https://dev.nabh.co/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/23.-Entry-Level-Standards-for-Hospital.pdf) establish patient-safety/continuity principles, not a statutory four-hour IT SLA. [AHRQ's hospital downtime study](https://digital.ahrq.gov/sites/default/files/docs/citation/r21hs024350-wernz-final-report-2018.pdf) documents inaccessible electronic procedures, paper reporting, read-only local references and safety problems when downtime procedures are missing/not followed. [Bahmni Outreach's documented Avni-to-Bahmni approach](https://bahmni.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/BAH/pages/3238625296/Bahmni+Outreach+App+for+Community+Healthcare+Offline+ready) separates offline clinical capture and later synchronization through an integration service; it does not demonstrate safe offline hospital money/stock writes. **[INFERENCE]** A bounded paper kit plus rehearsed backfill is lower-risk than treating that outreach design as an automatic billing-sync precedent. Four hours is our accepted engineering/operating ceiling, to be falsified by drills, not attributed to NABH/OpenMRS.

**Validation status:** no customer interviews or measured baseline exist. The 14+14 staffed-day observations and a real four-hour kit/backfill drill remain evidence requirements. No CA/owner choice remains unresolved. A real hospital must supply roster/contact details, actual manual books and printer/device access for rehearsal; paying/observed market need for heavier offline modes remains the Product gate.

## User Stories / Scenarios

1. As a receptionist, I want a dated patient/price reference and numbered paper slips already available, so reception can continue without pretending stale information is live.
2. As a cashier, I want to record the original collection time and paper reference after restoration, so issued Receipts do not conceal the downtime trail.
3. As a pharmacist, I want each paper sale recovered once against its actual batches, so stock is not decremented twice or overwritten to match a count.
4. As a shift lead, I want every allocated slip accounted for and ambiguous online submissions investigated, so reconnection does not create another loss.
5. As an administrator, I want coverage-aware outage evidence and remediation results, so I can distinguish a connectivity purchase from a justified offline-mode requirement.

## Implementation Decisions

### Options and evidence gates

Relative cost/risk below is engineering judgment, not supplier pricing.

| Option                                  | Cost and benefit                                                                                    | Risks and required evidence                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Client connectivity log                 | Low–medium: records failures at the affected desk and survives API downtime without patient data.   | Closed/suspended browser and power loss are unobserved; device clock can drift. Needs staffed-session coverage and manual incident corroboration.                                                                   |
| Server-side gap detection               | Low if using existing logs; identifies gaps in expected desk activity.                              | No requests may mean no patients, lunch, closed browser or failed internet. Cannot classify a gap as an outage alone; server outage may also stop logging. Use only as corroboration.                               |
| Prepared paper kit plus online backfill | Medium: independent of internet; paper survives terminal power loss.                                | Printing, custody, stale prices, re-entry and statutory-document questions. Needs drills, slip accounting and duplicate-safe recovery.                                                                              |
| Read-only PWA                           | Medium–high: can reopen a small cached reference after browser restart when the web server is down. | Staleness, PHI on devices, expired/revoked membership and service-worker upgrades. Does not solve money/stock writes or UPS loss. Consider only if inaccessible references remain a measured failure after the kit. |
| Full offline write/sync                 | Very high: can capture local work without paper while a powered device survives.                    | Distributed authorization, pricing, stock, ordering and legal document identity. Needs observed residual failures and a separately accepted architecture/spec, not a background queue added to existing mutations.  |

Prefer a bounded client observation record over server-only gap inference for the first measurement slice. Keep server readiness/logs and the shift lead's incident note as corroborating evidence, not another write model.

### Tenant, permission and audit ownership

All new domain/infrastructure rows carry `orgId NOT NULL`, tenant-leading indexes and scoped references (D001/D002). Procedures use `orgProcedure`; query/mutation keys include `orgSlug`. Permissions exist only in `packages/auth/src/access.ts` (D003). Add `continuity: observe, prepare, backfill, reconcile, readEvidence`: owner/admin explicitly receive all; reception/cashier/pharmacist receive observe/prepare/backfill; accountant receives reconcile/readEvidence. Backfill additionally requires the existing grants for each effect: OPD creation, billing write or pharmacy sell. Reference export requires the existing Patient/OPD/catalog/pharmacy read grants appropriate to its contents. No continuity grant confers clinical or financial authority by itself.

Audit kit allocation, backfill, price exception approval, void/missing explanations and reconciliation sign-off using existing fire-and-forget `audit()` (D004). Guarded denials remain centrally audited. Do not audit every probe or reference read. Ledger journals commit atomically with financial documents (D012), independent of audit availability.

### Paper identifiers and immutable recovery provenance

New schema ownership is `downtime-kits.ts`, `downtime-slips.ts`, `downtime-backfills.ts` and `connectivity-observations.ts` under `packages/db/src/schema/`, with generated append-only migrations (D022). A kit freezes its Organization timezone, preparation instant, business date and server-read price/tax facts. A slip belongs to one kit and Organization, has a distinct `DT` reference, allocation/custody attribution and a status: allocated, recorded, void or missing. A pending slip is allocated until committed; exceptions carry a reason and supervisor attribution. Reprints use the same references, visibly marked **Copy**.

Allocate internal `DT` references through a separate transactional `downtime-slip` counter; never reserve digital Invoice/Receipt/MRN numbers for paper. Account for unused, void and missing slips without recycling. Internal slips state **Downtime record — not a statutory invoice**, with hospital identity, original event times, Patient/MRN or temporary identity, items/quantities, amounts, method/reference, pharmacy batch/expiry, operator and digital links. **Also require pre-printed, serially numbered manual statutory books**, kept by the shift lead: Bill of Supply for exempt supplies, Tax Invoice for taxable supplies where applicable, and collection/advance receipts. Use their own documented manual series (for example `MBS/2627/00001`, `MTI/2627/00001`, `MRC/2627/00001`, each ≤16 characters and unique in the financial year). [CGST Rule 46(b),(c)](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule46_v1.00.html) allows multiple invoice series and requires issue date; [Rule 49](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/rules/cgst_rules/active/chapter6/rule49_v1.00.html) independently sets the same serial/date rules for Bill of Supply. A money receipt is not itself a Bill of Supply/Tax Invoice. Each slip cross-links its manual supply and collection numbers; retain cancelled book leaves and carbon copies. Printing books/provisioning their actual series is an operator/hospital action, not proof produced by this spec.

`downtime_backfills` is immutable provenance committed with typed links to actual OPD Appointment, Pharmacy sale, Invoice, Payment and Advance Receipt effects; use tenant-composite foreign keys, not an arbitrary polymorphic document id. Original care, supply and collection times are separate when different. Store the stated local time, kit timezone and resolved instant, paper reference, recording Member and server recording instant. New Patient registration uses the ordinary server MRN counter (D010); a paper identity never becomes an MRN. Existing Patient matching requires operator confirmation, never a guessed merge.

Recovery commands cover OPD walk-in/arrival, pharmacy sale, Payment against an existing Invoice and Advance Receipt. An ordinary OPD backfill creates its Appointment/Charges/Invoice/Payments atomically as D015 does. A past attendance keeps the original arrival date/time, receives the normal server token for that date and labels the paper queue reference separately. Pharmacy backfill posts ordinary batch movements (D041), using the recorded batch and supply date to check expiry, but current movement-derived shelf availability to avoid negative stock. Insufficient stock or changed eligibility is an explicit reconciliation exception; never substitute a batch or invent opening stock.

### Dates, prices and lost responses

Keep digital issuance semantics: `billingDocumentContext` uses **server recording time**, Organization-local Business Date and fiscal year; `createdAt`/journal `entryDate` are not backdated. Print the immutable manual statutory number/date and original care/supply/collection times alongside the recording date, visibly **Re-entry of manual document — not a second supply or collection**. The manual document remains the original statutory issuance record; the digital entry is its traceable recorded representation, not a second independent tax invoice/Bill of Supply or second receipt handed to the Patient. Retain the manual copy; never renumber it into the digital series. Manual and digital references must be linked in tax/handover exports so a supply/payment is counted once in its original reporting period, not once per representation.

**Fiscal-year boundary:** a 31 March collection recorded 1 April has a new-year digital Receipt/re-entry number and recording date, plus its old-year manual number/event date. Do not reopen counters or silently edit closed-day journals. The reconciliation supplement attributes the original cash/supply to 31 March and identifies its later digital posting; annual tax/accounting exports must expose that bridge, excluding double counting and supporting closing accrual/adjustment outside the billing ledger. This follows actual supply/collection rather than making delayed computer entry a new revenue event ([CGST s.31](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/documents/Central_Goods_and_Services_Tax_Act__2017_28-September-2022.html), [ICAI AS 9](https://indasaccess.icai.org/Volume-III/AS/asb.html?a=110)); a material omission from already finalized accounts is handled as an identified prior-period correction, not concealed by backdating ([AS 5](https://indasaccess.icai.org/Volume-III/AS/asb.html?a=108)). This accepts recording-date documents **only with that original-time/manual-document bridge**. Ordinary online documents remain unchanged.

A backfill preview compares frozen kit facts, paper amounts and current server facts. Amounts remain bigint paise with D031/D044/D049 rules. Matching prices use the verified kit snapshot; deviations require owner/admin approval, original tax basis and reason. Missing historical tax identity blocks the effect until the designated Finance Reconciliation Lead obtains the original manual document/registration evidence; never infer it from current settings or “approve” unsupported tax.

**D011's narrow amendment is accepted conditionally:** only controlled paper recovery may use server-retained historical facts or an explicitly approved historical-price exception with original tax evidence. Live commands remain unchanged; no arbitrary client-priced Charge path. Implementation must validate scope, retain the snapshot/approval and recheck it in the effect transaction.

**D039's slip-based amendment is accepted conditionally:** lock the scoped allocated slip before D040 resources and atomically commit effects plus immutable provenance. An identical canonical server-validated effect payload returns existing ids with no counter, money or stock effect; a different payload conflicts and returns the recorded ids. This is manual recovery identity, not general request-key infrastructure or automatic financial retry.

D039's accepted exception list also includes [onboarding source rows](./onboarding-import.md) under D059; ordinary commands retain no replay protection. Both exceptions are recorded together in the amended decision, rather than awaiting whichever implementation ships first.

For an ordinary online submission whose response was lost before paper fallback, show **Outcome unknown — check records before entering again**, not “failed” or “Try again.” After reconnection, review source documents by Patient, amount, time and non-cash reference. Link a confirmed existing effect only after explicit authorized review; never claim fuzzy matching proves duplication. Unresolved identity blocks recording/sign-off. Confirmed duplicates use normal Credit Notes/Refunds/returns, never document deletion.

### Prepared reference and measurement interfaces

Use a deliberately downloaded PDF reference in the kit, readable from a managed terminal without the app/server. This is an explicit export, not persisted TanStack Query or a service-worker cache. Include today's known OPD Patients with name/MRN, appointment time and Practitioner; Service prices/tax basis; pharmacy Product/batch price units, expiry and printed MRP. Exclude clinical notes, identifiers beyond MRN, phone/address, balances and live stock assurances. Every page shows Organization, date, generated time and **Reference only — may be stale**. Prepare each staffed day and after approved tariff changes; older files remain visibly expired. Secure custody and manual deletion are required: logout cannot revoke a downloaded file or printed paper. Paper still works when the terminal, printer or UPS fails after preparation.

**Custody and resolution policy:** only hospital-managed encrypted terminals with individually authenticated staff accounts may hold the dated PDF; no personal devices, shared credentials, cloud auto-sync or exported clinical notes. Delete/supersede the reference at the end of its staffed day (within 24 hours); lock printed references/manual books in shift-lead custody. Retain used/void slip evidence and statutory manual leaves under [D057's category schedule](./data-safety-backup-restore-export.md#exit-retention-and-incidents), not indefinitely; unused dated reference sheets are securely destroyed. Keep PHI-free connectivity observations for 90 days server-side, seven days locally; do not attach identifiers to probes. Security/incident logs remain under D057's longer requirements.

The **Shift Lead** owns book/slip custody, unidentified-Patient review and missing/void explanations; the **Finance Reconciliation Lead** owns unknown online outcomes, original tax evidence and money sign-off; the **Duty Pharmacist** resolves actual batch/stock shortages through normal correction movements. Owner/admin authorizes a supported historical price exception. If unresolved, retain an explicit exception and escalate to the Founder/operator; no guessed Patient merge, duplicate collection, batch substitution or “balanced” invented amount may pass sign-off. These are assigned roles; the live hospital must supply the actual people/contact details, not make the policy choice again.

Public contracts, all org-scoped: `continuity.prepareKit({orgSlug, slipCount})` returns kit id/reference/download data; `continuity.getSlip({orgSlug, paperReference})` returns status and recorded source ids; `continuity.previewBackfill({orgSlug, paperReference, events})` returns validated totals/exceptions; `continuity.recordBackfill({orgSlug, paperReference, events, expectedPreview})` returns source ids or a conflict; `continuity.reconcile({orgSlug, kitId, dispositions, expectedVersion})` records sign-off only when every slip is accounted for. `events` is a discriminated union of the four recovery operations above using existing domain input shapes plus original timestamps. Preview includes the kit identity and price/Charge revision; commit rechecks it, never trusts the preview total.

`continuity.recordObservations({orgSlug, observations})` accepts at most 100 bounded observations per request, deduplicated by Organization/device/event identity; `continuity.evidence({orgSlug, from, to})` returns covered/unavailable/unknown minutes, cause-confidence and recovery outcomes. Record browser/API reachability, readiness result, visibility and monotonic duration, not Patient data, URLs or form values. Probe the existing API `/` readiness route, which checks PostgreSQL. `navigator.onLine` is a hint, not proof. Sample once per 30 seconds only while visible, timeout after 5 seconds, and mark unavailable after two consecutive failed probes. Hide/suspend/clock discontinuity becomes unknown. Keep a bounded seven-day PHI-free local queue partitioned by org/member; upload only after fresh authorization, never into another Organization. Logout purges unsent local observations. Operator incidents cover unpowered terminals and explain missing coverage; uploaded observations retain 90 days under the approved policy.

## Test Seams

- In-process guarded router seam, following existing integration tests: foreign-tenant isolation, effect permissions, kit allocation, atomic source/journal commit and slip-based lost-response recovery. Add procedures to `GUARDED_CALLS`.
- Existing business-date/counter/billing seams: midnight and fiscal-year crossing, original versus issuance time, rollback without number gaps, historical price approval and immutable print provenance.
- Existing query-client/RPC seams: queries may retry under current policy; financial backfill never auto-retries, ambiguous ordinary mutation messages preserve uncertainty, reconnection does not submit hidden work. Keep D036 global invalidation.
- Browser reference/probe seam: hidden tabs and offline/server/database failure are classified correctly; a previously downloaded PDF opens without networking. Use the existing test runner plus a managed-terminal/printer drill, not a new end-to-end framework.

## Out of Scope

Clinical downtime care, emergency safety policy, IPD implementation, payer claims, payment gateway acceptance, bulk historical import, backup automation and general accounting close. Future IPD uses Admission (`admissions`), `/$orgSlug/ipd`, `wards`/`beds`, stream `ipd`, Charge source `admission` and an existing Admission-linked Advance Receipt; this spec creates none of them. A future Claim (`claims`) links Admission/Payer; payer shares remain distinct receivables under D029, never fake Payments.

## Explicitly Deferred

Read-only PWA waits for evidence that the prepared reference is inaccessible often enough to matter after remediation. Its follow-on spec must approve an explicit D008 offline-shell exception, a bounded authorized snapshot distinct from live TanStack Query, expiry/unlock behavior, managed-device PHI storage and purge, maximum revocation exposure, service-worker upgrade/rollback and an uncached auth/RPC boundary. Read-only must mean no editable operational forms or queued mutations.

Full offline writes wait for the Product gate and a separate accepted spec. Transactional server counters cannot guarantee gapless final numbering across disconnected terminals: preallocated blocks strand unused numbers, local counters collide and interleaved commits lose global order. Provisional documents can become final only after server acceptance, with visible identity mapping; already handed-out immutable final documents cannot simply be renumbered or repriced. Alternatives include an authoritative powered hospital-local server, or an approved change to numbering/document rules—not an invisible sync workaround.

That decision also needs durable replay protection revisiting D039 beyond backfill, bounded offline authority/revocation policy revisiting D008/D009, trusted tariff versions revisiting D011, stock reservations/conflict resolution, clock provenance, patient identity reconciliation, conflict UI, ledger atomicity, recovery after browser loss and upgrade testing. Evaluate these against actual residual losses and buyer willingness to fund them. Do not add speculative write-sync slices before those contracts are settled.
