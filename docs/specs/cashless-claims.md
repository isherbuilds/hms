# Spec: Cashless and TPA claims

Status: staged draft — design accepted in D055; implementation remains market-gated
Authority: founder request, 2026-10-04 — implementation-ready options for 10–50 bed private Indian hospitals, evaluated on hospital desire and moat only.
Supersedes: none

## Problem

A cashless Admission does not end financially at discharge. The insurance desk must obtain authorisation, answer queries, submit the right documents and reconcile later remittances. Sponsor capture currently records only potential coverage; it neither transfers liability nor proves collection. Recording an insurer as a Payment would falsely settle an unpaid Invoice. An unpaid or disputed deduction must remain visible rather than disappearing into a spreadsheet or an edited bill.

## Solution

Add a Claim linked to an Admission and its liable Payer. Staff continue using the Payer's portal/email/manual submission channel, recording evidenced requests and decisions in Edernal Care. The Claim holds stage-specific private documents, authorisation clocks, payer tariff/package snapshots and coded outcomes. The final IPD Invoice remains one immutable supply document, with an explicit patient/payer liability split. Bank receipts, TDS and disputed or accepted deductions resolve that split through linked source documents. Admission discharge and bed release remain clinical/operational decisions, never consequences of insurer response or payment.

## Validation / Evidence

### Hospital desire and moat

**Hospital desire: medium evidence, not validated demand.** The supplied target-list census has 162 tricity prospects and 42 tier-A prospects; 39 of those 42 appear on insurer or government cashless lists. Most targets are IPD hospitals; two advertise an existing HIS. Listing demonstrates an addressable cashless workflow, not insured volume, dissatisfaction or willingness to switch. The [buyer research](../../../edernal/research_notes/Edernal%20Care%20pilot%20go%20to%20market/buyers_market_competitors.md) identifies small-hospital TPA complexity, but this is a **vendor claim**, without an independent 10–50 bed buyer survey. The [discovery evidence](../../../edernal/research_notes/Edernal%20Care%20pilot%20go%20to%20market/sales_script_motion.md) names document gaps, delayed claims and denials; numerical leakage estimates are consultant/vendor claims, not a baseline. The [market report](../../../edernal/reports/Edernal%20Care%20pilot%20go%20to%20market.md#the-sales-script-ask-about-last-week-show-their-own-bill) explicitly asks what happened to the last delayed/rejected cashless claim. No customer interviews have happened yet.

**Moat contribution: high potential, unproved.** The [moat thesis](../../../edernal/reports/Edernal%20Care%20moat.md#claims-is-the-best-later-compounding-layer-not-an-exclusive-rail) places trusted revenue records first and claims learning later. Linking Admission → Charge → Invoice → Claim → actual settlement gives outcomes, not merely uploaded PDFs. Versioned checklists and coded payer queries/deductions may improve future first-pass completeness. Public NHCX connectivity is not exclusivity. Existing insurance-desk products, including [ABI ClaimBook](https://www.abi-health.com/marketplace/product/claimbook-839e5e78-3ae7-44bc-a5d5-e9e3d718071d), claim similar workflow coverage; their feature pages do not prove comparative outcomes.

**Baseline and targets:** collect the hospital's preceding 30 consecutive claims, or all claims from the preceding 90 days if fewer, with missing outcomes explicit. Measure first-pass document completeness, preventable-query rate, submission-to-first-cash days, unreconciled payer balance and staff minutes per claim. Proposed pilot targets, not industry benchmarks: every new cashless Admission captured; 100% of posted payer balances reconcile to source documents and ledger; at least 95% of closed claims have coded outcomes and evidence; at least 20% relative reduction in preventable-query rate without increased median desk minutes. Compare same Payer/package/specialty cohorts over 90 days; show counts and unresolved cases, and do not claim improvement for incomparable or small cohorts.

**Remaining market gate:** a paying hospital and named insurance-desk owner records meaningful insured volume/value over a pilot month, or supplies a signed payer requirement with tariffs and lifecycle, under the [Insurance/TPA product gate](../product.md#roadmap-gates). Last-three-discharge/remittance observation, baseline counts and real contract evidence are adoption inputs, not unresolved accounting policy. D055 decides TDS, deductions, authority, staffing and retention without CA/owner consultation. Implementation reuses IPD's accepted contract; live IPD is a delivery dependency, not a new design approval or permission to invent paid demand.

### Regulatory and rail evidence

[IRDAI Health Insurance Regulations 2016](<https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_2_33_00044_193804_1523351752525&filename=irdai_(health_insurance)_regulations%2C_2016.pdf&type=regulation>) provide historical policy/claims provenance; the **current one-/three-hour cashless clocks come from the 29 May 2024 Master Circular**, not a claim that those timelines existed in 2016 ([official circular](https://irdai.gov.in/document-detail?documentId=4942918), [IRDAI Health FAQ question 24](https://irdai.gov.in/health-dept)). Insurers decide pre-authorisation within **one hour of receipt** and final discharge authorisation within **three hours of receipt of the hospital request**. The circular says additional hospital charges caused by delay beyond three hours are borne by the insurer, not the policyholder; record those as an evidenced insurer claim, never an automatic patient extra. These clocks neither guarantee bank settlement within three hours nor apply automatically to PM-JAY/corporate contracts.

[NRCeS ABDM FHIR guide v6.5.0](https://nrces.in/ndhm/fhir/r4/6.5.0/hcx-profile.html) verifies open NHCX profiles for eligibility, pre-auth, claims, status and payment communication. It does not verify this hospital's credentials, a reachable production transport API, or its Payers' live readiness. The moat packet records limited-uptake reporting in November 2025 and a March 2026 NHA innovation announcement; no consolidated official 2026 live-claims total is verified. Vendor onboarding totals are not claims throughput. Conventional submission remains the accepted path.

### Reference systems and accounting evidence

[Frappe/Marley Health v16 Insurance Claim](https://marleyhealth.io/wiki/insurance-claim) separates invoiced coverage, insurer response and actual Payment Entry; [Insurance Payor](https://marleyhealth.io/docs/v16/user/manual/en/healthcare/patient-insurance/insurance-payor) explicitly owns claims-receivable and rejected-claims-expense accounts. These observed document contracts support our debtor split and loss account, not automatic copying of their journal implementation. ERPNext's [Inpatient Record](https://frappehealth.com/docs/v13/user/manual/en/healthcare/inpatient_record), [Bahmni/OpenMRS-backed ADT](https://bahmni.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/BAH/pages/114923665/Admit,+Discharge+and+Transfer+Patients) and [Odoo's third-party hospital module](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/17.0/cerevantix_health_care_management) provide Admission/movement/stock/billing precedents; Odoo's module page is vendor feature evidence only, not verified cashless compliance.

[CBDT Circular 8/2009 (24 November 2009)](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/8/2009-circular-no.-8/2009-dated-24-11-2009) applies Income-tax Act 1961 **s.194J** to TPA payments to hospitals for professional medical services; TDS is tax paid on the hospital's behalf, not a reduction in the healthcare fee. Section labels/rates must follow the law effective on the settlement date (the 1961 Act citation is legacy provenance, not an assertion that its section numbering survives the 2026 transition). The [Income Tax Department's Form 26AS mismatch guidance](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/e-filing-manage-tax-credit-mismatch-faq) requires deductor corrections for missing/mismatched TDS credit. [ICAI AS 9 §9.3](https://resource.cdn.icai.org/27275asb-as-9.pdf) separates subsequent collection uncertainty from revenue originally earned: actual tariff corrections reduce revenue; collection losses do not retrospectively rewrite the supply. HMS remains a source-document subledger, not an income-tax return or Ind AS impairment engine.

[NHA HBP 2.2 §1.3](https://nha.gov.in/strapi/uploads/User_guidelines_for_HBP_2_2_6d2bc0412a.pdf) includes bed/nursing/medicines/diagnostics in package rates and prohibits beneficiary collection for covered care; exhaustion exceptions require advance communication to beneficiary and SHA. Use the actual state/scheme/version and authorized exceptions, not an invented universal “non-payable” list. A payer deduction letter alone never establishes patient liability.

## User Stories / Scenarios

1. As an insurance-desk Member, I want evidenced authorisation and query history with deadlines, so the next shift can act without reconstructing email.
2. As a cashier, I want patient share, available Credit and payer share separately, so I collect only the patient's liability without pretending insurance is cash.
3. As an accountant, I want one remittance allocated across named Claims with TDS and deductions, so bank, receivables and source documents agree.
4. As an administrator, I want approved Payer tariffs/packages and checklist versions, so staff do not silently use retail prices or obsolete requirements.
5. As an owner, I want aged unsettled Claims and reason-coded outcomes, so delayed cash and recurring preventable queries are distinguishable.

## Implementation Decisions

### Dependency, scope and ownership

Requires [IPD admission-to-discharge](./ipd-admission-to-discharge.md): `Admission`/`admissions`, `wards`, `beds`, `/$orgSlug/ipd`, typed Admission parents, Charge source `admission`, IPD stream `ipd`, Admission-linked existing Advance Receipts, Charge revision and final-Invoice commands. Claims does not implement a second admission or invoice path. Use Claim sections on the Admission and `/$orgSlug/billing/claims` for cross-Admission worklists; financial activity stays on the IPD billing desk (D013, D019).

Reuse `payers` and `patient_payers`. Registration Sponsor stays measurement-only and one-per-Patient; Claim creation snapshots policy/employee identifiers after explicit confirmation. Later Sponsor edits never rewrite Claims. `Claim.payerId` names the legally liable insurer/scheme, not merely the processing TPA; optional `processingPayerId` references the existing TPA master. One non-cancelled Claim per Admission; multi-policy coordination is deferred. Payer deactivation prevents new Claims but does not block servicing existing debt.

New source rows: `claims`, `claim_events`, `claim_requests`, `claim_queries`, `claim_documents`, `claim_invoice_allocations`, `claim_remittances`, `claim_settlements`, `claim_adjustments`, `payer_tariffs`, `payer_tariff_lines`, `payer_checklists` and `payer_checklist_items`. All carry `orgId NOT NULL`, composite tenant foreign keys and tenant-leading operational indexes. Claims hold state and a monotonically increasing `revision`; monetary totals and aged balances derive from immutable children. D031 bigint paise and D042 shared vocabulary constants/zod apply. Migrations are generated and append-only (D022).

### Exact lifecycle and requests

Exact Claim states: `draft`, `preauth_requested`, `approved`, `partially_approved`, `query`, `denied`, `enhancement_requested`, `discharge_requested`, `discharge_authorised`, `discharge_partially_authorised`, `discharge_denied`, `submitted`, `settled`, `short_settled`, `rejected`, `cancelled`. A request additionally records `kind = preauth | enhancement | discharge | submission`, sequence, requested amount, submitted time, payer-received time/evidence, response time/document and outcome. Query identifies its request, coded reason, staff assignee, due time and reply evidence; Claim `query` retains the interrupted stage. Approved amounts are **cumulative ceilings**, not increments.

Transitions are explicit:

- `draft → preauth_requested`; its response produces `approved`, `partially_approved`, `query` or `denied`. Full means approved amount equals requested amount; partial means positive and smaller; denial approves zero.
- Approved/partially approved Claims may request enhancement; its response uses the same outcomes. A denied enhancement preserves the previously approved ceiling, displayed separately from the denial. Re-request from denial requires a new evidenced request, never overwriting the rejected one.
- With an existing approval, including after enhancement denial, staff may enter `discharge_requested`; response produces `discharge_authorised`, `discharge_partially_authorised`, `query` or `discharge_denied`. A denial does not erase liability or prevent clinical discharge.
- A query reply returns to that request's waiting state, or `submitted` for a submission query. Multiple queries remain separate and must all be answered or explicitly resolved by payer evidence before recording the stage decision.
- Discharge authorisation plus issued final Invoice permits `submitted`. An explicit admin override permits submission after discharge denial, with reason/evidence, without inventing approval. Payer adjudication may query, reject or remit. Partial non-final remittances leave `submitted`; an evidenced final payment produces `settled` if fully satisfied by cash/TDS, otherwise `short_settled`. Zero-payment final rejection produces `rejected`.
- `short_settled` and `rejected` may return to `submitted` through an evidenced appeal; recovery stays linked to the same Claim. Only an unissued/unsettled Claim can be cancelled. Cancellation with issued debt is refused, not a write-off.

Requests, decisions, submissions and monetary documents are append-only. Corrections append a superseding event with author/reason, retaining original times and documents; conditional revision checks prevent stale shifts recording incompatible outcomes (D026, D040). Authorization is evidence, not a guarantee of payment.

### Private checklist and clocks

Publish immutable Payer checklist versions, selected and snapshotted per request. Default stage items are proposed requirements, not universal insurance rules:

| Stage       | Checklist items                                                                                                                             |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pre-auth    | policy/card and identity proof, signed request, Admission/Practitioner clinical note, estimate, eligibility evidence                        |
| Enhancement | prior authorisation, revised estimate, progress/clinical justification and supporting investigation scans                                   |
| Query       | payer query letter, requested supporting items and signed response                                                                          |
| Discharge   | signed discharge summary, reviewed final-bill estimate or issued Invoice, treatment/procedure records, reports and prior authorisations     |
| Submission  | issued Invoice, final authorisation, signed claim form, discharge summary, required supporting bills/reports and submission acknowledgement |
| Settlement  | remittance advice, bank reference, deduction letter, TDS advice/certificate when applicable                                                 |

Checklist items have stable codes, required/conditional applicability, and a documented condition selected by staff. Before sending, every applicable required item must have a ready private file or immutable internal-document reference; a missing item requires admin override with reason, retained in the request snapshot. No forced identity document for a Payer that does not require it. TDS advice can support posting before a certificate arrives; the missing certificate remains a follow-up.

Use existing `file.createUpload/finalizeUpload/getReadUrl`, private storage and 15-minute links (D005); claim-owned document links prove same-org Claim/file and lock file metadata before attaching. Extend `file.delete` dependency protection to claim files and published tariff/checklist evidence. Sent versions cannot detach/delete; replace with a new version. Generic file access remains Organization-scoped under existing grants; this spec does not promise finer-grained document ACLs. Audit attachment correction, download/export, overrides and sensitive mutations without storing clinical text, policy identifiers or URLs in audit metadata (D004).

Store distinct readiness, submission, evidenced payer receipt, query, reply, authorisation and cash times. From payer receipt, derive `dueAt = receivedAt + 60 minutes` for insurer pre-auth and `+180 minutes` for discharge. Unknown receipt shows **Receipt unconfirmed**, with submission elapsed time separately; never substitute created/submitted time. Queries do not pause/reset the original clock; a query is not an approval. A final decision stops elapsed time and retains lateness. Scheme/corporate/enhancement/query/submission deadlines come from the versioned contract, without invented national defaults. Timestamp corrections retain the prior clock evidence. No background scheduler or email provider is required.

### Invoice liability and settlement documents

Extend `ipd.previewFinalInvoice({orgSlug, admissionId, billingEndAt})` to return allocation preview alongside the unchanged `billingEndAt`. Extend `ipd.issueFinalInvoice` with `claimAllocation: {claimId, expectedClaimRevision, allocationLines}` alongside top-level `billingEndAt`, `expectedChargeRevision`, `expectedGrandTotal`, advance allocations and Payments; pass `billingEndAt` through unchanged in both input and result, preserving IPD's billing-end validation. Allocation lines identify reviewed Charges, payer gross share and reason/authorisation references; the server re-verifies totals and authorization. Claim allocation is mandatory for an Admission with an active Claim; a zero payer share requires explicit reason. It maps Charges to issued Invoice lines atomically. Patient share plus payer share equals the grand total exactly, including tax/rounding; cap payer share at evidenced final authorisation unless admin expressly accepts uncovered payer risk.

`issueInvoiceTx` debits Patient Receivables only for patient share and a distinct asset account for payer share; revenue/GST treatment stays unchanged. Add nullable tenant-FK `accounts.payerId`, unique per Organization/Payer; use code `1200:PAYER:<payerId>`, null `systemKey`, and no manual account editor. Extend the existing journal line account reference to either a system key or verified Payer reference; retain one balanced atomic posting path and D012 source uniqueness. Patient Payments and Advance Allocations reduce only patient share. Deposit remains an existing Admission-linked Advance Receipt (D035), never a payer receipt.

One `claim_remittances` source records liable Payer, bank credit amount/reference/date and advice. `claim.recordRemittance` explicitly allocates it to invoice-linked Claims of that Payer, creating `claim_settlements` and existing bank Payments/Receipts for **cash only**. Add nullable `payments.payerId`; ordinary billing collection cannot set it. Payer Payments credit that Payer's receivable, not Patient Receivables; total cash allocations must equal bank credit, with no over-allocation. A remittance can span Claims and a Claim can receive several remittances. No receipt is issued for TDS or a disallowance.

Each deduction line names the original Invoice/Claim, affected lines when applicable, amount, reason code and evidence. Staff choose one disposition, never infer it from the payer letter:

- TDS: immutable linked adjustment debits TDS Receivable (`1250`) and credits payer receivable at evidenced settlement; no cash Receipt, Credit Note, fee discount or GST change. Store hospital PAN, actual deductor TAN/name (insurer or processing TPA), financial year/quarter, applicable provision, deduction amount/date and advice/Form 16A reference. Accountant reconciles each quarter and before tax handover against Form 26AS, with `awaiting_certificate | awaiting_26as | matched | mismatch` evidence status; these statuses do not post another adjustment. Missing/mismatched credit remains a TDS-asset follow-up to the deductor, never re-collected from Patient or silently expensed. ₹100 due, ₹90 cash plus ₹10 evidenced TDS clears payer debt but leaves ₹10 TDS asset until tax-credit utilization is handled in the hospital's accounting system.
- Co-pay/non-covered patient liability: **before financial discharge review/final allocation**, exclude non-payable items from payer share and inform Patient with policy/contract and acceptance evidence; do not bill the same item twice. If an issued payer allocation genuinely needs correction, accountant posts a linked liability transfer debiting Patient Receivables and crediting payer receivable only with evidence of an existing permitted patient obligation. Already allocated co-pay cannot transfer twice. No retrospective transfer of unexplained short-payment, prohibited PM-JAY package items, or insurer-delay charges. Clinical departure remains independent; outstanding review does not detain Patient.
- Contractual tariff deduction: if discovered after issuance, accountant creates a line-linked **Credit Note against payer receivable** for the actual overbilled supply, never a write-off. Apply the agreed tariff before invoicing whenever known. Financial correction reduces revenue; output GST reduces only when CGST §15(3)/§34 conditions permit (tax policy below).
- Unexplained short-payment/accepted collection loss: initially code it and leave payer debt open/disputed. When owner/admin explicitly approves non-recovery with recorded amount, reason, remittance/evidence and approval actor/time, accountant posts an immutable write-off to **TPA deductions expense (`5100`)**, crediting payer receivable; do not change revenue/GST or transfer it to Patient. Approval is a required source reference, not an unchecked free-text “approved” flag. Later recovery/reclassification reverses the exact write-off before reapplication. This chosen control is consistent with AS 9 and Marley's rejected-claim expense account; it is not a promise that the loss is automatically income-tax deductible.
- Disputed/unknown deduction without approved write-off: record the coded observation but post nothing; payer balance stays due and appealable. Final insurer response never forces write-off.

Corrections reverse the referenced adjustment with a linked document and re-apply, never edit/delete it. Extend Credit Notes and Refunds with liability attribution so corrections reverse the correct debtor, cap against its source balance and refund actual money to its original payer; patient refunds cannot consume payer settlements. Reports/worklists read these sources and existing journals only. The invoice-wide balance includes TDS/write-offs/credits once; transfers net to zero. Patient and payer subtotals must reconcile to it. Settled clinical care or final payer response never zeroes unresolved debt.

Claims Slice 1 owns extending `invoiceBalancesFor` in `packages/api/src/lib/invoice-balance.ts` and revenue-control's `report.invoiceRegister` row/totals contract with signed `adjustmentsTotal` (TDS/write-off debt reductions are negative) and `adjustmentReversalsTotal` (linked reversals reinstating debt are positive). Outstanding becomes `netBilled − paymentsTotal − allocationsTotal + refundsTotal + adjustmentsTotal + adjustmentReversalsTotal`; these sources are neither Payments nor Credit Notes and do not change net billed revenue. Liability transfers and their reversals have opposite patient/payer effects and net to zero invoice-wide. Shared balance consumers, Invoice Register screen and Excel use the same source aggregation, counting each adjustment/reversal once.

Document the lock order as Admission → Claim(s, id order) → existing Charge/Invoice order → remittance → existing Advance Receipt order → file metadata, skipping unused records; remittances lock all target Admissions/Claims/Invoices deterministically before checking balances. Follow D040 without automatic retry; no replay-key machinery is added (D039). After a lost response, staff inspect recorded documents before resubmitting.

### Tariffs, permissions and outcome dataset

Admin publishes immutable effective-dated Payer tariff versions with contract evidence: insurer service rates or PM-JAY package code, scheme/state/version, contracted gross amount, mapped Services, inclusions, exclusions, permitted extras and patient-collection rule. External package codes belong here, not on `catalog_items` (D048). An Admission explicitly chooses a version; changes affect future Charges only. Package delivery creates one package Charge via the IPD posting path; included work remains traced as included, not duplicate Charges. Extras require a mapped permitted contract line. Allocate a package's revenue across mapped Service categories using snapshotted amounts summing to package value; no unclassified default. Unsupported combinatorial eligibility/pricing remains manual reviewed scope, not an invented PM-JAY rules engine.

Add `claim: [read, manage, allocate, settle, adjust, approveLoss, configure, export]` only in `packages/auth/src/access.ts` (D003). **Staffing fit accepted:** use existing roles, no insurance-desk role or new permission store. Owner/admin receive all and exclusively approve losses/configure contracts; reception read/manage (requests/queries/private uploads, never adjustments); cashier read/allocate (reviewed split and actual patient collection); accountant read/settle/adjust/export (TDS reconciliation, evidenced patient transfers, Credit Notes and remittance/loss posting). Pharmacist none. Give accountant `file:upload` for advice/certificates; generic file ACL remains org-scoped, so this is not fine-grained clinical privacy. Issue requires `billing:write` plus `claim:allocate`. Invoice Credit Notes/refunds retain **`billing:creditNote`**, currently accountant/owner/admin; unused-advance refunds retain **`billing:advanceRefund`**, currently cashier/accountant/owner/admin ([existing grants](../../packages/auth/src/access.ts), [billing guards](../../packages/api/src/routers/billing.ts)). Cashier cannot refund an insurer settlement or reverse an Invoice by virtue of its advance-refund grant. Small hospitals may combine existing roles by D003's union, with named shift responsibilities and attributed approvals; do not grant admin merely to process ordinary Claims.

`claim.worklist` returns keyset-paged pending pre-auth/enhancement, unanswered queries ordered by due time, discharge requests and unsettled receivables by submitted age (0–30/31–60/61–90/90+ days), with separate unsubmitted-discharge age. Financially open short-settled/rejected Claims remain visible. Reuse list filters, polling and invalidation (D036–D037, D043).

Versioned reason codes cover missing document, clinical justification, identity/coverage mismatch, tariff/package mismatch, non-covered item, co-pay, TDS, duplicate, late submission and other/unknown. Preserve payer raw reason and contract clause privately, mapped code, responsible stage, checklist/tariff version, amounts and event times. Unknown/unresolved are explicit, never negative training labels. `claim.exportOutcomes` yields tenant-local rows and source IDs, not files or patient identifiers. Cross-hospital reuse, model training and automated adjudication need separate lawful-purpose approval; hashes are not anonymisation. Dataset completeness is measured independently of financial recovery.

D055 conditionally amends D029 **when Claims ships**: paid/volume-gated Claims is the accepted payer-domain implementation, retaining distinct receivables and the four actual Payment methods; no further CA/founder decision is required. D012's conditional source list includes atomic remittance cash settlements, TDS, approved write-offs, liability transfers and exact linked reversals; authorisation and deduction observations alone post nothing. D025/D049 amendments remain owned by accepted IPD D056; Claims adds no conflicting price basis.

### Tax, correction and retention policy

**Tax follows the supply, not who remits.** Apply IPD's accepted composite-healthcare classification and statutory documents (D052/D056): payer allocation/TDS/patient transfer/write-off/refund do not change supply value or GST merely by moving/settling debt. A tariff Credit Note is a commercial revenue correction; [CGST §34](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter7/section34_v1.00.html) allows tax reduction only subject to statutory conditions and reporting deadline (30 November following supply FY or relevant annual-return filing, whichever earlier), and [§15(3)](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/CGST-bill-e.html) governs post-supply discounts. Snapshot whether the Credit Note reverses eligible output GST or is financial-only with no GST reduction; never use bad-debt write-off to reverse output tax. Qualifying healthcare remains exempt under [CBIC Circular 32/06/2018 §5](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/circularno-32-cgst.pdf); independent taxable goods/room exceptions retain their own classification. Refund only evidenced actual cash to its original remitter within source caps, never TDS assets or payer money to Patient.

**Retention decided:** financial Claim sources, corrections, tariff/checklist versions used in a claim, TDS advice/certificates and necessary supporting documents remain available for the **longest applicable** retention period: six years from the end of the relevant assessment year under [s.44AA/Rule 6F(5)](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/rule-6f) ([government explanation, FAQs 30–31](https://www.investindia.gov.in/faq-pdf/52/en)); 72 months from the annual-return **due date**, not bill/filing date, under [CGST §36](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter8/section36_v1.00.html); and any longer entity/state/scheme obligation. Those legacy income-tax citations describe the requested baseline: apply the settlement/tax-year's operative successor law if longer, not a frozen section/rate. Indoor clinical records have a separate national three-year floor from treatment commencement under [NMC medical-ethics §1.3.1](https://nmc.org.in/storage/cms/Ethics-Regulations-2002.pdf), not a universal state-law maximum. Claim evidence that is also clinical follows the longer financial period; state-specific longer clinical periods override the floor. Keep unresolved debt/appeals, litigation, inquiry and tax-reopening records on hold; CGST proceedings retain records until one year after final disposal or normal expiry, whichever later. No automatic deletion at three/six years and no destruction on settlement; this spec permits controlled authorized export and append-only corrections, not erasure of source history. Minimize duplicate clinical files, keep them private, and record retention basis/hold; actual facility state/contract is setup data, not a deferred legal-design question.

## Out of Scope

Clinical coding/EMR replacement, eligibility guarantees, automated insurer decisions, patient reimbursement claims, general accounting journals, lender/factoring products, money custody, historic claim import, and a universal PM-JAY adjudication engine.

## Explicitly Deferred

NHCX transport integration and ABDM certification: separate accepted slice only after verified sandbox/production API documentation, credentials, signatures, live named Payer, compliance owner and end-to-end test evidence. No guessed endpoint, placeholder adapter or readiness claim. Also deferred: cheque acceptance/clearing (D029), multi-policy/multi-Payer Claims, automated reminders, external portal scraping, cross-hospital outcome sharing and predictive denial models. Existing four actual Payment methods remain fixed.

## Open Questions

Only market gates remain: a **paying hospital with named operational/insurance-desk owner**, and **measured meaningful insured volume or a signed payer requirement with real tariffs/lifecycle**, under the [IPD and Insurance/TPA product gates](../product.md#roadmap-gates). Real contract versions, service mappings, checklists and submission deadlines must be supplied/configured as part of that paid onboarding; there is no guessed default that makes an unsupported contract billable. IPD is the accepted delivery dependency. D055 resolves TDS/26AS, deduction-versus-Credit-Note/write-off, tax, patient transfer/refund authority, existing-role staffing, retention and conditional D029/D012 amendments; no CA, legal or owner design consultation remains. Citations do not prove paid demand or insured volume. NHCX access gates only its explicitly deferred integration, not manual-channel Claims.
