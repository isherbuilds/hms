# Supplier bill reconciliation

Observed 2026-10-04. Decision: [D060](../decisions.md#d060--supplier-bills-need-tax-aware-non-stock-adjustments).
Implemented 2026-10-04 in receiving. Actual supplier validation remains the [pilot gate below](#next-falsification).

## Question

Does the original Receive goods model — stock-line net sum, with at most ±₹0.99 difference — reproduce Indian pharmaceutical supplier invoices, including freight, handling, cash discount, schemes, credit adjustments, TCS and round-off?

## Answer

**Not universally.** Seventeen distinct, populated B2B invoices were inspected, not blank invoice templates. Fifteen reconcile numerically to the stock-line model within ₹0.99 after entering the printed line discounts and free quantities. Two have separately taxed non-stock acquisition charges that the original receipt could not represent: freight ₹270 + ₹48.60 GST, and packing/forwarding ₹100 + ₹18 GST. Implemented Bill adjustments now retain those charges and their separately printed GST. Creating a fictitious stock Product for these charges is not a valid workaround.

This convenience sample does **not** establish that header adjustments are common across Indian distributors or the pilot's suppliers. CD, scheme, CN/DN and TCS footer fields recur, but a printed zero/blank field is not evidence of an actual adjustment. The observed non-zero CDs are already applied to the lines before tax; do not deduct their footer totals twice. The conclusion is narrower and sufficient: the purchasing contract cannot promise universal reconciliation using stock lines plus round-off alone.

Receiving now accepts reasoned freight/packing charges and bill discounts with their GST. Settlement credits and historical TCS were left out: none appeared non-zero in the sample, and TCS stopped on 1-Apr-2025. The investigation itself changed no code; the implemented D060 contract below supersedes the stock-only limitation.

## Evidence

### Exact shipped model

- [`receiptLineCost`](../../packages/api/src/core/receipt-math.ts): `gross = (billed stock qty / packSize) × printed rate`; `taxable = gross × (1 − discountPercent/100)`; `GST = taxable × gstPercent/100`; `net = taxable + GST`. Arithmetic uses bigint in 10⁻⁸ paisa, with no per-line rounding; only the document stock sum rounds half-up to paise. The supplier rate is tax-exclusive. `freeQty` adds stock but not money. `adjustmentsTotal` adds charges and subtracts discounts, each with its GST; `billMatches` checks the ±99-paise residual.
- [`pharmacy-stock.ts`](../../packages/api/src/routers/pharmacy-stock.ts), `receiveGoods`: every delivery needs supplier name, `billTotal` and priced lines. Billed quantity must be divisible by the priced pack size. `goods_receipt_adjustments` rows retain the kind, reason, positive amount and GST. Saving rejects a residual outside ±99 paise with `BAD_REQUEST`. Opening counts refuse pricing, bill total and adjustments. Existing receipts get no adjustment rows or money reinterpretation. No supplier-payable, inventory-cost or input-GST journals are created.
- [`receipt-lines.ts`](../../apps/web/src/lib/receipt-lines.ts), `billSummary`, uses the same arithmetic. [`receive.tsx`](../../apps/web/src/routes/$orgSlug/pharmacy/receive.tsx) offers **Add freight or discount** rows and one totals line: lines, adjustments and round-off.

### Statute and reference-system evidence

- [CGST Act, sections 15(2)(c), 15(3), 16 and 17 (CBIC)](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/CGST-bill-e.html): supplier-charged incidental expenses including packing enter supply value; invoice-recorded discounts can reduce taxable value; business input GST has eligibility conditions; exempt-use/common credits require restriction/apportionment. This CBIC page is the original Act, not a consolidated 2026 filing manual. [Current CBIC section 16 endpoint](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content/html/tax_repository/gst/acts/2017_CGST_act/active/chapter5/section16_v1.00.html) was indexed but its direct fetch failed certificate validation. Nothing here claims the original page's old deadlines are current.
- [CBIC Circular 92/11/2019-GST](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/circular-cgst-92.pdf), parts A–D: distinguish gratuitous gifts/samples from “buy one get one” single-price offers; discounts and financial/commercial CNs are not interchangeable with tax credit notes. Do not infer GST treatment from the label “free” or “CD” alone, and do not infer an ITC reversal from a settlement offset alone.
- [ICAI AS 2 (revised 2016), paragraphs 6–7 and 11–13](https://resource.cdn.icai.org/69232asb55316-as2.pdf): cost includes directly attributable acquisition/freight, **excludes recoverable taxes**, and deducts trade discounts/rebates. A genuine prompt-payment financing discount is not automatically a trade discount reducing inventory cost. Retain the invoice's taxable/tax facts independently of how the supplier settles it.
- [Income Tax Department, enacted Finance Act 2025 highlights](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/20117/14614766/Highlights-to-Finance-Act-2025.pdf/da0e29ac-e70f-f705-824e-a8efe2460a55?t=1767817037874): indexed primary text states 206C(1H) sale-of-goods TCS withdrawn from 1-Apr-2025; direct fetch returned 403. [PSPCL Income Tax Circular 01/2025](https://docs.pspcl.in/docs/dycaoaar20250404111604606.pdf) was fetched and quotes the enacted proviso that the subsection no longer applies from that date, instructing its billing system to stop collecting it. Do not automatically calculate historical 206C(1H) TCS on new pharmaceutical bills in 2026; #7's legacy zero template field does not establish a live obligation.
- [ERPNext Landed Cost Voucher](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/landed-cost-voucher): additional acquisition charges attach to purchase receipt/invoice items and valuation, rather than being fictitious stock quantities. This is reference behavior, not evidence that the pilot uses ERPNext. [Marg's purchase-discount scheme help](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/rate-&-discount-master/165042/1/Sales-Scheme-on-Purchase-Discount) describes item purchase discount and free-quantity scheme representation; it does not justify automatically changing HMS's selling price.

## What this proves / does not prove

- Proves that real-looking, populated B2B artifacts contain non-stock, GST-bearing freight/handling and recurring footer summaries. Stock-only reconciliation fails on two observed invoices; line discounts/scheme quantities explain the non-zero CDs in this set.
- Does not prove nationwide prevalence, authenticity of third-party uploads, the pilot's supplier mix, legal correctness of each supplier's tax classification, or that CN/TCS adjustments were non-zero: **none was observed non-zero in this set**. The set spans manufacturer/PCD/consignment/wholesale and adjacent medical-device/API suppliers; it is clustered by seller and upload bundle, not random.
- “Pass” is arithmetic only. Gift rows, fractional API grams, missing tax provenance and a supplier's old balances may still exceed the current stock workflow. These samples do not license changing the no-fractional-units rule or building a filing engine.

## What this means for us

### Accepted boundary

The receiving extension keeps the strict ±99-paise residual validator and never plugs a mismatch. A settlement credit or historical TCS line is not recorded until a real pilot bill needs one.

### Implemented receiving contract

1. `pharmacy.receiveGoods` accepts `adjustments: []` by default. Each row has `kind` (`landed_charge` for freight/packing/handling, `invoice_discount` for a bill-level discount not already in the lines), a required `reason`, and positive `amount` and `gstAmount` in paise. The kind sets the sign.
2. Rows persist in `goods_receipt_adjustments` (`id`, `orgId`, `receiptId`, `kind`, `reason`, `amount`, `gstAmount`) with a tenant-scoped composite FK `(orgId, receiptId)` and a positive-amount CHECK. UUIDv7 ids keep entry order. Opening receipts accept no adjustments.
3. `billTotal` is the printed grand total: `residual = billTotal − roundToPaise(sum(exact stock-line nets)) − charges + discounts`, bounded to ±99 paise. Original line rates, discounts, GST and free quantities are preserved; MRP and sale prices never change.
4. When purchasing valuation is built, allocate charge base plus nonrecoverable GST, and discounts, across affected receipt lines by post-discount taxable value; keep eligible GST out.
5. `tests/unit/receipt-lines.test.ts` covers freight turning the Zestica mismatch into a match, a discount, and a 100-paise residual; `tests/integration/pharmacy-stock.test.ts` covers persistence, the residual refusal and the tenant composite FK.

## Next falsification

At the first actual supplier deliveries, compare 10–20 **consecutive, authorised pilot supplier invoices**, including a mixed-GST bill, a scheme, freight and any credit-offset example. Record numerator/denominator by seller, the supplier's printed rounding convention and whether consideration/payable differ. Revisit the implemented kinds if a real invoice has a component that cannot be represented without changing tax/cost meaning. This remains a real operational gate, not an unresolved CA/owner design choice; public artifacts and implementation tests are not pilot validation.
