# Marg pharmacy retail: fields to features

## Question

How does Marg ERP's chemist setup work, from the fields staff enter to the features those fields enable?

## Answer

Marg is a master-data and transaction system. Staff first define medicines, parties, patients, doctors and settings. A purchase records a supplier document and adds stock by batch. A counter sale selects a patient or cash party, medicine, batch and quantity; the medicine's pack conversion, batch price and patient discount drive the bill. Returns and breakage/expiry use separate transactions. Reports and reminders read those records. This is the workflow shown in the supplied reseller walkthrough and supported in the linked Marg help pages; exact screens depend on the chemist setup and control-room settings. [Walkthrough transcript](/Users/docbook/.codex/attachments/122c2155-c55f-4884-9e1b-91958357048a/Pasted%20text.txt), [Marg pharmacy overview](https://margcompusoft.com/pharmacy-software.html).

## The daily loop

1. **Set up:** Create or import item and party masters. The demo reseller promises a preloaded medicine list; this is a reseller offer, not proof that every Marg installation includes that data. [Walkthrough transcript](/Users/docbook/.codex/attachments/122c2155-c55f-4884-9e1b-91958357048a/Pasted%20text.txt)
2. **Receive:** Select supplier and invoice, then record each item's batch, expiry, paid/free quantity and price. This makes that batch available for sale. [Marg purchase bill](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/purchase/4260/1/create%20purchase%20bill), [Marg free quantity](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/purchase/167199/1/How-to-create-a-purchase)
3. **Sell:** Find or create the patient, optionally select the doctor and a saved prescription, choose item and batch, enter strips/tablets or pieces, review price and discount, then save and print. [Walkthrough transcript](/Users/docbook/.codex/attachments/122c2155-c55f-4884-9e1b-91958357048a/Pasted%20text.txt), [Marg multi-unit billing](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/inventory/2001/1/How-we-can-enable-multi), [Marg prescription](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/rate-and-discount-master/53183/1/how-to-create-prescription-in-marg-erp-software)
4. **Resolve exceptions:** Add a shortage at the counter; receive more stock; record sale returns and supplier-facing breakage or expiry issues separately. [Marg shortage in bill](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/sale/3787/1/How-to-print-Item-Shortage), [Marg sale return](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/sale-return/28701/1/sale%20return), [Marg breakage/expiry issue](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/breakage-expiry-issue/79016/1/create-brk-exp-issuet)
5. **Review:** Use batch stock, expiry, shortage, sales and accounting reports to order and reconcile. [Marg pharmacy overview](https://margcompusoft.com/pharmacy-software.html), [Marg sale reports](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/sale-report)

## What this proves / does not prove

The supplied transcript and first-party help prove that Marg offers these screens and workflows in at least the demonstrated ERP/chemist configuration. They do not prove that every feature is included in every plan or enabled by default. Control-room settings change behavior: multi-unit billing and even whether a sale can drive stock below zero are configurable. The demo explicitly shows negative stock after an earlier sale, and Marg help documents a no-stock mode. Do not treat the demo's stock behavior as an inventory safety guarantee. [Marg multi-unit billing](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/inventory/2001/1/How-we-can-enable-multi), [Marg no-stock mode](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/inventory/551/1/how-to-set-stock-position-of-a-particular-item-will-not-effect-while-billing-in-marg-software), [Walkthrough transcript](/Users/docbook/.codex/attachments/122c2155-c55f-4884-9e1b-91958357048a/Pasted%20text.txt)

The walkthrough also has speech-recognition errors and sales claims. Its market-share numbers, import speed, plan coverage and promise of one lakh preloaded medicines were not independently verified. Its printed loose-tablet arithmetic is inconsistent in one sentence, so the ₹80 / 10 × 5 = ₹40 example above is the reliable one. [Walkthrough transcript](/Users/docbook/.codex/attachments/122c2155-c55f-4884-9e1b-91958357048a/Pasted%20text.txt)

## What this means for HMS

Marg is useful evidence for the **operator's field vocabulary and counter sequence**, especially pack conversion, supplier bill entry, sale batch choice, shortage capture and repeat-prescription reminders. It is not itself a scope decision for HMS. The current HMS pharmacy spec deliberately uses one counting/pricing unit per product, with a loose tablet as a separate product, and requires returns into quarantine. Those choices differ from the Marg demo's single-item strip/tablet conversion and its description of returning goods to stock. Any change needs the pilot pharmacy owner's observed workflow and a revised spec. [HMS pharmacy spec](../specs/pharmacy-counter-sale-and-stock.md#stage-0-pilot-workflow-sign-off), [HMS goods and services spec](../specs/pharmacy-goods-and-services.md), [Walkthrough transcript](/Users/docbook/.codex/attachments/122c2155-c55f-4884-9e1b-91958357048a/Pasted%20text.txt)

## Next falsification

At the pilot counter, ask staff to perform one real purchase and a three-item sale that includes loose tablets, an unavailable item and a returned item. Record the invoice, supplier bill, batch labels and printed output. Confirm whether they use one medicine identity with pack conversion, how they price loose tablets, whether shortages are written down during a sale, and what happens to returned stock before reuse. This checks the demo against observed work and the HMS Stage 0 assumptions. [HMS pharmacy spec](../specs/pharmacy-counter-sale-and-stock.md#stage-0-pilot-workflow-sign-off)
