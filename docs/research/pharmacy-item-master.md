# Pharmacy item master

2026-09-28. Research for the Indian hospital counter pharmacy; this memo compares item identity, units, batch price and master fields, while [pharmacy reference flows](./pharmacy-reference-flows.md) covers sale/return and [hospital inventory models](./hospital-inventory-models.md) covers movements and stock architecture.

> **Historical comparison.** The HMS code and line references below describe
> the tree reviewed before the 2026-09-29 cutover. The current contracts are
> [goods and services](../specs/pharmacy-goods-and-services.md) and
> [packs and loose units](../specs/pharmacy-packs-and-loose-units.md).
> Products now own sale facts; stock supports packs and loose units. Do not
> use the former catalog link or no-loose-sale premise as current guidance.

## Question

For an Indian hospital counter pharmacy, how should HMS model (1) goods versus services and the invoice-line item reference, (2) units, pack conversion and loose sale, (3) MRP and batches, and (4) the minimum product-master fields?

## Answer

[INFERENCE] Prefer one authoritative sold-product identity and a direct billable reference rather than a second hollow pharmacy catalog row: ERPNext and Odoo invoice lines reference their stock/service Item or Product directly, OpenBoxes invoices reference Product, while Danphe is an explicitly different design with distinct pharmacy and service invoice tables and a billing context that reads both. [ERPNext invoice item](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/version-15/erpnext/accounts/doctype/sales_invoice_item/sales_invoice_item.json), [Odoo invoice line](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/account/models/account_move_line.py), [OpenBoxes invoice item](https://github.com/openboxes/openboxes/blob/develop/grails-app/domain/org/pih/warehouse/invoice/InvoiceItem.groovy#L36-L56), [Danphe pharmacy context](https://github.com/ashwanthreddychalla/danphe-hims-emr/blob/main/Code/Components/DanpheEMR.DalLayer/PharmacyDbContext.cs#L139-L158), [Danphe billing context](https://github.com/ashwanthreddychalla/danphe-hims-emr/blob/main/Code/Components/DanpheEMR.DalLayer/BillingDbContext.cs#L218-L229). [INFERENCE] This is a research challenge to HMS's current D027 linkage, not approval to change its billing schema before the pilot workflow and affected charge consumers are checked (`docs/decisions.md:280-302`; `packages/db/src/schema/charges.ts:27-80`).

[INFERENCE] Keep D049's single integral counted unit and descriptive pack unless the owner walkthrough and actual bills show loose-from-strip sales; if they do, a named sale unit, product-specific count per pack, normalized stock quantity and explicit per-unit pricing become necessary. D049 records the owner's no-loose-sale premise and a separately stocked tablet product as the present alternative (`docs/decisions.md:676-694`; `docs/specs/pharmacy-counter-sale-and-stock.md:75-85`). [INFERENCE] Keep printed MRP with the batch rather than turning it into a product sale-price tier; HMS already stores immutable batch MRP per counted stock unit (`packages/db/src/schema/stock-batches.ts:18-43`). [INFERENCE] Keep HSN as item classification copied into a bill snapshot and GST treatment/rate on each line, with the operational product master limited to fields serving the signed-off workflow ([India Compliance fields](https://github.com/resilient-tech/india-compliance/blob/version-15/india_compliance/gst_india/constants/custom_fields.py#L1319-L1367), [Odoo India invoice line](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/l10n_in/models/account_move_line.py), `packages/api/src/routers/pharmacy.ts:176-255`).

## Evidence

### Goods, services and invoice reference

- ERPNext v15's Item has `is_stock_item`, sale/purchase flags and a shared item code; its Sales Invoice Item links Item directly. Marley procedure and lab templates and billable Healthcare Service Unit Type create/link **non-stock service Items**; Medication creates/links **stock Items** through Medication Linked Item, rather than requiring a hollow second medicine billable row. [ERPNext Item](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/version-15/erpnext/stock/doctype/item/item.json), [invoice line](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/version-15/erpnext/accounts/doctype/sales_invoice_item/sales_invoice_item.json), [procedure](https://github.com/earthians/marley/blob/develop/healthcare/healthcare/doctype/clinical_procedure_template/clinical_procedure_template.py), [lab](https://github.com/earthians/marley/blob/develop/healthcare/healthcare/doctype/lab_test_template/lab_test_template.py), [service unit type](https://github.com/earthians/marley/blob/develop/healthcare/healthcare/doctype/healthcare_service_unit_type/healthcare_service_unit_type.py), [medication](https://github.com/earthians/marley/blob/develop/healthcare/healthcare/doctype/medication/medication.py).
- Odoo 18 has goods/service `product.template.type` with separate `is_storable` for tracked goods; its invoice line references `product.product`. Bahmni maps OpenMRS drugs and lab/radiology services into Odoo products rather than a paired empty charge-item master. [Odoo product](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/product/models/product_template.py), [stock extension](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/stock/models/product.py), [invoice line](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/account/models/account_move_line.py), [Bahmni drug feed](https://github.com/Bahmni/bahmni-odoo-modules/blob/main/bahmni_api_feed/models/drug_data_service.py), [reference feed](https://github.com/Bahmni/bahmni-odoo-modules/blob/main/bahmni_api_feed/models/reference_data_service.py).
- OpenBoxes has one Product with `productType` and an InvoiceItem that points to Product. Danphe instead has `PHRM_MST_Item` and `BIL_MST_ServiceItem`, distinct pharmacy and service invoice tables, and pharmacy invoice mappings in its billing context: **separate**, not wholly disconnected. The accessible Danphe source is a community copy; the requested upstream URL was unavailable at inspection. [OpenBoxes product](https://github.com/openboxes/openboxes/blob/develop/grails-app/domain/org/pih/warehouse/product/Product.groovy#L81-L101), [invoice item](https://github.com/openboxes/openboxes/blob/develop/grails-app/domain/org/pih/warehouse/invoice/InvoiceItem.groovy#L36-L56), [Danphe pharmacy context](https://github.com/ashwanthreddychalla/danphe-hims-emr/blob/main/Code/Components/DanpheEMR.DalLayer/PharmacyDbContext.cs#L129-L158), [billing context](https://github.com/ashwanthreddychalla/danphe-hims-emr/blob/main/Code/Components/DanpheEMR.DalLayer/BillingDbContext.cs#L209-L229), [unavailable upstream](https://github.com/danphe-healthcare/Danphe-EMR).
- HMS's product owns its name and nullable saleability link, while catalog holds active/HSN/GST (`packages/db/src/schema/products.ts:28-58`; `packages/db/src/schema/catalog-items.ts:13-62`). Product creation inserts a same-name pharmacy catalog row with `unitPrice: 0`, and updating creates or synchronizes it; sale joins it for tax/active and writes its ID on Charge (`packages/api/src/routers/pharmacy-stock.ts:219-250,310-343`; `packages/api/src/routers/pharmacy.ts:176-199,232-255`). Pharmacy-category rows are guarded in **four checks across three procedures**: `catalog.create`, two checks in `catalog.update`, and `catalog.setActive` (`packages/api/src/routers/catalog.ts:116-123,161-178,208-226`).

### Units, packs and loose sale

- ERPNext item-specific `UOM Conversion Detail` relates sale/purchase units to stock UOM; an invoice line retains `uom`, conversion factor and `stock_qty = qty × factor`. Its price lookup checks the requested UOM then stock UOM; it **does not** infer a tablet rate from only a strip-price row when stock UOM is tablet. [conversion](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/version-15/erpnext/stock/doctype/uom_conversion_detail/uom_conversion_detail.json), [invoice line](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/version-15/erpnext/accounts/doctype/sales_invoice_item/sales_invoice_item.json), [price lookup](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/version-15/erpnext/stock/get_item_details.py#L1154-L1265).
- Odoo distinguishes product stock/purchase UOM, category-scoped UOM factor and product packaging quantity; a sale line retains sale UOM/quantity and price, while its pricelist converts rates by requested UOM. [product](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/product/models/product_template.py), [UOM](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/uom/models/uom_uom.py), [packaging](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/product/models/product_packaging.py), [sale line](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/sale/models/sale_order_line.py), [pricelist](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/product/models/product_pricelist_item.py). Bahmni's inspected order feed requires exact mapped UOM ID equality with product UOM and otherwise defaults quantity to zero, so the Odoo capability does not prove Bahmni dispensing converts strips ([order feed](https://github.com/Bahmni/bahmni-odoo-modules/blob/main/bahmni_api_feed/models/order_save_service.py)).
- Danphe's packing master has `PackingQuantity`, and receipt lines retain packing quantity, strip rate/MRP and unit MRP; its receipt UI divides received quantity by packing quantity. This does not establish a loose-tablet checkout or monetary rounding rule. [packing](https://github.com/ashwanthreddychalla/danphe-hims-emr/blob/main/Code/Components/DanpheEMR.ServerModel/PharmacyModels/PHRMPackingTypeModel.cs#L10-L20), [receipt](https://github.com/ashwanthreddychalla/danphe-hims-emr/blob/main/Code/Components/DanpheEMR.ServerModel/PharmacyModels/PHRMGoodsReceiptItemsModel.cs#L46-L65), [receipt UI](https://github.com/ashwanthreddychalla/danphe-hims-emr/blob/main/Code/Websites/DanpheEMR/wwwroot/DanpheApp/src/app/pharmacy/order/phrm-gr/phrm-goods-receipt.component.ts#L412-L426).
- OpenEMR's template `pkgqty` is written as `1.0` by its current editor, not demonstrated pack conversion; OpenBoxes explicitly says it does not currently support quantities at multiple levels despite ProductPackage quantities. [OpenEMR editor](https://github.com/openemr/openemr/blob/master/interface/drugs/add_edit_drug.php#L353-L378), [OpenBoxes limitation](https://github.com/openboxes/openboxes/blob/develop/grails-app/domain/org/pih/warehouse/product/Product.groovy#L163-L202), [package](https://github.com/openboxes/openboxes/blob/develop/grails-app/domain/org/pih/warehouse/product/ProductPackage.groovy#L32-L48).
- In the [Marg reseller demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YXCf71ryjUw), strip-to-tablet conversion is presented as the central retail-counter field: a ₹80 strip of 10 yields ₹16 for two tablets; whole-piece syrup uses zero conversion. This is an **unverified reseller claim**, not proof of this hospital's practice ([feature inventory](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YXCf71ryjUw)). D049 removes pack conversion because the owner says the pharmacy does not sell loose; `pack` is printed text and all quantities/prices use one integral stock unit (`docs/decisions.md:676-694`; `docs/specs/pharmacy-counter-sale-and-stock.md:75-85`).

### MRP, batch and tax

- Danphe StockMaster holds batch, expiry and MRP and Bahmni adds lot `mrp` and sale price; ERPNext v15 Batch and the inspected Odoo 18 core product/lot/expiry/pricelist fields do **not** define an Indian printed-MRP field. These are bounded observations, not claims about all extensions or legal MRP enforcement. [Danphe stock](https://github.com/ashwanthreddychalla/danphe-hims-emr/blob/main/Code/Components/DanpheEMR.ServerModel/PharmacyModels/PHRMStockMaster.cs#L12-L35), [Bahmni lot](https://github.com/Bahmni/bahmni-odoo-modules/blob/main/bahmni_stock/models/stock_production_lot.py), [ERPNext Batch](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/version-15/erpnext/stock/doctype/batch/batch.json), [Odoo product](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/product/models/product_template.py), [lot](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/stock/models/stock_lot.py), [expiry](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/product_expiry/models/production_lot.py), [pricelist](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/product/models/product_pricelist_item.py).
- HMS batch number, expiry and MRP per stock unit are immutable; the sale uses batch MRP as unit price and catalog tax values for Charge (`packages/db/src/schema/stock-batches.ts:18-43`; `packages/api/src/routers/pharmacy.ts:176-188,232-251`). India Compliance adds item HSN and fetches it onto invoice lines; GST treatment/rate comes from an item tax template. Odoo's India localization copies product HSN/SAC onto a draft invoice line and Odoo applies line taxes ([India Compliance item/line fields](https://github.com/resilient-tech/india-compliance/blob/version-15/india_compliance/gst_india/constants/custom_fields.py#L49-L58), [India Compliance tax fields](https://github.com/resilient-tech/india-compliance/blob/version-15/india_compliance/gst_india/constants/custom_fields.py#L810-L835), [Odoo India product](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/l10n_in/models/product_template.py), [India invoice line](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/l10n_in/models/account_move_line.py), [Odoo account line](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/account/models/account_move_line.py)).

### Minimum operational master and Marg field triage

ERPNext's Item requires code/group/stock UOM and offers sale/purchase flags; Marley's Medication holds generic identity, strength, dosage form and prescription defaults while linked Items represent branded stock SKUs. HMS instead currently has name, optional generic/form/strength/manufacturer, stock unit, printed pack and schedule on Product, with saleability/HSN/tax on linked Catalog and MRP on Batch ([ERPNext Item](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/version-15/erpnext/stock/doctype/item/item.json), [Marley Medication](https://github.com/earthians/marley/blob/develop/healthcare/healthcare/doctype/medication/medication.json), [linked item](https://github.com/earthians/marley/blob/develop/healthcare/healthcare/doctype/medication_linked_item/medication_linked_item.json), `packages/db/src/schema/products.ts:28-46`; `packages/db/src/schema/catalog-items.ts:35-46`; `packages/db/src/schema/stock-batches.ts:18-30`). [INFERENCE] Do not turn clinical dose/composition into required sold-SKU fields merely to mirror Marg; use an explicit clinical medication layer if that workflow earns one ([Marley Medication](https://github.com/earthians/marley/blob/develop/healthcare/healthcare/doctype/medication/medication.json), [Marg reseller inventory](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YXCf71ryjUw)).

[INFERENCE] Dispositions below mean **keep current**, **derive from an existing fact**, **drop from this master**, or **defer until a workflow proves need**; Marg fields are reseller-demo observations, not independently verified vendor behavior ([Marg inventory](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YXCf71ryjUw); `docs/specs/pharmacy-counter-sale-and-stock.md:75-106,704-740`).

## What this proves / does not prove

[INFERENCE] The surveyed direct-item invoice designs show that a second hollow medicine catalog row is not a prerequisite for shared billing, but Danphe demonstrates that separate masters/invoices can coexist with cross-module reads; none proves that replacing HMS's D027 linkage is a safe immediate migration ([ERPNext invoice line](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/version-15/erpnext/accounts/doctype/sales_invoice_item/sales_invoice_item.json), [Odoo invoice line](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/account/models/account_move_line.py), [Danphe billing context](https://github.com/ashwanthreddychalla/danphe-hims-emr/blob/main/Code/Components/DanpheEMR.DalLayer/BillingDbContext.cs#L218-L229), `docs/decisions.md:280-302`). [INFERENCE] ERPNext and Odoo show how explicit units support loose sales; Danphe receipt packing is narrower evidence, and OpenEMR/OpenBoxes do not establish complete loose-sale support ([ERPNext conversion](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/version-15/erpnext/stock/doctype/uom_conversion_detail/uom_conversion_detail.json), [Odoo UOM](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/uom/models/uom_uom.py), [Danphe receipt UI](https://github.com/ashwanthreddychalla/danphe-hims-emr/blob/main/Code/Websites/DanpheEMR/wwwroot/DanpheApp/src/app/pharmacy/order/phrm-gr/phrm-goods-receipt.component.ts#L412-L426), [OpenEMR editor](https://github.com/openemr/openemr/blob/master/interface/drugs/add_edit_drug.php#L353-L378), [OpenBoxes limitation](https://github.com/openboxes/openboxes/blob/develop/grails-app/domain/org/pih/warehouse/product/Product.groovy#L163-L202)). [INFERENCE] Neither an external demo nor the current owner premise alone proves whether this pilot counter actually sells loose tablets ([Marg reseller demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YXCf71ryjUw); `docs/specs/pharmacy-counter-sale-and-stock.md:75-85`).

## What this means for HMS

[INFERENCE] Preserve D049 pending field evidence; do not parse printed `pack` text as a divisor, infer loose prices from strip MRP, or add Marg's long item form as a prerequisite for counter sale (`docs/decisions.md:676-694`; [ERPNext price lookup](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/version-15/erpnext/stock/get_item_details.py#L1154-L1265); [Marg inventory](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YXCf71ryjUw)). [INFERENCE] If loose sale proves common, revisit the one-unit decision together with receipt quantities, stock ledger, batch MRP denomination, invoice snapshot and price rounding rather than adding a conversion textbox in isolation (`docs/decisions.md:676-694`; `packages/db/src/schema/stock-batches.ts:18-30`; `packages/api/src/routers/pharmacy.ts:232-255`; [ERPNext invoice line](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/blob/version-15/erpnext/accounts/doctype/sales_invoice_item/sales_invoice_item.json)). [INFERENCE] If revisiting goods/services identity, map all `products.catalogItemId` and `charges.catalogItemId` consumers and preserve existing charge/invoice tax snapshots before changing D027 (`packages/db/src/schema/products.ts:28-58`; `packages/db/src/schema/charges.ts:27-80`; `packages/api/src/routers/pharmacy.ts:176-255`; `docs/decisions.md:280-302`).

## Next falsification

At the pharmacy-owner Stage 0 walkthrough, ask whether loose tablets are sold and how often, and count **loose-tablet versus whole-strip lines** across one week of the pilot's paper or incumbent bills; compare the actual tally with the no-loose premise in D049 (`docs/specs/pharmacy-counter-sale-and-stock.md:75-85`; `docs/decisions.md:676-694`). [INFERENCE] A repeatable loose-sale pattern falsifies the premise for one-unit strip stock and warrants a measured unit/price contract; a zero or negligible tally supports retaining D049 for the pilot rather than importing a reseller's workflow ([Marg reseller demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YXCf71ryjUw); `docs/decisions.md:676-694`).
