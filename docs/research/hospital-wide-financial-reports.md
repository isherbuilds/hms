# Hospital-wide financial reports and invoice series

## Question

What should an accountant get from Reports when OPD, pharmacy, and future care settings issue separate invoice series? Does Marley Health with ERPNext suggest a better model, and what changes if HMS later becomes the accounting system?

## Answer

Keep distinct, unique invoice series for each genuine billing stream and one organization-wide Invoice Register preserving original numbers. Collections, event-period net billed revenue and current balances are separate measures, not one “all transactions” total. D054 accepts this direction in the [Ready-for-implementation revenue-control spec](../specs/revenue-control.md), retaining D025's separate stream series. Earning-department reporting requires an issue-time dimension snapshot and is not part of the present read-only increment. The billing ledger lacks expenses, inventory valuation, opening balances and other entries needed for complete books or final ITR figures. [D025](../decisions.md#d025--one-invoice-per-stream-with-its-own-numbering-series), [ERPNext accounting reports](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/accounting-reports), [Product](../product.md#opd-and-money).

## Evidence

### External systems and rules

- Marley Health is the separate healthcare app used with ERPNext. Its v15 service and medication requests can feed an ERPNext Sales Invoice; healthcare workflow is linked to a financial document. Its v13 encounter docs also describe pharmacy/accounts staff fetching prescription lines into Sales Invoice. These are documented workflows, not proof of how a particular hospital configures them. [Marley introduction](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/v12/user/manual/en/healthcare/therapy_plan), [Marley v15 requests](https://marley.frappe.cloud/docs/v15/user/manual/en/healthcare/servicemedicationrequest), [Marley v13 encounter](https://marley.frappe.cloud/docs/v13/user/manual/en/healthcare/patient_encounter).
- ERPNext permits multiple naming series for Sales Invoices, including fiscal-year series. Submission posts receivable, income, and applicable tax to its general ledger. Payment Entry settles the invoice. Thus separate series do not inherently prevent a consolidated financial view. [Naming series](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/naming-series), [Sales Invoice](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/sales-invoice), [Payment Entry](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/payment-entry).
- ERPNext offers a Sales Register and detailed General Ledger. Its reports advise matching company, dates, status, dimensions, and return treatment when totals differ. Cost centers can mark individual invoice lines and ledger income/expense entries, which then support departmental P&L. This is a reporting dimension, not a second invoice sequence. [Accounting reports](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/accounting-reports), [Sales reports](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/sales-analytics), [Cost center](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/cost-center).
- TallyPrime supports multiple voucher numbering series and voucher-wise period Sales Registers. **Manual** numbering accepts the supplied number and offers duplicate prevention; automatic numbering's **Retain Original Voucher No.** prevents existing numbers changing on insertion/deletion, whereas **Renumber Vouchers** does not. The Sales Register distinguishes invoice transaction value including GST from revenue excluding GST. Credit Notes retain the original Invoice reference but post on the return date: Tally's October sale/December return example enters the correction in December. [Numbering methods](https://help.tallysolutions.com/use-voucher-numbering-methods/), [Sales Register](https://help.tallysolutions.com/how-to-work-with-sales-register-in-tallyprime/), [Correction period](https://help.tallysolutions.com/sales-return-using-credit-note/).
- CGST Rules 46, 49, 50 and 53 allow one or multiple consecutive document series, unique for a financial year, using letters, numerals, hyphen/dash and slash, with a 16-character ceiling. [D052](../decisions.md#d052--gst-registration-determines-document-particulars-and-bounded-numbering) settles HMS's registered OPD Bill of Supply, pharmacy Tax Invoice and exempt-care Receipt Voucher classifications; without a GSTIN, documents are neutral and make no GST claim. The GST offline tool separately records document ranges in “Documents Issued”. [CBIC rules](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/03042020-CGST-Rules-2017-Part-A-Rules.pdf), [GST offline tool](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/invoiceuploadofflineutility.pdf), [Product classification rules](../product.md#opd-and-money).
- Income-tax reporting concerns gross receipts and, where applicable, P&L and balance-sheet figures; an invoice register is supporting evidence, not an ITR form or a complete set of accounts. The exact schedules depend on legal entity and filing position. [Income Tax Department ITR-3 AY 2026–27 validation rules](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/sites/default/files/2026-06/CBDT_e-filing_ITR-3_Validation%20Rules_V1.0_AY%2026-27.pdf), [Product ledger scope](../product.md#opd-and-money).
- ERPNext's Credit Note is a separately dated negative Sales Invoice reversing income/tax and receivables; Refund Payment Entries do not reverse sales again. Its Accounts Receivable uses allocation-aware invoice balances and a selected reporting date, unlike a sales activity report. Gross Profit needs buying/valuation facts; missing cost cannot be replaced by MRP. [Credit Note](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/credit-note), [Accounts Receivable](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/accounts-receivable-and-payable), [Gross Profit](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/sales-analytics).
- Bahmni's Odoo subsystem links clinical drug orders to billing, maintains patient financial transactions and offers standard accounting reports. This proves linkage/report capability, not a particular daily owner-review habit. [Billing and Accounting](https://bahmni.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/BAH/pages/32604217/Patient+Billing+and+Accounting). Marg's expiry tutorial shows quantity-based expiry reports and its source screen displays MRP and purchase rate separately; it does not establish that MRP exposure is book cost. [Expiry report](https://care.margcompusoft.com/margerp/expiry-stock/109499/1/how-to-view-expiry-stock-report-in-marg-erp-software), [MRP/PurRate screen](https://care.margcompusoft.com/userfiles/CareImg070620190101479.png). [ICAI AS 2 paragraphs 5–7, 16–17 and 20–25](https://resource.cdn.icai.org/27269asb-as-2.pdf) require cost/NRV valuation and support separating operational MRP exposure from statutory inventory value.

### Current HMS

- D025 intentionally gives OPD and pharmacy one invoice per operational parent, separate fiscal-year counters, and distinct prefixes. The invoice table has an organization-wide unique number constraint. [D025](../decisions.md#d025--one-invoice-per-stream-with-its-own-numbering-series), [`invoices.ts`](../../packages/db/src/schema/invoices.ts).
- The invoice stores stream, number, fiscal year, business date, and totals. Its lines store revenue category and tax facts, but no explicit earning-department snapshot. OPD appointments have a department; pharmacy sales are a separate parent. Therefore a “department” column derived from today's master data would not be a durable accounting dimension. [`invoices.ts`](../../packages/db/src/schema/invoices.ts), [`invoice-lines.ts`](../../packages/db/src/schema/invoice-lines.ts), [`report.ts`](../../packages/api/src/routers/report.ts).
- Reports currently link GST outward register, daily collections, OPD register, trial balance, and billing-ledger balance sheet. The GST report does include invoice and credit-note numbers, but groups by document and tax bucket and does not expose stream or a one-row-per-invoice register. Daily collections includes advances and refunds by payment method; the OPD register covers appointments only. [Report navigation](../../apps/web/src/lib/navigation.ts), [`report.ts`](../../packages/api/src/routers/report.ts), [Reports](../reports.md).
- Journals post with source documents, but the product explicitly excludes manual journals, expenses, opening balances, bank reconciliation, inventory accounting, and final accounts. A future “own accounting” scope would be a new product decision, not a new Reports screen over the existing partial ledger. [D012](../decisions.md#d012--double-entry-journals-commit-with-billing-documents), [Architecture ledger](../architecture.md#billing-ledger), [Product](../product.md#opd-and-money).
- **Numbering rule, replacing the earlier gap:** [D052](../decisions.md#d052--gst-registration-determines-document-particulars-and-bounded-numbering) limits configured prefixes to four permitted characters, reserving seven for the fiscal year, one slash and four sequence digits within the 16-character ceiling. `documentNumber` fails on overflow rather than issuing an overlength number; a full four-character prefix therefore supports sequence 1–9999. Separate stream series remain valid. Pilot evidence must confirm actual prefixes, uniqueness, consecutive ranges and accountant handover; the rule does not mean that sign-off has occurred. [`settings.ts`](../../packages/api/src/routers/settings.ts), [`invoice-math.ts`](../../packages/api/src/lib/invoice-math.ts), [CBIC Rules 46/49/50/53](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/03042020-CGST-Rules-2017-Part-A-Rules.pdf).

## What this proves / does not prove

Multiple series can be valid and workable when document type, series and sequence are clear and a consolidated register traces each Invoice. The cited docs do not prove Marley has a dedicated hospital-wide department report, the incumbent's actual numbering/export configuration, customer demand, or repeated daily use. “Marli” is interpreted as Marley Health, not a verified identification of a hospital's incumbent. D052 settles product classification rules; actual issuer identity/GST registration must match deployment records. D054 authorizes implementation autonomously, not by claiming customer interviews or a reconciled hospital month.

## What this means for us

1. **First useful report:** the [revenue-control spec](../specs/revenue-control.md#invoice-register-contract) owns the all-stream Invoice Register for owner, admin and accountant, its fiscal-year default, source columns, series summaries and complete filtered XLSX export. Invoice issue dates select the population; balances include all linked movements visible at generation and are labelled “Invoices issued in period; balances now,” not historical debt. Separate series never become a fabricated global sequence.
2. **Then segment revenue:** the same spec owns stream/category/practitioner reporting and the issue-period versus correction-period bridge. D054 defines earning department as the service owner and requires an immutable issued-line and revenue-ledger dimension for a future departmental report, crediting the original snapshot and leaving legacy lines unassigned. Clinical attendance department and cashier desk are not that dimension. [ERPNext Cost Center](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/cost-center) stores it per invoice row and ledger posting; current HMS lacks that snapshot.
3. **Keep reconciliation views separate:** invoice/Credit Note event-period net sales, receipt/advance/refund cash flow, GST outward values and billing-journal Trial Balance are the four month/year handover ties in [D054's accepted dispositions](../specs/revenue-control.md#accepted-dispositions). Date, entity, scope, tax, round-off and advance-liability bridges explain differences. Billed value is not a claim of complete final accounts. [ERPNext accounting reports](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/accounting-reports), [Tally Sales Register](https://help.tallysolutions.com/how-to-work-with-sales-register-in-tallyprime/).
4. **If HMS becomes the accounting system:** establish a single source of books and controlled migration date; add opening balances, purchase/expense documents, inventory cost and NRV, bank/cash reconciliation, approved adjustments, period closing, full financial statements and source-to-ledger drilldown. Tag dimensions at posting and reconcile opening Trial Balance to incumbent books, avoiding dual entry. This requires a separate full-accounting specification and evidenced cutover, not a reporting screen or an unanswered consultation gate. [ERPNext accounting entries](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/accounting-entries), [ERPNext P&L](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/profit-and-loss-report), [ICAI AS 2](https://resource.cdn.icai.org/27269asb-as-2.pdf), [Product cutover](../product.md#delivery-rules).

## Next falsification

Implementation falsification is a cross-month fixture spanning OPD and pharmacy, a later Credit Note, Payment, Advance Receipt/Allocation and Refund. Reconcile event-period net sales, receipts, GST outward and billing-journal Trial Balance at the same entity/date scope; prove both correction bridge amounts and negative balances. Use D052 for issuer/registration, classifications and bounded numbering, and D054 for department and valuation meanings. [ERPNext filter/reconciliation guidance](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/accounting-reports) and [Tally's correction-period example](https://help.tallysolutions.com/sales-return-using-credit-note/) supply the reference bases. No CA answer is required before implementation.

Tally handover uses one voucher per Invoice/Credit Note with the HMS number as voucher number and bill-wise reference, not summary sales. Use dedicated Manual-numbered HMS voucher types with duplicate prevention; any automatic-type migration retains original numbers and must prove that supplied numbers survive import. [Tally numbering](https://help.tallysolutions.com/use-voucher-numbering-methods/) and [voucher-wise Sales Register](https://help.tallysolutions.com/how-to-work-with-sales-register-in-tallyprime/) support the policy. Before adopting an import format, test five OPD and five pharmacy documents plus a cross-month Credit Note in a non-production Tally company; inserting an earlier-dated voucher must not renumber existing HMS documents. No adapter or successful Tally round-trip is claimed. A real redacted hospital month, paying pilot, independently entered hospital denominator and seven-day daily-use measurements remain unavailable operational evidence, not decisions desk research can satisfy.
