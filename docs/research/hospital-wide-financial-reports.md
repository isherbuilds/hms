# Hospital-wide financial reports and invoice series

## Question

What should an accountant get from Reports when OPD, pharmacy, and future care settings issue separate invoice series? Does Marley Health with ERPNext suggest a better model, and what changes if HMS later becomes the accounting system?

## Answer

Keep distinct, unique invoice series for each genuine billing stream. Provide **one organization-wide invoice register** that includes every stream and preserves each original document number. A separate **collections register** explains money received and refunded. A **revenue by stream/category/department** view explains what earned the money. These are different measures; none should be presented as a single ambiguous “all transactions” total. The existing billing ledger is a useful bridge, but it does not yet contain the expenses, inventory valuation, opening balances, and other entries needed for complete books or final ITR figures. This is a proposed reporting direction, not a change to D025. [D025](../decisions.md#d025--one-invoice-per-stream-with-its-own-numbering-series), [Product](../product.md#opd-and-money), [Reports](../reports.md).

## Evidence

### External systems and rules

- Marley Health is the separate healthcare app used with ERPNext. Its v15 service and medication requests can feed an ERPNext Sales Invoice; healthcare workflow is linked to a financial document. Its v13 encounter docs also describe pharmacy/accounts staff fetching prescription lines into Sales Invoice. These are documented workflows, not proof of how a particular hospital configures them. [Marley introduction](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/v12/user/manual/en/healthcare/therapy_plan), [Marley v15 requests](https://marley.frappe.cloud/docs/v15/user/manual/en/healthcare/servicemedicationrequest), [Marley v13 encounter](https://marley.frappe.cloud/docs/v13/user/manual/en/healthcare/patient_encounter).
- ERPNext permits multiple naming series for Sales Invoices, including fiscal-year series. Submission posts receivable, income, and applicable tax to its general ledger. Payment Entry settles the invoice. Thus separate series do not inherently prevent a consolidated financial view. [Naming series](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/naming-series), [Sales Invoice](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/sales-invoice), [Payment Entry](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/payment-entry).
- ERPNext offers a Sales Register and detailed General Ledger. Its reports advise matching company, dates, status, dimensions, and return treatment when totals differ. Cost centers can mark individual invoice lines and ledger income/expense entries, which then support departmental P&L. This is a reporting dimension, not a second invoice sequence. [Accounting reports](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/accounting-reports), [Sales reports](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/sales-analytics), [Cost center](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/cost-center).
- TallyPrime also supports multiple voucher numbering series and separate Sales voucher types. Its Sales Register can show voucher-wise sales for a period, and Excel transaction imports use mapping templates for voucher and ledger fields. Tally warns that duplicate voucher/document numbers can create GST-report exceptions and that imported vouchers need appropriate numbers. **Inference:** the accountant's objection may be to manual entry, automatic Tally renumbering, duplicate unprefixed numbers, or the absence of one consolidated source file; these are distinct failure modes. [Tally numbering series](https://help.tallysolutions.com/set-up-vch-numbering-and-numbering-series-for-gst/), [Tally Sales Register](https://help.tallysolutions.com/how-to-work-with-sales-register-in-tallyprime/), [Tally Excel mapping](https://help.tallysolutions.com/getting-started-with-importing-data-into-tallyprime/), [Tally duplicate numbers](https://help.tallysolutions.com/manage-duplicate-nos-for-gst-vouchers/), [Tally import numbering](https://help.tallysolutions.com/tracking-modifications/).
- CBIC's GST FAQ says Rule 46 allows one or multiple invoice series that are consecutive within the series, unique for a financial year, and no more than 16 characters for a tax invoice. The GST offline tool has a separate “Documents Issued” table for document ranges. The CA must decide which HMS documents are tax invoices, bills of supply, or other documents before this is treated as a compliance design. [CBIC FAQ](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/sectoral-faq.html), [GST offline tool](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/downloads/invoiceuploadofflineutility.pdf), [Product document-classification gate](../product.md#opd-and-money).
- Income-tax reporting concerns gross receipts and, where applicable, P&L and balance-sheet figures; an invoice register is supporting evidence, not an ITR form or a complete set of accounts. The exact schedules depend on legal entity and filing position. [Income Tax Department ITR-3 AY 2026–27 validation rules](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/sites/default/files/2026-06/CBDT_e-filing_ITR-3_Validation%20Rules_V1.0_AY%2026-27.pdf), [Product ledger scope](../product.md#opd-and-money).

### Current HMS

- D025 intentionally gives OPD and pharmacy one invoice per operational parent, separate fiscal-year counters, and distinct prefixes. The invoice table has an organization-wide unique number constraint. [D025](../decisions.md#d025--one-invoice-per-stream-with-its-own-numbering-series), [`invoices.ts`](../../packages/db/src/schema/invoices.ts).
- The invoice stores stream, number, fiscal year, business date, and totals. Its lines store revenue category and tax facts, but no explicit earning-department snapshot. OPD appointments have a department; pharmacy sales are a separate parent. Therefore a “department” column derived from today's master data would not be a durable accounting dimension. [`invoices.ts`](../../packages/db/src/schema/invoices.ts), [`invoice-lines.ts`](../../packages/db/src/schema/invoice-lines.ts), [`report.ts`](../../packages/api/src/routers/report.ts).
- Reports currently link GST outward register, daily collections, OPD register, trial balance, and billing-ledger balance sheet. The GST report does include invoice and credit-note numbers, but groups by document and tax bucket and does not expose stream or a one-row-per-invoice register. Daily collections includes advances and refunds by payment method; the OPD register covers appointments only. [Report navigation](../../apps/web/src/lib/navigation.ts), [`report.ts`](../../packages/api/src/routers/report.ts), [Reports](../reports.md).
- Journals post with source documents, but the product explicitly excludes manual journals, expenses, opening balances, bank reconciliation, inventory accounting, and final accounts. A future “own accounting” scope would be a new product decision, not a new Reports screen over the existing partial ledger. [D012](../decisions.md#d012--double-entry-journals-commit-with-billing-documents), [Architecture ledger](../architecture.md#billing-ledger), [Product](../product.md#opd-and-money).
- **Potential compliance gap to verify before live statutory use:** settings allow an invoice prefix up to 10 characters without an allowed-character rule, and `documentNumber` appends a seven-character fiscal year, slash, and an unbounded sequence. The generated number can exceed 16 characters or contain disallowed characters. This is a code observation against the CBIC tax-invoice rule; the CA first needs to classify the document and approve its numbering. [`settings.ts`](../../packages/api/src/routers/settings.ts), [`invoice-math.ts`](../../packages/api/src/lib/invoice-math.ts), [CBIC FAQ](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/hindi/sectoral-faq.html).

## What this proves / does not prove

Multiple series can be valid and workable when the legal document type, series, and sequence are clear, and when a consolidated register traces each invoice to its source. The cited docs do not show that Marley has a dedicated hospital-wide department report out of the box, nor do they prove the incumbent software's actual numbering or export behavior. “Marli” is interpreted here as **Marley Health** pending confirmation. The accountant's requested report and the hospital's legal/tax status still need direct validation.

## What this means for us

1. **First useful report:** add an accountant-only all-stream Invoice Register, defaulting to a fiscal-year date range. One row per issued invoice: business date, stream/series, full invoice number, patient or counter-sale identity, taxable value, tax, discount/rounding, grand total, credit-note total, net billed, paid/allocated, outstanding, and a source-document link. Export the same filtered result to XLSX. Sort by business date plus stable issuance order; never pretend `OPD.../7` and `PH.../7` are one global sequence. Show a summary by stream and series, including first/last number and count. Reconcile gross issued, credits, and net billed separately from collections.
2. **Then segment revenue:** distinguish operational **stream** (OPD/pharmacy), **revenue category** (consultation/medicines/etc.), and **earning department** (the service owner). Do not infer all three from an OPD appointment department. Ask the CA and desk owners whether department means cashier desk, treating department, service provider, or cost center. If a stable departmental financial view is required, snapshot that dimension on each issued line and carry it to the relevant revenue ledger line for future accounting. Handle unassigned or shared lines explicitly.
3. **Keep reconciliation views separate:** invoice/credit-note register for earned or billed value; receipt/advance/refund register for cash flow; GL detail for posting trace; trial balance for account totals; tax register for statutory classification. Each view needs a stated date basis and source. Existing GST and collection reports can become drilldowns, not competing totals.
4. **If HMS becomes the accounting system:** establish a single source of books and a controlled migration date. Add opening balances, purchase and expense documents, inventory value and cost of goods, bank and cash reconciliation, manual adjustments with approval, period closing, full P&L/balance sheet/cash flow, and source-to-ledger drilldown. Tag income and expenses with stable reporting dimensions at posting time. Reconcile the opening trial balance to the incumbent books; avoid dual entry. This scope requires a separate accounting specification and CA sign-off. [ERPNext accounting entries](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/accounting-entries), [ERPNext P&L](https://docs.frappe.io/erpnext/profit-and-loss-report), [Product cutover](../product.md#delivery-rules).

## Next falsification

Ask the CA for one redacted sample of the expected year-end export and the exact totals it must tie to: invoice/credit-note net sales, cash and bank receipts, GST outward values, or trial balance. Confirm legal entity, GST registrations, document classes, the intended meaning of “department,” and whether the incumbent's series are actually duplicate, nonconsecutive within each series, or merely interleaved in one list. Reconcile one real month across all active desks and each report before writing the implementation spec.

For the Tally handover specifically, ask whether the CA enters **each invoice** or only a **period summary**, whether Tally is set to retain HMS invoice numbers or assigns its own voucher numbers, and which number appears in the statutory return and audit trail. Preserve the issued HMS invoice number as a searchable source reference even if Tally uses a separate internal voucher number. Test five OPD and five pharmacy documents, including a credit note, in a copy of the accountant's Tally company before choosing an import format.
